## Market Analysis

Washington State has a dense small business ecosystem in food service and retail. The Seattle metro area alone has thousands of independently owned and franchise restaurant locations. The market is not saturated with operational consultants targeting this segment.

**Key insight:** most small business owners in this segment are operators, not managers. They run the business day-to-day and have no time or tools to build systems. That is the gap we fill.
