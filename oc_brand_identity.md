# Brand Identity

![Ordina Cresce](./oc_logo.svg)

## Concept

Deep heritage green + ivory paper + antique gold + muted botanical accents.

The visual language draws from aged documents, botanical illustration, and archival print. Materials feel physical — pressed paper, gold foil, inked line work. The palette communicates heritage, precision, and quiet authority.

## Gold Treatment

Antique gold (`#B48748`) is a foil color, not a flat fill.

- In print: treat as metallic foil or spot UV.
- In digital: use with subtle gradient or luminance variation to suggest depth. Avoid flat solid fills at large scale.
- Never use gold on gold. Always pair against `#0D1919` or `#D9CAB7`.

## Usage Rules

- Dark backgrounds use `#0D1919` or `#111D1D`. Do not mix both on the same surface.
- Text on ivory uses `#1E2423` (Soft ink). Never pure black.
- Text on dark uses `#D9CAB7` (Warm ivory). Never pure white.
- Accent colors support, not lead. One accent per composition.
- Gold marks structure: headlines, dividing rules, foil stamps. Not decoration.
