# ORDINA

## Cash Handling – Register Security & Cash Over/Short Policy

### Summary

Cash must always be secure, traceable, and accurately reported.

Registers, cash drawers, and deposits must follow company procedures at all times. Cash overages and shortages must be reported honestly and may never be hidden, transferred, or adjusted using other funds.

Transparency protects both the business and the associate.

---

## Purpose

Protect company assets, maintain accurate financial records, and ensure accountability for all cash transactions.

This policy applies to all associates responsible for cash handling.

---

## Register Security

### 1. Register Keys and Access

Register keys, access cards, and security devices must remain under the control of the Manager, PIC, or authorized person responsible for the location.

Unauthorized access to registers is prohibited.

---

## End of Day Procedures

### 1. Empty Cash Drawers

All register drawers must be emptied at the end of the day.

Cash drawers should remain visibly open overnight.

### 2. Overnight Security

Leaving drawers open demonstrates that no cash remains inside and helps discourage theft and break-ins.

### 3. Opening Funds

Register opening funds may be prepared in advance and secured inside the designated section of the safe.

---

## Cash Over and Short

### 1. Definition

A cash over or short is the difference between:

- The expected cash amount.
- The actual deposit amount.

The system automatically calculates this difference.

### 2. Reporting

All cash overages and shortages must be reported.

Hiding discrepancies is prohibited.

---

## Cash Overages

Cash overages must always be included in the daily deposit.

Cash overages may never be:

- Held to cover future shortages.
- Used to purchase supplies.
- Used for team events.
- Placed in donation boxes.
- Used as tips.
- Kept by associates.

---

## Cash Shortages

Cash shortages must be reported immediately.

Associates may never use personal money to cover shortages.

Donation funds may never be used to offset shortages.

The purpose of reporting shortages is to identify and correct the cause, not to conceal the loss.

---

## Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Hiding cash shortages.
- Saving cash overages for future shortages.
- Using overages for supplies or events.
- Using donation money to cover shortages.
- Using personal money to balance deposits.
- Leaving cash inside registers overnight.
- Leaving cash drawers closed with money inside.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
