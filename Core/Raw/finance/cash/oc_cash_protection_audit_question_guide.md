# ORDINA

## Cash Protection Audit Question Guide

**Effective Date:** 2026-03-22

---

# Purpose

The Cash Protection Audit Question Guide provides leaders with a consistent method for evaluating financial controls, asset protection, security practices, and operational compliance.

Questions are designed to identify risk, improve accountability, and protect company assets.

---

# Risk Levels

| Level | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Automatic Failure | Fraud, theft, or intentional misconduct |
| High Risk | Major security or financial exposure |
| Medium Risk | Operational deficiencies requiring correction |
| Low Risk | Minor opportunities for improvement |
| Information Only | Observation and coaching opportunities |

---

# Asset Protection

### Register Security

- Are coin drawers secured?
- Are donation containers secured?
- Are manager keys properly controlled?
- Are emergency register procedures understood?

### Gift Cards & Promotions

- Do associates understand gift card procedures?
- Are discounts properly applied?
- Are promotions authorized appropriately?

### Scam Awareness

- Do associates recognize scam attempts?
- Do associates understand that no cash leaves the store for payments?
- Do associates know reporting procedures?

### Associate Safety

- Do associates feel safe working at the location?
- Is the location free from aggressive panhandling or loitering?
- Is vandalism addressed immediately?

---

# Cash Management

### Safe Controls

- Are safe counts completed daily?
- Are safe balances accurate?
- Are deposits completed timely?
- Is excess cash removed from registers?

### Register Accountability

- Do associates only use their assigned registers?
- Do cashiers count their own drawers?
- Are cash drops completed immediately?
- Are funds never left unattended?

### Cash Limits

- Have register cash limits been exceeded?
- Are cash pulls completed promptly?

---

# Associate Accountability

- Have required training programs been completed?
- Are meals properly recorded?
- Are discounts properly documented?
- Are receipts offered during transactions?

---

# Closing Procedures

- Are all drawers secured at close?
- Are doors locked during cash counts?
- Is the buddy system used after hours?
- Is lighting maintained for security purposes?
- Are donation containers emptied?

---

# Security Systems

### CCTV

- Is the system operational?
- Are cameras recording properly?
- Are critical areas visible?
- Are issues reported promptly?

### Alarm System

- Are users current?
- Are alarm codes protected?
- Is the alarm armed daily?

### Safe Security

- Is the safe secured?
- Are time delay features active?
- Are safe users current?
- Are codes unique to each user?

---

# Exterior Security

- Is the back door locked?
- Is the back door unused after dark?
- Are exterior areas adequately illuminated?
- Are dumpster areas secured?

---

# Fraud Indicators

The following conditions may result in immediate investigation:

- Unauthorized free food.
- Improper discounts.
- Register sharing.
- Employee meals taken improperly.
- Undercharging transactions.
- Cash theft.
- Inventory manipulation.
- Falsification of records.

Any confirmed fraudulent activity results in an automatic audit failure.

---

# Leadership Responsibility

Leaders conducting audits are expected to:

- Observe objectively.
- Verify facts.
- Document findings.
- Coach where appropriate.
- Escalate serious concerns.
- Protect people and assets.

---

# End In Mind

The purpose of the audit is not simply to find mistakes.

The purpose is to:

- Protect people.
- Protect assets.
- Reduce risk.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Improve operations.
