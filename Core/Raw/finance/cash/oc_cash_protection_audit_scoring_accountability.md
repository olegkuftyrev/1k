# ORDINA

## Cash Protection Audit Scoring & Accountability

### Summary

The Cash Protection Audit measures compliance with company standards designed to protect people, cash, and company assets.

The audit evaluates operational execution, security practices, and accountability.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of the Cash Protection Audit is to:

- Protect company assets.
- Reduce operational risk.
- Improve accountability.
- Identify training opportunities.
- Support operational excellence.

---

# Pass and Fail Standards

### Passing Score

- **80% or higher = PASS**

A passing score demonstrates that the location is meeting company expectations.

### Failing Score

- **79% or lower = FAIL**

A failing score indicates significant opportunities requiring corrective action.

---

# Automatic Failure

The following conditions result in an automatic audit failure regardless of the overall score:

- Fraud.
- Theft.
- Intentional policy violations.
- Falsification of records.
- Deliberate misuse of company assets.
- Unauthorized cash handling activities.

Integrity violations override all scoring results.

---

# Scoring Method

Each audit question carries weighted points based on:

- Risk level.
- Financial impact.
- Security impact.
- Operational importance.

Higher-risk areas receive greater point values.

---

# Score Categories

## Total Score

The total score determines:

- Pass or fail status.
- Overall compliance.
- Management accountability.

---

## Category Scores

Category scores identify:

- Areas of strength.
- Operational opportunities.
- Training needs.
- Follow-up priorities.

Examples include:

- Cash Handling
- Security
- Asset Protection
- Fraud Prevention
- Equipment Compliance

---

# Corrective Action

Locations that fail an audit may require:

- Coaching.
- Retraining.
- Follow-up audits.
- Action plans.
- Additional oversight.

The purpose of corrective action is improvement and risk reduction.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Understanding audit standards.
- Reviewing results.
- Addressing deficiencies.
- Training associates.
- Maintaining compliance.

---

# Guiding Principle

The score measures compliance.

The audit measures execution.

Accountability protects people, assets, and the business.
