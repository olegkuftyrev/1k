# ORDINA

## Cash Handling Policy Statement

### Summary

Cash handling standards protect our associates, guests, and company assets. Every associate responsible for handling cash must follow these procedures without exception.

Failure to follow cash handling procedures may result in corrective action, up to and including termination.

---

## 1. Daily Deposits

Deposits must be completed every business day.

This requirement includes:

- Weekends.
- Holidays.
- Special operating days.

---

## 2. Safe Counts

Safe and change funds must be counted during:

- Opening procedures.
- Closing procedures.

All counts must be documented in the approved cash management system.

---

## 3. No Shared Registers

Each register is assigned to a single cashier.

- Associates may only use the register they are logged into.
- Register accountability belongs to the assigned cashier.

---

## 4. Guest Receipts

Cashiers must:

- Count change back to the guest.
- Provide a receipt for every transaction.

---

## 5. Register Drawer Security

Register drawers must remain closed after every transaction.

Drawers may not remain open or unattended.

---

## 6. Cash Pulls

Excess cash must be removed from registers and secured in the safe.

- Maximum drawer balance: **$700**
- Cash pulls should occur throughout the day.
- Cash should never accumulate unnecessarily in the register.

---

## 7. No Sale Function

The "No Sale" function may only be used during emergency situations.

Improper use of the drawer release function is prohibited.

---

## 8. End-of-Shift Cash Counts

Cashiers are responsible for:

- Counting their assigned drawer.
- Completing the end-of-shift cash count.
- Reporting all overages or shortages.

A manager or secondary verifier must confirm the count.

---

## 9. Manager Keys

Manager keys and security devices must:

- Remain in the possession of the Manager or Person in Charge.
- Be secured when not in use.
- Never be shared.

---

## 10. Store Funds

Store cash may never leave the restaurant.

Associates may not:

- Pay vendors with store cash.
- Pay personal expenses.
- Satisfy outside requests for payment.
- Deposit company funds into cryptocurrency or digital currency machines.
- Remove funds from the building without authorization.

---

## Personal Funds

Associates may never use personal money to:

- Cover shortages.
- Complete refunds.
- Balance drawers.
- Replace missing funds.

All cash discrepancies must be reported immediately.

---

## Accountability

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination.

Managers are expected to enforce these standards consistently and investigate all cash handling concerns promptly.
