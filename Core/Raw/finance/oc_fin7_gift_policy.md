# Gift Policy

## Purpose

This policy supports integrity, fairness, and transparency in the workplace.

Associates and their immediate family members are discouraged from receiving gifts from vendors, customers, partners, or other outside organizations, except as described below. Gifts can create conflicts of interest and influence business decisions.

**Immediate family** includes: spouse or partner, parents, children, siblings, and others living in the same household.

Exceptions to this policy require approval from the Chief People Officer or Chief Executive Officer.

---

## What Counts as a Gift

Includes but is not limited to:

- Physical items (electronics, clothing, gift cards).
- Meals or entertainment without a business purpose or required approval.
- Discounts or perks not available to all associates.

---

## Gift Rules

| Type | Rule |
|------|------|
| Gifts over $100 | Must be returned or shared with the team (raffle, donation, or display). Notify your supervisor and HR. Whenever possible, notify before accepting. |
| Entertainment (sports, golf, events) | The supplier must attend. If only one associate is invited and value exceeds $100, obtain supervisor or HR approval before accepting. |
| Gifts between associates for special occasions | Limit: **$200 per person**. Group gifts must be reasonable and not extravagant. |

---

## Unacceptable Gifts

Associates may never accept:

- Cash, loans, or financial instruments.
- Anything given in exchange for a favor or favorable treatment.
- Illegal, offensive, or inappropriate items.
- Exclusive discounts not available to all associates.
- Any gift related to government contracts.

---

## Generally Allowed

- Promotional items (pens, t-shirts) from public events.
- Reasonable meals or entertainment with a clear business context when the supplier is present.
- Small group gifts for birthdays, farewells, or similar occasions.
- Peer meals (no supplier present) under $100.
- Donations to public fundraising on behalf of associates.

---

## What to Do If You Receive a Non-Compliant Gift

1. Return it if possible.
2. If returning is not possible:
   - Share it with your team or department.
   - Notify your supervisor.
   - Or donate it to an approved charitable cause.

---

## Working with Vendors

Inform vendors and partners about this gift policy and ask for their cooperation.

---

## Best Practices

- Decline or return gifts that seem inappropriate or too valuable.
- Report unusual or borderline cases to your supervisor or HR.
- When in doubt, ask before accepting.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Disciplinary action up to and including termination.
- Termination of vendor or contractor relationships.

---
