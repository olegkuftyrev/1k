# ORDINA

## FIN-3 Expense Reimbursement Policy

### Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish consistent procedures for submitting, approving, and reimbursing business expenses.

Associates may be reimbursed for approved business expenses incurred while conducting company business.

---

# Eligible Expenses

Examples of reimbursable expenses include:

- Business travel.
- Mileage.
- Lodging.
- Meals during approved travel.
- Training expenses.
- Business supplies.
- Company-related purchases.
- Approved operational expenses.

All expenses must serve a legitimate business purpose.

---

# Expense Report Requirements

A reimbursement request must include:

- Associate name.
- Employee identification number.
- Department or cost center.
- Date of expense.
- Business purpose.
- Description of expense.
- Amount requested.
- Required approvals.

Incomplete submissions may delay reimbursement.

---

# Expense Documentation

Receipts are required for reimbursable expenses whenever possible.

Associates should:

- Retain original receipts.
- Protect receipts from damage.
- Submit documentation promptly.
- Organize receipts clearly.

Digital receipts are acceptable when permitted.

---

# Receipt Preparation

Receipts should:

- Be legible.
- Clearly show the vendor.
- Show the purchase date.
- Show the amount paid.
- Support the reported expense.

Multiple receipts should be organized to allow efficient review and approval.

---

# Submission Timeline

Expense reports should be submitted promptly after the expense occurs.

Whenever possible, expenses should be submitted during the same accounting period in which they were incurred.

Delayed submissions may result in reimbursement delays.

---

# Management Review

Approving leaders are responsible for reviewing:

- Business purpose.
- Reasonableness.
- Supporting documentation.
- Policy compliance.
- Accuracy.

Approval confirms that the expense appears appropriate and complies with company guidelines.

---

# Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Examples include:

- Personal expenses.
- Traffic violations.
- Alcohol unless specifically approved.
- Unapproved purchases.
- Expenses without a business purpose.
- Excessive or unreasonable spending.

---

# Fraud Prevention

Submitting false, altered, duplicated, or misleading expenses is prohibited.

Examples include:

- Duplicate receipts.
- Personal purchases.
- Altered amounts.
- False business purposes.
- Missing documentation.

Violations may result in disciplinary action.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing submissions.
- Verifying business necessity.
- Ensuring policy compliance.
- Maintaining financial accountability.
- Approving only legitimate expenses.

---

# Guiding Principle

Spend responsibly.

Document everything.

Submit promptly.

Approve carefully.
