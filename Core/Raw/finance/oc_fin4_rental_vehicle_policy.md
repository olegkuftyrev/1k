# Rental Vehicle Policy

## Coverage

The company carries commercial auto insurance for rental vehicles used by associates on company business.

Associates must read and understand this policy before renting a vehicle.

---

## Driver Requirements

To drive a rental vehicle on company business, an associate must:

- Hold a valid driver's license from their state of residence.
- Be at least 21 years of age.

Associates under 25 must book through the company's travel team to ensure the rental falls under the company's preferred program and insurance coverage at no additional cost.

---

## Preferred Rental Vendors

Book through company-approved preferred vendors whenever possible. Rentals through the preferred program include comprehensive, collision, and auto liability coverage built into the rate.

Contact the company travel team for the current list of approved vendors and booking procedures.

---

## Who May Drive

Only company associates on company business may drive the rental vehicle.

---

## Domestic Rentals — Preferred Program

For domestic rentals through the preferred program:

- Decline the rental agency's Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) and Liability Damage Waiver (LDW). The company's insurance already covers this.
- Do not purchase any additional insurance. Any extra coverage (e.g., personal items) is a personal expense and will not be reimbursed.

---

## Domestic Rentals — Outside Preferred Program

For domestic rentals from agencies outside the preferred program:

- Decline CDW and LDW. The company's policy still provides collision coverage.
- Contact Risk Management before finalizing to confirm coverage applies.

---

## International Rentals

For rentals in international locations (including Guam, Puerto Rico, and Canada):

- Purchase the local comprehensive/collision and liability insurance from the rental agency.
- The cost will be reimbursed by the company.
- The company's auto liability insurance applies as excess coverage after local insurance is purchased.

---

## Rental Period

Rental period may not exceed 30 days.

If a longer rental is needed, contact Risk Management before extending the rental.

---

## Fuel

Before returning the vehicle:

- Refill the tank with regular gasoline (not premium).
- Do not agree to prepaid refuel options offered by the rental agency.

---

## Specialized Vehicles

Fifteen-passenger vans, cargo trucks (with or without power lift gates), and some SUVs may not be covered under the company's auto insurance policy.

If a specialized vehicle is needed:

1. Purchase full insurance coverage from the rental agency.
2. Carry the appropriate valid driver's license for that vehicle class.

The cost of insurance for approved specialized vehicles will be reimbursed.

---

## Accidents

### Immediate Steps

1. Call 911 for any life-threatening or emergency situation.
2. Report the accident to local police immediately.
3. Obtain the investigating officer's full name, department, and police report number.
4. Contact Risk Management as soon as possible.

### Information to Exchange with Other Driver(s)

- Full name.
- Address.
- Phone number.
- Driver's license — state and number.
- Vehicle make, year, model, and color.
- Insurance information (photograph insurance cards if permitted).

### Documentation

- Take photos of all vehicle damage, including the rental vehicle.
- Report the accident to Risk Management within 24 hours.
- Notify the rental agency within 24 hours.

### Credit Card Charges

Some rental agencies may require a credit card to cover damages before insurance is processed.

If a personal card is charged, submit the expense to Risk Management for reimbursement. Reimbursement is subject to review and requires supporting documentation.

---

## Questions

Direct questions about rental vehicle coverage to Risk Management before the trip.
