# Travel and Expense Policy

## Purpose

This policy covers reimbursement for travel, lodging, meals, entertainment, and other business expenses. It ensures compliance with federal and state tax requirements.

All associates are covered. Violations may result in discipline, delay, or denial of reimbursement.

---

## Before Booking Travel

Determine whether the meeting can be held via video or teleconference. If in-person travel is the most effective approach, obtain supervisor approval before booking.

All travel arrangements — airfare, car rentals, lodging, shuttles, international visas, offsite conferences — must be made through the company's travel team or approved travel booking portal.

For emergencies or after business hours, contact the company's designated emergency travel service.

---

## Expense Reporting

Submit all expenses through the company expense system in a timely manner.

Expenses are reimbursable when they:

1. Have proper approvals.
2. Are ordinary, necessary, and reasonable.
3. Have a valid, specific business purpose.
4. Are supported by receipts and adequate detail.

Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

---

## Air Travel

### Booking Timing

- Book domestic flights at least **14 days in advance**.
- Book flights for conferences and annual events at least **21 days in advance**. Use the conference promotional code if one is available.
- All international travel must be coordinated through the company travel team.

### Ticket Class

**Domestic (under 8 hours non-stop):** Economy/Coach only. Upgrades are personal expenses.

**International (over 8 hours non-stop):**

- Senior leaders (Director level and above in field operations; Executive Director and above at support functions) may upgrade to Business Class at a reasonable cost, subject to second-level supervisor approval.
- All other associates may book Premium Economy if available, or Economy with better seating if available.
- All upgrades require second-level supervisor approval.

### Fare Flexibility

- Preferred flight times or airlines may be selected if the fare difference is less than **$200** over the lowest available domestic fare.
- For international flights, the allowable fare difference is **$400**.

### Unused Tickets

Reuse unused or partially used tickets. Notify the travel team promptly when a trip may cause tickets to go unused.

### Not Reimbursable

- Flight upgrades beyond what is permitted above.
- Frequent flyer membership costs.
- Airline lounge memberships.

### Spouse or Partner Travel

Spouse or partner travel is not reimbursed. A spouse or partner should not accompany an associate on company travel unless pre-approved by the department head (for support functions) or the associate's second-level supervisor (for operations).

### Key Personnel — Same Flight Restriction

To reduce organizational risk, certain combinations of senior leaders may not travel on the same commercial flight or company aircraft. Contact HR or the travel team for current restrictions before booking if multiple senior leaders are traveling together.

---

## Rail Travel

Rail travel follows the same guidelines as air travel when the cost exceeds $100.

---

## Car Rental and Ground Transportation

### Driver Requirements

A valid, current government-issued driver's license is required to rent a vehicle on company business.

### Ground Transportation

When a rental car is not needed, associates may use local ground transportation — airport shuttle, taxi, rideshare, or hotel pickup.

When traveling in a group, coordinate shared transportation.

When using rideshare apps, select a standard vehicle type. Upgrades to premium or luxury rides require supervisor approval.

### Rental Car Class

Book mid-size class whenever possible. Upgrades require prior supervisor approval.

Exception: if 3 or more associates are traveling together, no prior approval is required to upgrade to accommodate the group.

Luxury, premium, and specialty vehicles are prohibited.

### Preferred Rental Vendors

Book through the company's designated car rental vendors whenever possible. These rentals include collision, comprehensive, and auto liability coverage built into the rate.

### Insurance

**Preferred vendor rentals:** Coverage is included. No additional insurance needed.

**Other domestic agencies (associates 25 and older):** Decline collision, comprehensive, and liability insurance — company auto insurance covers this. Any additional insurance is a personal expense.

**Other domestic agencies (associates under 25):** Accept collision and liability insurance. The company will reimburse this cost.

**International rentals (including Canada, Guam, Puerto Rico):** Accept collision and liability insurance from the rental agency. The company will reimburse this cost. Company insurance applies as excess coverage.

### Parking and Tolls

Parking and toll fees incurred for business purposes are reimbursable.

### Traffic and Parking Tickets

Not reimbursable.

### Fuel

- Refill the tank before returning the vehicle.
- Use regular gasoline — not premium.
- Do not accept prepaid refuel options from the rental agency.

---

## Lodging

Book lodging through the company travel team to obtain negotiated rates.

### Nightly Rate Caps

- Standard rate: **$165/night** (excluding tax and fees).
- High-cost cities: **$235/night** (excluding tax and fees). High-cost cities include Boston, New York City, Bay Area (San Francisco, San Jose, Oakland), Portland, Washington DC, Honolulu, Chicago, Nashville, Los Angeles, Seattle, New Orleans, and Miami.

Rates above these limits require supervisor pre-approval.

### Room Sharing

- Senior field leaders and equivalent support function levels may have a single-occupancy room.
- Groups of 10 or more associates are generally assigned double-occupancy rooms. Associates are never required to share a room with another associate. Any associate may request a single room — contact the travel team or HR.
- Groups under 10 are encouraged but not required to share rooms.
- Supervisors and their direct reports are not recommended to share a room.
- When traveling internationally, only associates required for the trip may stay in company-provided lodging, unless pre-approved by the appropriate supervisor.

### Cancellations

- All rooms must be guaranteed for late arrival.
- If cancellation is foreseeable, notify the travel team in advance.
- If cancellation is unforeseeable, notify the travel team as soon as possible.
- Cancel within the hotel's required timeframe and retain the cancellation confirmation number.
- Avoidable no-show and late cancellation charges are not reimbursable.

## Group Meetings and Travel

For groups of 10 or more, contact the travel team to coordinate travel arrangements.

Expenses — airfare, ground transport, meals, equipment rentals, meeting rooms — are reimbursable in accordance with this policy.

---

## Mileage

Associates are eligible for mileage reimbursement when driving from one business location to another, excluding their normal commute. Associates with a company car allowance or company car program are not eligible.

Reimbursement rate: current IRS standard mileage rate (see IRS.gov for the current rate).

The expense report must include the starting point, destination, date, and business purpose.

---

## Meals During Travel

Meals are a reimbursable travel expense when an associate is traveling out of town or overnight for business.

- Per diem cap: **$60/day**, or as required by applicable law.
- Tips are reimbursed up to **18%** of the bill. Mandatory gratuities and service charges are also reimbursed.

---

## Business Meals

A business meal is food and beverages provided for the purpose of conducting a business discussion.

Reimbursable when:

- A business relationship exists with those present.
- The discussion before, during, or after the meal directly relates to that business relationship.
- Meals with employment candidates during the recruitment process.
- Meals initiated by a supervisor with their direct report(s) when a business discussion occurs.

**In-office meals:** Reimbursed up to **$20/person**.

**Tips:** Up to 18% of the bill. Mandatory gratuities and service charges are also reimbursed.

**Alcohol:** Associates 21 and older may include alcoholic beverages in a business dinner, provided the total is within the pre-approved budget amount authorized by the supervisor.

**Group meals:** When multiple associates attend a business meal or entertainment event, the most senior leader is responsible for paying and submitting for reimbursement. All participants' names must be included in the expense report.

---

## Business Entertainment

Entertainment expenses are reimbursable when they directly relate to company business — obtaining new business, continuing an existing relationship, or building associate morale — and either directly relate to a business discussion or directly precede or follow one.

---

## Associate Morale Events

The following are reimbursable up to the pre-approved budget set by the department head or regional leader:

- Team building events.
- Welcome and farewell events for associates.
- Holiday parties.

The following are reimbursable with department head or regional leader approval:

- Department-wide birthday celebrations: up to **$150** per month (covering all birthdays in the month). Individual birthday celebrations are personal and not reimbursable.
- Baby or wedding showers: up to **$100** per event per department.
- Holiday decoration contests: up to **$200** per department.
- Holiday decorations: up to **$200** per department.
- Bereavement flowers: up to **$100** from the applicable department.

---

## Gifts

- Gifts from associates to individual coworkers (birthdays, showers, holidays) are personal and not reimbursable.
- Gifts or gift cards to clients for a valid business purpose: up to **$50** are reimbursable.
- Gifts or gift cards over **$50** require supervisor pre-approval.

---

## Other Reimbursable Items

- Business postage and office supplies.
- Laundry starting from the second week of travel.
- Baggage tips (portage).
- First checked bag fee.
- Additional bag fees for trips of at least one week (requires supervisor approval).
- Internet service up to $15/day. Over $15 requires supervisor approval.
- Hotel self-parking. Valet parking requires supervisor pre-approval.

---

## Non-Reimbursable Items

- Video or movie rentals (in-flight or hotel).
- Alcohol outside of approved business meal context.
- Hotel mini-bar or room fridge items.
- Personal phone calls when a company phone is available.
- Personal grooming items (shampoo, toothbrush, soap, razors) and services (shoeshine, spa).
- Non-emergency medical expenses.
- Late fees or annual fees for personal credit cards.
- Room charges not otherwise covered by this policy.

---

## Personal Vehicle — Insurance and Accidents

Associates using their personal vehicle on company business are responsible for maintaining a valid driver's license and adequate personal auto insurance as required by law.

In an accident involving a personal vehicle while on company business:

- Call 911 immediately for any emergency.
- Report the incident to Risk Management with the police report and relevant information.
- Personal auto insurance is primary. Company insurance is secondary.
- For accidents where the associate is not at fault and losses are documented with a police report, the company may reimburse out-of-pocket vehicle damage expenses up to **$500** not recovered from the at-fault party.

---

## Corporate Credit Cards

Senior field leaders and equivalent support function managers may request a corporate credit card on an as-needed basis.

Cardholders are liable for all expenses on the card. A credit check is required before a card is issued.

Late payment penalties and fees are the cardholder's personal responsibility — they are not reimbursable.

---

## Phones and Devices

The company may provide mobile phones and computers for business use.

Associates are not required to use personal devices for company business. If a role requires it, notify your supervisor or HR so the company can assess options and potential reimbursement.

---

## Personal Property

The company is not responsible for loss or damage to personal property during business travel.

When traveling:

- Carry minimal cash.
- Use credit cards or traveler's checks.
- Leave personal valuables at home when possible.
- Stay alert in unfamiliar locations.

Associates with a medical condition should consult their doctor before traveling.

---

## Approval Process

1. Obtain supervisor approval before booking any travel.
2. Book through the company travel team or approved travel portal.
3. The booking confirmation is sent to the supervisor for final approval and compliance review.
4. The supervisor may approve, decline, or flag items that do not comply with this policy.
