# ORDINA

## FIN-2 Sales Tax Exemption Policy

### Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for processing tax-exempt transactions and ensuring compliance with applicable tax laws.

Sales tax exemptions may only be applied when supported by valid documentation and approved according to legal requirements.

---

# Eligible Tax-Exempt Organizations

Tax exemption may apply to:

- Government agencies.
- Public institutions.
- Qualified nonprofit organizations.
- Educational institutions.
- Medical facilities.
- Religious organizations.
- Approved charitable events.
- Resale businesses with valid resale certificates.

Tax exemptions must comply with local and state laws.

---

# Organizations That May Qualify

Examples include:

### Government Organizations

- Government agencies.
- Public departments.
- Official governmental meetings.

### Medical Facilities

- Hospitals.
- Rehabilitation facilities.
- Long-term care facilities.
- Patient meal programs.

### Nonprofit Organizations

Organizations established and operated exclusively for charitable purposes.

---

# Employee Purchases

Employees of exempt organizations are not automatically exempt from sales tax.

Personal purchases remain taxable unless specifically covered by applicable laws.

---

# Required Documentation

Before removing sales tax, the customer must provide one of the following:

- Tax exemption certificate.
- Resale certificate.
- Government exemption documentation.
- State-issued exemption documentation.
- Other legally accepted documentation.

If documentation is unavailable, the customer may be required to complete an exemption form.

---

# Approval Requirements

Managers should verify:

- Organization name.
- Exemption status.
- Required documentation.
- Valid dates.
- Applicable use of the exemption.

Tax exemptions should only be applied to qualifying transactions.

---

# Record Retention

All exemption documents shall be retained according to company record retention policies.

Documentation should include:

- Organization name.
- Date of purchase.
- Amount.
- Supporting documentation.
- Manager approval.

---

# Point-of-Sale Procedures

Tax-exempt transactions should:

1. Be entered according to approved operating procedures.
2. Include proper documentation.
3. Be recorded accurately.
4. Be available for audit and review.

---

# Improper Use of Tax Exemptions

Sales tax exemptions may not be used for:

- Employee meals.
- Personal purchases.
- Unauthorized discounts.
- Unsupported claims.
- Transactions lacking documentation.

Improper application of tax exemptions may result in:

- Financial liability.
- Tax assessments.
- Disciplinary action.
- Additional reporting requirements.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Verifying documentation.
- Protecting company compliance.
- Maintaining records.
- Training associates.
- Reporting tax-related concerns.

---

# Guiding Principle

No documentation.

No exemption.

Protect the business and comply with the law.
