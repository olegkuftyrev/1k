# Meals Policy

## Travel Meals

Meals are reimbursable when an associate is traveling out of town or overnight for business.

- Daily cap: **$60/day**, including tax and tips, or as required by applicable law.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable during travel, except as permitted under Business Meals or Celebration Meals below.
- Tips: up to **20%** of the bill, or any mandatory service charge — whichever is higher.
- When multiple associates are present, the highest-ranking leader pays and submits for reimbursement.

### Documentation Required

- Itemized meal receipt (hotel billing statements are not accepted).
- Location and business purpose of the trip.
- Names and titles of all attendees.

Submit within **60 days** of the travel date. Late submissions may be treated as taxable income.

Associates using a company credit card are only required to submit receipts for expenses over **$250**.

---

## Business Meals

A business meal is reimbursable when it includes a business-related conversation.

- Meals with job applicants during the interview process are reimbursable.
- Office meeting meals: up to **$30/person**, including tax and tips.
- When paying for others, list all names and titles in the expense report.
- For group meals, the highest-ranking leader pays and submits the expense.

### Alcohol

Associates 21 and older may have one alcoholic drink at dinner only, when:

- A senior field leader (Area Leader level or above) is present for operations associates.
- A people leader with direct reports is present for support function associates.
- The total stays within the $30/person limit.

Tips: up to **20%** or any mandatory service charge.

### Documentation Required

- Itemized meal receipt (hotel billing statements are not accepted).
- Location and business purpose.
- Names and titles of all attendees.

Submit within **60 days** of the expense date. Late submissions may be treated as taxable income.

Associates using a company credit card are only required to submit receipts for expenses over **$250**.

---

## Celebration Meals

Celebration meals are for milestones, holidays, or special team events.

- Annual allowance: **$120/person**, including tax and tips.
- The allowance may be used in one event or split across multiple events during the year.
- Supervisors are responsible for tracking usage against the per-person limit.
- For group celebrations, the highest-ranking leader pays and submits the expense.

### Alcohol

Associates 21 and older may have one alcoholic drink at dinner only, when:

- A Director or higher is present.
- The total stays within the $120/person annual allowance.

Tips: up to **20%** or any mandatory service charge.

### Documentation Required

- Itemized meal receipt (hotel billing statements are not accepted).
- Location and purpose of the event.
- Names and titles of all attendees.

Submit within **60 days** of the expense date. Late submissions may be treated as taxable income.

---

## Alcohol Conduct

Associates must use good judgment and maintain professional standards at all company-related events.

Overconsumption, misconduct, unprofessionalism, or unsafe behavior involving alcohol will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## Quick Reference

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Stay within the daily cap ($60 travel, $30 business meals) | Claim alcohol outside permitted situations |
| Tip up to 20% or follow the mandatory service charge | Exceed per-person limits without approval |
| Have one alcoholic drink at dinner only (21+, senior leader present) | Drink alcohol at lunch or without proper supervision |
| Track celebration allowance ($120/person/year) | Use celebration funds for non-approved events |
| Have the most senior leader pay and submit | Split bills to avoid policy limits |
| Include names and titles of all attendees on expense reports | Submit without itemized receipts |
| Maintain professionalism at all company events | Misuse alcohol — violations may result in termination |
