# ORDINA

## FIN-1 House Accounts & Customer Credit

### Purpose

House Accounts allow approved customers to receive products or services and pay through invoicing at a later date.

This program supports:

- Business accounts.
- Corporate clients.
- Schools and organizations.
- Non-profit organizations.
- Internal company departments.
- Approved catering customers.

Proper management of accounts receivable protects company assets and ensures timely collection of payments.

---

# Account Approval

New credit accounts must be approved before services are provided.

The following information should be collected:

- Business or organization name.
- Contact person.
- Billing address.
- Phone number.
- Email address.
- Tax documentation, if applicable.
- Requested credit limit.

Leadership approval is required before an account becomes active.

---

# Credit Limits

Each approved account shall receive an authorized credit limit.

Credit limits should consider:

- Payment history.
- Order frequency.
- Average order value.
- Organizational size.
- Financial risk.

Accounts may be suspended if balances exceed approved limits.

---

# Internal Accounts

Internal departments may charge approved expenses to designated cost centers.

Examples include:

- Meetings.
- Training events.
- Company events.
- Marketing activities.
- Leadership programs.

All internal charges require:

- Cost center identification.
- Authorized requestor.
- Description of the expense.

---

# Tax Exemption

Tax-exempt organizations must provide appropriate documentation as required by local laws.

Examples may include:

- Resale certificates.
- Government exemption certificates.
- Nonprofit exemption documentation.

Tax exemptions must be verified before removing sales tax.

---

# Invoicing

Invoices should include:

- Customer name.
- Invoice date.
- Products or services provided.
- Amount due.
- Payment terms.
- Contact information.

Invoices should be issued promptly following the transaction.

---

# Payment Collection

Payments should be:

- Recorded immediately.
- Deposited promptly.
- Applied to the correct account.

Supporting documentation should be retained according to company record retention requirements.

---

# Aging Accounts

Outstanding balances should be reviewed regularly.

Common aging categories:

- Current.
- 30 days.
- 60 days.
- 90 days.
- Over 90 days.

Leadership should follow up on overdue balances.

---

# Delinquent Accounts

Accounts with unpaid balances may be:

- Suspended.
- Restricted.
- Referred for collection.
- Written off according to accounting guidelines.

Large write-offs may require leadership approval.

---

# Recordkeeping

Accurate records shall be maintained for:

- Account approvals.
- Credit limits.
- Invoices.
- Payments.
- Outstanding balances.
- Collection efforts.
- Adjustments.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

Leaders are responsible for:

- Following approval procedures.
- Monitoring outstanding balances.
- Ensuring timely collection.
- Protecting company assets.
- Maintaining accurate records.

---

# Guiding Principle

Extend credit carefully.

Document every transaction.

Collect promptly.

Protect company assets.
