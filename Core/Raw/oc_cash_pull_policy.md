# ORDINA

## Cash Pull Policy

### Summary

Cash pulls protect associates, guests, and the business.

Excess cash should never remain inside the register. Large bills and excess cash must be transferred to the safe promptly to reduce theft risk and maintain proper cash balances.

---

## Purpose

Maintain register security, reduce cash exposure, and protect associates from theft and cash loss.

This policy applies to all associates responsible for operating a register.

---

# Cash Pull Requirements

Excess cash must be removed from the register throughout the shift.

Cash pulls should include:

- $100 bills
- $50 bills
- Excess $20 bills
- Additional cash that causes the drawer to exceed authorized limits

---

# Maximum Register Balance

The total amount of cash inside a register may not exceed:

**$700**

The POS system may alert the cashier when a cash pull is required.

Associates should perform the cash pull immediately after receiving the notification.

---

# Smart Safe Deposits

Cash pulled from the register must be deposited into the Smart Safe as soon as possible.

Cash should not remain:

- Inside the register.
- Inside pockets or aprons.
- At the service counter.
- Unsecured in work areas.

---

# Cash Handling Standards

Cash pulls should be performed discreetly and according to operational procedures.

Managers or Persons in Charge should support associates whenever additional assistance is needed.

Maintaining lower cash balances reduces risk and improves overall safety.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Allowing register balances to exceed authorized limits.
- Delaying required cash pulls.
- Storing excess cash inside the register.
- Keeping pulled cash outside the safe.
- Carrying company cash in personal belongings.
- Leaving cash unattended.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
