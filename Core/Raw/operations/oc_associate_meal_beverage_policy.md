# ORDINA

## Associate Meal & Beverage Policy

### Summary

Ordina provides one complimentary meal and unlimited fountain drinks to associates during each workday.

Meals are intended to support associates while working and must be consumed inside the restaurant. Every transaction, discount, and associate purchase must be properly recorded in the register.

The purpose of this policy is to maintain fairness, accountability, and consistent operational standards across all Ordina locations.

---

## Purpose

Provide meals and beverages to associates during their workday while maintaining clear operational and accountability standards.

This policy applies to all Ordina associates.

---

## Meal Policy

### 1. Associate Meal

Every associate receives one complimentary meal during each workday.

The meal may be taken:

- during the meal break, or
- after the shift while still in uniform.

Shift length does not affect eligibility.

### 2. Dine-In Only

Associate meals are for dine-in only.

Food may not leave the restaurant.

### 3. Fountain Drinks

Associates may consume fountain drinks at no cost while working or during breaks.

### 4. Associate Discount

Food and beverages purchased outside working hours or taken to-go receive a 20% associate discount.

---

## Register Procedures

### 1. Ring Every Item

All discounted purchases must be entered into the register.

No food or beverage may be provided without a transaction.

### 2. Receipt Approval

The associate and the Person in Charge (PIC) must sign the receipt.

The signed receipt must be placed under the register coin tray.

### 3. Daily Accounting

All signed receipts must be attached to the daily cash sheet.

---

## Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Giving unauthorized discounts.
- Providing free food or beverages without approval.
- Failing to ring transactions into the register.
- Allowing associate meals to leave the restaurant.
- Sharing discounts with guests or non-associates.
- Removing or discarding receipts.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
