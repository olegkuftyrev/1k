# ORDINA

## Discounts & Associate Benefits Policy

### Summary

Discounts and meal benefits are provided to support associates and create great guest experiences.

All discounts must be approved, properly recorded, and used only for their intended purpose. Unauthorized discounts or misuse of company benefits may result in corrective action.

---

## Purpose

Provide clear guidelines for associate meals, associate discounts, and promotional cards while protecting company assets and ensuring fairness.

This policy applies to all Ordina associates.

---

## Associate Meals

### 1. Family Meals

Family-style meals are provided twice daily following the lunch and dinner rush for associates working the applicable shift.

### 2. Dine-In Only

Associate meals are intended for on-site consumption only.

Meals may not be removed from the restaurant.

### 3. Fountain Drinks

Associates may consume fountain beverages at no charge while working or during breaks.

### 4. Policy Violations

Misuse of associate meals may result in corrective action, up to and including termination.

---

## Associate Discount

### 1. Associate Discount

Associates receive a 20% discount on food and beverages.

### 2. Eligible Use

The discount may be used:

- When the associate is off duty.
- When visiting another Ordina location with proper identification.
- For family or friends purchasing a meal while the associate is working.

### 3. Management Approval

All discounts require approval from the Manager or Person in Charge.

Unauthorized discounts are prohibited.

---

## Guest Recovery & Promotional Cards

### 1. Guest Cards

Guest recovery cards may only be issued by a manager.

These cards have a fixed value and cannot be exchanged for cash.

### 2. Redemption

When a guest card or promotional card is redeemed, the associate must notify the Manager or PIC.

### 3. Card Collection

Redeemed promotional cards must be turned in at the end of the shift.

---

## Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Giving unauthorized discounts.
- Sharing discounts without approval.
- Using another associate's discount.
- Taking associate meals off-site.
- Providing free food without authorization.
- Keeping redeemed promotional cards.
- Exchanging promotional cards for cash.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
