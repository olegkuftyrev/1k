# ORDINA

## Associate Illness Reporting Policy

### Summary

Associates who are sick can spread illness to guests, coworkers, and food.

Every associate is responsible for reporting symptoms, illnesses, or medical conditions that may affect food safety. Associates who are ill must notify management immediately and may not work until they are cleared to return.

Protecting guests always comes first.

---

# Purpose

Prevent foodborne illness and maintain a safe environment for guests and associates.

This policy applies to all associates.

---

# Reporting Requirements

Associates must notify the Manager or Person in Charge before starting work if any of the following conditions apply.

---

# Symptoms That Must Be Reported

Associates must immediately report:

- Diarrhea.
- Vomiting.
- Nausea.
- Sore throat with fever.
- Runny nose with uncontrolled symptoms.
- Persistent cough.
- Flu-like symptoms.

Associates experiencing these symptoms may not work until approved to return.

---

# Returning to Work

Associates returning from an illness-related absence must notify management before returning to work.

Management may require additional information before an associate resumes food handling duties.

---

# Household Illness

Associates must report if a household member or close contact is experiencing:

- Vomiting lasting more than 24 hours.
- Diarrhea lasting more than 24 hours.
- Certain communicable illnesses.

Management will determine whether work restrictions are necessary.

---

# Reportable Illnesses

Associates must immediately report a diagnosis or carrier status involving:

- Norovirus.
- Hepatitis A.
- Salmonella.
- Shigella.
- E. coli.
- Campylobacter.
- H1N1.
- Entamoeba histolytica.
- Staphylococcal infections associated with foodborne illness.
- Other communicable diseases that may affect food safety.

Associates diagnosed with these illnesses may not work until medically cleared.

---

# Wounds and Skin Conditions

Open wounds, cuts, or lesions containing drainage must be properly protected.

The following standards apply:

- Hand or finger wounds must be covered with an impermeable bandage and a single-use glove.
- Arm wounds must be covered with an impermeable covering.
- Other body wounds must be covered with a clean, secure bandage.

Associates with unprotected wounds may not handle food.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Evaluating reported symptoms.
- Restricting or excluding associates when necessary.
- Protecting guests and associates.
- Maintaining confidentiality.
- Following food safety requirements.

---

# Associate Responsibility

Compliance with this policy is a condition of employment.

Associates are expected to:

- Report symptoms honestly.
- Follow medical guidance.
- Practice proper hygiene.
- Follow all food safety procedures.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Reporting to work while experiencing excluded symptoms.
- Failing to report an illness.
- Concealing a diagnosis.
- Handling food with uncovered wounds.
- Returning to work without approval when required.
- Allowing an ill associate to continue working.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action and removal from food handling duties.
