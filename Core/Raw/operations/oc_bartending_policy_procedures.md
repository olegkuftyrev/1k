# ORDINA

## Bartending Policy & Procedures

### Summary

Bartenders are responsible for guest experience, beverage quality, inventory integrity, and alcohol compliance.

Every drink must be properly rung into the POS, measured accurately, and served according to company standards. Cleanliness, accountability, and responsible alcohol service are required at all times.

---

## Purpose

Provide consistent beverage quality, protect company assets, and deliver exceptional guest experiences.

This policy applies to all bartenders, managers, and associates working in the bar.

---

# Bartender Standards

## Guest First

Bar guests are the bartender's highest priority.

Bartenders are also responsible for preparing beverage orders placed by servers and service staff.

Service should always be:

- Prompt
- Friendly
- Accurate
- Professional

---

## Product Knowledge

Bartenders should be able to answer guest questions regarding:

- Cocktails
- Beer selections
- Wine selections
- Menu items
- Ingredients
- Restaurant history

Knowledge creates confidence and improves the guest experience.

---

## Pride in the Bar

Bartenders are responsible for:

- Clean workstations
- Organized bars
- Fully stocked supplies
- Proper drink presentation
- Smooth shift transitions

Every bartender should leave the station ready for the next shift.

---

# Hiring Requirements

All bartender candidates must complete the required background screening process before working with alcohol.

Candidates must meet company standards for alcohol service and cash handling.

---

# Opening and Closing Procedures

## Opening Duties

The bartender is responsible for:

- Stocking liquor.
- Stocking beer and wine.
- Stocking coolers and shelves.
- Preparing supplies.
- Counting daily inventory.

---

## Closing Duties

The closing bartender must:

- Restock products.
- Clean the bar.
- Complete side work.
- Prepare the station for the next shift.

Managers must approve checkout before associates leave.

---

# Beverage Orders

## POS Requirements

Every beverage requires a POS transaction.

Verbal orders are not permitted.

No drink may be prepared without an order ticket.

---

## Order Management

- Complete orders in ticket order.
- Mark completed tickets.
- Organize drinks for servers.
- Ensure beverages reach guests promptly.

---

## Bar Guests

Every bar guest must receive:

- An itemized receipt.
- An updated check for additional orders.
- Accurate charges for all beverages.

---

# Alcohol Service

## Associates

Associates may not:

- Consume alcohol before work.
- Consume alcohol during work.
- Consume alcohol immediately after work.
- Sit at the bar while working.

Off-duty associates are treated as regular guests.

---

# Cash Accountability

The bar operates under a cash accountability system.

Every item sold must be:

- Entered into the POS.
- Properly charged.
- Accurately recorded.

Bartenders are responsible for:

- Liquor inventory.
- Beverage inventory.
- Sales accuracy.
- Cash accountability.

---

# Beverage Preparation Standards

## Glassware

- Use the correct glass for every beverage.
- Inspect glassware for chips or cracks.
- Never use damaged glassware.
- Handle glasses by the base or stem.
- Never touch the rim.

---

## Ice Standards

Ice must be:

- Clean
- Hard
- Dry
- Properly stored

Never:

- Handle ice with hands.
- Scoop ice with glassware.
- Store products inside ice bins.

If glass breaks near an ice bin, discard all ice immediately.

---

## Beverage Temperatures

Maintain proper serving temperatures:

- Beer: cold
- White wine: chilled
- Red wine: room temperature
- Chilled glassware when required

---

## Measuring Drinks

All liquor pours must use a jigger.

Free pouring is prohibited.

Measured pours ensure:

- Consistency
- Quality
- Inventory accuracy
- Cost control

Over-pouring may result in corrective action.

---

## Mixers and Garnishes

Mixers and garnishes must remain:

- Fresh
- Clean
- Properly stored

Do not use products that fail quality standards.

Garnishes should be placed on the rim of the glass unless otherwise specified.

---

# Server Beverage Orders

Servers must ring beverages before pickup.

Bartenders prepare the beverages.

Servers garnish and deliver drinks to guests.

Drinks should never sit unattended.

Managers should be notified if support is needed.

---

# Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Reviewing inventory counts.
- Verifying beverage orders.
- Monitoring inventory levels.
- Conducting spot checks.
- Reviewing sales and inventory variances.
- Monitoring compliance.
- Supporting bartenders during busy periods.

Inventory counts should be rotated among managers and supervisors.

---

# Loss Prevention

Management may review:

- Inventory counts.
- CCTV footage.
- Sales records.
- Bottle levels.

Suspected theft or inventory discrepancies must be investigated immediately.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Free pouring.
- Serving alcohol without a transaction.
- Accepting verbal orders.
- Drinking while on duty.
- Allowing associates to drink during shifts.
- Over-pouring alcohol.
- Failing to ring beverages into the POS.
- Falsifying inventory counts.
- Using poor quality products.
- Leaving the bar unclean.
- Concealing theft or inventory loss.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
