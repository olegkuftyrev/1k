# Right Items, Right Amount — Guest FAQ

**Internal training use only. Do not distribute externally.**

These responses are for use by operations teams when guests ask about order verification or portioning procedures.

---

## Right Items

**Q: Why is a team member double-checking my bag or plate?**

A: We are verifying your order to make sure you received exactly what you ordered.

---

## Right Amount

**Q: Why are team members weighing plate containers?**

A: Consistent portions matter to us. The scale is a training tool that helps team members maintain the same portion size on every plate, so every guest gets the same experience.

**Q: Why isn't every plate being weighed?**

A: Our team is trained to portion using standardized scoops and recipes. The scale supports training and does not need to be used on every order.
