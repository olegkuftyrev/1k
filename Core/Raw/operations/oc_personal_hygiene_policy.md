# ORDINA

## Personal Hygiene Policy

### Summary

Personal hygiene protects our guests, our associates, and our food.

Every associate is responsible for maintaining the **Five Food Safety Foundations.** These standards reduce contamination risks, support food safety, and create a clean and professional environment.

Food safety begins with people.

---

# Purpose

Establish personal hygiene standards that protect guests, associates, and food products.

This policy applies to all associates.

---

# The Five Food Safety Foundations

Personal hygiene at Ordina is built upon five standards:

1. Appearance
2. Health
3. Handwashing
4. Hand Care
5. Hair Control

Every associate is responsible for maintaining these standards during every shift.

---

# 1. Appearance

Associates must:

- Wear a clean uniform each shift.
- Maintain a professional appearance.
- Replace damaged or heavily soiled uniforms.
- Wear approved uniforms and protective equipment.

Personal appearance reflects our standards and our brand.

---

# 2. Health

Associates must immediately notify management if they experience:

- Vomiting.
- Diarrhea.
- Fever.
- Jaundice.
- Sore throat with fever.
- Flu-like symptoms.
- Any communicable illness.

Associates experiencing symptoms that may impact food safety must not work until approved to return.

Protecting guests is more important than completing a shift.

---

# 3. Handwashing

Handwashing is the most important food safety practice.

## Proper Handwashing

1. Wet hands with warm water.
2. Apply soap.
3. Scrub hands, fingers, and wrists for at least 20 seconds.
4. Rinse thoroughly.
5. Dry with a single-use paper towel.

---

## Handwashing Required

Associates must wash their hands:

- When arriving to work.
- At least once every hour.
- After using the restroom.
- Before handling food.
- After handling raw products.
- After coughing or sneezing.
- After eating, drinking, or smoking.
- After touching hair, face, or clothing.
- When changing tasks.
- After taking out trash.

Hand sinks must always have:

- Hot water above 100°F.
- Soap.
- Paper towels.

---

# 4. Hand Care

Hands and fingernails must remain clean and properly maintained.

The following standards apply:

- Fingernails must be short and clean.
- Artificial nails are prohibited.
- Nail polish is prohibited.
- Only one plain wedding band may be worn.
- Gloves must cover any bandaged finger or hand.
- Watches and wrist jewelry are prohibited while preparing food.

---

# 5. Hair Control

Hair must be:

- Clean.
- Controlled.
- Restrained away from the face.

Long hair must be secured using:

- Hair nets.
- Braids.
- Buns.
- Approved hats or head coverings.

Hair must never create a contamination risk.

---

# Glove Standards

Gloves are required when handling ready-to-eat foods.

Associates must:

- Wash hands before putting on gloves.
- Wash hands after removing gloves.
- Change gloves when changing tasks.
- Replace gloves at least every hour.
- Replace gloves immediately if contaminated or damaged.

Gloves do not replace proper handwashing.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Working while sick.
- Failing to report symptoms.
- Wearing artificial nails.
- Wearing nail polish.
- Wearing watches or jewelry during food preparation.
- Failing to wash hands properly.
- Reusing disposable gloves.
- Wearing dirty uniforms.
- Touching ready-to-eat food with contaminated hands.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action and removal from food handling duties.
