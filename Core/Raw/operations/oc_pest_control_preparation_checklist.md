# ORDINA

## Pest Control Preparation Checklist

### Purpose

Prepare the restaurant for scheduled pest control service while protecting food, equipment, and food contact surfaces.

Managers are responsible for ensuring the restaurant is properly prepared before service begins.

---

# Before Service

## Access

☐ Unlock all restricted areas.

☐ Provide access to:

- Manager office.
- Storage rooms.
- Chemical storage.
- Lockers.
- Trash and dumpster areas.

☐ Leave exhaust hoods and ventilation systems operating.

☐ Place pest control records and service documents at the manager station.

---

# Kitchen Preparation

## Clear

☐ Pull movable equipment 18–24 inches from walls where possible.

☐ Move exposed food into approved cold storage.

☐ Empty sauce carts and store products properly.

☐ Remove exposed utensils, containers, cups, straws, trays, and smallwares from wall-mounted shelving.

☐ Clear shelves beneath work tables and sinks.

---

## Cover

Approved materials:

- Plastic wrap.
- Plastic sheeting.
- Disposable plastic bags.

☐ Cover exposed utensils.

☐ Cover cups and lids.

☐ Cover food containers.

☐ Cover trays and flatware.

☐ Cover condiments.

☐ Tuck covering material completely under items.

☐ Ensure items stored on wire shelving are covered on all sides.

---

## Clean

Before pest service:

☐ Clean under equipment.

☐ Clean behind equipment.

☐ Clean floor drains.

☐ Clean grease traps.

☐ Clean fryer areas.

☐ Clean storage areas.

☐ Remove all food debris.

---

# Beverage Station

## Clear

☐ Remove exposed cups.

☐ Remove straws.

☐ Remove condiments.

☐ Remove employee beverages.

---

## Cover

☐ Cover cups.

☐ Cover flatware.

☐ Cover trays.

☐ Cover straws.

☐ Cover condiments.

---

## Clean

☐ Clean beneath beverage stations.

☐ Clean behind equipment.

☐ Remove spills and debris.

---

# Dining Room

## Clean

☐ Clean under booths.

☐ Clean under tables.

☐ Clean under chairs.

☐ Remove food debris.

☐ Remove standing water.

☐ Sweep and mop dining room floors.

---

# After Service

## Remove Protective Materials

☐ Discard all plastic coverings.

☐ Never reuse plastic coverings.

☐ Wash hands after cleanup.

---

## Sanitize Food Contact Surfaces

☐ Wash surfaces with warm soapy water.

☐ Apply approved sanitizer.

☐ Allow surfaces to air dry.

This includes:

- Countertops.
- Tables.
- Beverage stations.
- Food contact surfaces.
