# ORDINA

## Food Safety Bonus Structure

### Purpose

Food safety performance directly impacts leadership compensation.

Audit results measure the ability of leaders to maintain safe operations, develop teams, and sustain operational standards.

---

# General Manager

| Final Audit Score | Bonus Eligibility |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| 90% or higher | Full Bonus |
| 85% – 89% | 50% Bonus |
| 84% or below | No Bonus |

---

# Area Manager

| Area Passing Rate | Bonus Eligibility |
|-------------------|-------------------|
| 87.5% or higher | Full Bonus |
| 80% – 87.4% | 50% Bonus |
| 79% or below | No Bonus |

Passing rate is determined by the percentage of locations that pass food safety audits within the assigned area.

---

# Regional Director

| Regional Passing Rate | Bonus Eligibility |
|-----------------------|-------------------|
| 90% or higher | Full Bonus |
| 80% – 89% | 50% Bonus |
| 79% or below | No Bonus |

Regional leaders are responsible for maintaining food safety standards across all assigned locations.

---

# Leadership Expectations

Food safety performance is measured by:

- Audit results.
- Operational consistency.
- Corrective actions.
- Team training.
- Sustained compliance.

Leaders are expected to maintain audit-ready standards every day.

---

# Philosophy

Food safety is not only an operational metric.

It is a leadership responsibility that directly impacts:

- Guest safety.
- Associate safety.
- Brand reputation.
- Business performance.
- Leadership accountability.
