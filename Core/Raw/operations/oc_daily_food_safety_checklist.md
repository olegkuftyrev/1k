# ORDINA

## Daily Food Safety Checklist

### Purpose

This checklist is used to verify food safety standards during daily operations.

Managers are expected to complete the checklist, correct deficiencies immediately, and coach associates when standards are not met.

---

# Thermometers

☐ All thermometers calibrated to 32°F.

☐ Dedicated thermometer available for grilled chicken.

---

# Reach-In Coolers

☐ Food temperature below 41°F.

☐ Raw chicken stored below other raw meats.

☐ Food covered with lids.

☐ Products properly date marked.

---

# Prep Cooler

☐ Kale and cabbage properly stored.

☐ Cooler not overfilled.

---

# Hot Holding

☐ Hot food maintained above 135°F.

☐ Induction units operating properly.

☐ Steam table water between 180°F and 200°F.

☐ Rice maintained above 135°F.

☐ Rice holding cabinet above 144°F.

---

# Cooking Temperatures

☐ All products cooked to 165°F minimum.

☐ Thickest part of grilled chicken above 165°F.

---

# Walk-In Cooler

☐ Food maintained below 41°F.

☐ Cooling procedures followed.

☐ Approved thawing methods used.

☐ Food covered.

☐ Products date marked.

☐ Food stored 6 inches above floor.

☐ Ready-to-eat foods stored above raw meats.

☐ Raw chicken stored below other proteins.

---

# Equipment & Food Contact Surfaces

☐ Equipment cleaned after use.

☐ Food contact surfaces clean.

☐ Thermometers sanitized.

☐ Rice cabinet cleaned nightly.

---

# Water & Handwashing

☐ Water available throughout the restaurant.

☐ Hot water reaches 120°F.

☐ Hand sinks reach 100°F.

☐ Hand sinks accessible.

☐ Hand sinks used only for handwashing.

☐ Three-compartment sink above 110°F.

☐ Soap and paper towels available.

---

# Employee Practices

☐ Gloves used when required.

☐ Associates appear healthy.

☐ Proper handwashing observed.

☐ Handwashing when entering kitchen.

☐ Handwashing between tasks.

☐ Food and drinks consumed only in approved areas.

☐ Associate drinks properly stored.

☐ Temperature logs complete.

---

# PPE

☐ Masks and gloves available.

☐ PPE worn correctly.

☐ Hand sanitizer available.

---

# Operations

☐ Food containers labeled.

☐ Food prepared in approved areas.

☐ Produce washed.

☐ Prep sink sanitized between uses.

☐ Microwave procedures followed.

☐ Utensils cleaned every four hours.

---

# Sanitizer & Chemicals

☐ Sanitizer available.

☐ Sanitizer buckets available.

☐ Test strips available.

☐ Sanitizer concentration correct.

☐ Chemical bottles labeled.

☐ Chemicals stored properly.

☐ Approved chemicals only.

---

# Sewage & Plumbing

☐ Drains operating properly.

☐ No sewage backups.

☐ Restrooms functioning properly.

---

# Pest Control

☐ No rodents.

☐ No droppings.

☐ No cockroaches.

☐ No flies.
