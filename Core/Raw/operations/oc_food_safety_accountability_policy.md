# ORDINA

## Food Safety Accountability Policy

### Summary

Food safety is a leadership responsibility.

Failed food safety audits affect accountability, bonus eligibility, and continued leadership status. General Managers and Area Managers are expected to maintain food safety standards every day and correct risks before they become audit failures.

---

## Purpose

Set clear accountability standards for EcoSure and Health Department audit results.

This policy applies to General Managers, Area Managers, and other leaders responsible for restaurant food safety performance.

---

# Audit Failure Standard

An EcoSure or Health Department audit is considered failed when the final result does not meet company standards.

For EcoSure audits, a final score of **89% or below** is considered a failed audit.

---

# General Manager Accountability

## First Failed Audit

The first failed EcoSure or Health Department audit results in a **Written Warning**.

## Second Failed Audit

A second failed audit within 12 months of the first failure results in a **Final Warning**.

If the second failure occurs more than 12 months after the first failure, a second Written Warning may be issued.

## Third Failed Audit

A third failed audit within 12 months of the second failure may result in disciplinary action, up to and including reclassification or termination.

If the third failure occurs more than 12 months after the previous failure, a second Final Warning may be issued.

---

# General Manager Bonus Impact

## Final Score of 90% or Higher

General Manager receives full bonus eligibility.

## Final Score of 85% to 89%

General Manager receives half bonus eligibility.

## Final Score of 84% or Below

General Manager receives no bonus eligibility.

---

# Area Manager Accountability

## Two Failed Audits Within 6 Months

Two failed EcoSure or Health Department audits within 6 months result in a **Written Warning**.

## Additional Failed Audit

One additional failed audit within 6 months of the last failure results in a **Final Warning**.

If the second failure occurs more than 6 months after the first failure, a second Written Warning may be issued.

## Failure After Final Warning

One additional failed audit within 6 months of a Final Warning may result in disciplinary action, up to and including reclassification.

If the third failure occurs more than 6 months after the last failed audit, a second Final Warning may be issued.

---

# Area Manager Bonus Impact

## Passing Rate of 87.5% or Higher

Area Manager receives full bonus eligibility.

This also applies if no more than one assigned location fails, whichever standard is lower.

## Passing Rate of 80% to 87.4%

Area Manager receives half bonus eligibility.

## Passing Rate of 79% or Below

Area Manager receives no bonus eligibility.

---

# Leadership Expectations

Leaders are expected to:

- Maintain audit-ready standards daily.
- Train associates on food safety procedures.
- Correct risks immediately.
- Review audit results with the team.
- Create corrective action plans after failures.
- Follow up until standards are sustained.

---

# Corrective Action Process

After a failed audit, leadership must:

1. Review the audit results.
2. Identify root causes.
3. Assign corrective actions.
4. Retrain the team.
5. Verify completion.
6. Monitor for repeat issues.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Ignoring food safety risks.
- Falsifying logs or records.
- Coaching only before scheduled audits.
- Failing to correct repeat violations.
- Concealing audit failures.
- Pressuring associates to hide issues.

Failure to maintain food safety standards may result in corrective action, bonus impact, reclassification, or termination.
