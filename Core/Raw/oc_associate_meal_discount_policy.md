# ORDINA

## Associate Meal & Discount Policy

### Summary

Ordina provides meal benefits to support associates during and outside of work.

Associates receive complimentary meal options during approved meal breaks and discounted meals while on or off duty. All meals must be properly rung into the POS, consumed according to policy, and may not be shared.

Alcohol is excluded from all discounts and complimentary offerings.

---

## Purpose

Provide clear meal benefits while maintaining fairness, accountability, and proper food cost controls.

This policy applies to all Ordina associates.

---

# Complimentary Meal During Shift

Associates are eligible for one complimentary meal during their scheduled 30-minute meal break.

Complimentary meals:

- Must be consumed inside the restaurant.
- May not be taken to-go.
- May not be shared.
- Apply only during the scheduled meal break.

### Eligible Complimentary Items

- WB
- WB Jr.
- Whatachick'n Chicken Strips (3 pieces)
- Breakfast on a Bun
- Honey Butter Biscuit
- Taquito with Cheese
- Slice of Cheese Pizza
- Slice of Pepperoni Pizza

Complimentary meals are limited to eligible menu items only.

---

# Associate Discounts

### While Working

Associates receive a 50% discount on:

- Food
- Fountain beverages

Alcohol is excluded.

### Off Duty

Associates receive a 20% discount on:

- Food
- Fountain beverages

Alcohol is excluded.

Discount eligibility applies regardless of shift length.

---

# Meal Rules

### Separate Benefits

Complimentary meals and discounted purchases may not be combined within the same transaction.

### No Sharing

Associate meals, discounts, and complimentary items are intended only for the associate receiving the benefit.

Sharing meals or discounts is prohibited.

### Unclaimed Orders

Online orders or third-party delivery orders that are not picked up must be discarded.

Unclaimed food may not be taken home by associates.

---

# Register Procedures

### Ring Every Transaction

All associate meals must be entered into the POS.

The appropriate benefit must be applied:

- Complimentary meal during an approved break.
- 50% discount while working.
- 20% discount while off duty.

### Separate Transactions

Each associate must complete their own transaction.

Multiple associate meals may not be combined into one transaction.

### Receipts

Receipts must be provided for all associate purchases.

Complimentary meals must still be recorded through the designated POS function.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Taking complimentary meals to-go.
- Sharing associate meals.
- Sharing discounts.
- Combining multiple associate meals in one transaction.
- Taking unclaimed online orders home.
- Applying unauthorized discounts.
- Combining complimentary meals with discounted purchases.
- Providing alcohol through associate discounts.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
