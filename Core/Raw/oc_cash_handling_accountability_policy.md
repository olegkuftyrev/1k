# ORDINA

## Cash Handling & Accountability Policy

### Summary

Every transaction must be accurate, recorded, and traceable.

Each associate is responsible for their own register, cash, and transactions. Registers may not be shared, unauthorized discounts are prohibited, and all cash must be properly deposited at the end of each shift.

Accountability protects both the business and the associate.

---

## Purpose

Maintain cash accuracy, prevent loss, and ensure accountability for all cash transactions.

This policy applies to all associates handling cash or operating a register.

---

## Start of Shift

### 1. Count the Register

The associate assigned to the register must count the starting cash before beginning work.

### 2. Authorized Starting Amount

The register must begin at the approved company balance.

Register funds may not exceed the authorized amount.

### 3. Individual Login

Associates must log into the register using their own credentials.

Sharing login credentials is prohibited.

### 4. Personal Banking

Associates responsible for tips or cash banks must maintain their own assigned funds.

---

## During the Shift

### 1. One Associate Per Register

Registers may only be used by the associate currently logged in.

Managers and other associates may not use another person's register.

### 2. Ring Every Transaction

All food and beverage items must be entered into the POS.

Orders may not be prepared without a valid transaction.

### 3. Provide Receipts

Guests must receive a receipt for every transaction.

### 4. Keep the Drawer Closed

Register drawers must remain closed between transactions.

### 5. Deletes and Refunds

All deletes, voids, and refunds require manager or PIC approval.

### 6. Discounts

All discounts require approval from the manager or person in charge.

Unauthorized discounts may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

### 7. Emergency Drawer Access

The emergency drawer key may only be used during emergencies, including robbery situations.

Associates should know the emergency procedure but may not use the key for normal operations.

---

## End of Shift

### 1. Close the Register

Associates must close their register before leaving their shift.

### 2. Print Checkout Report

A drawer checkout report must be printed at the end of every shift.

### 3. Tip Distribution

Tip calculations and tip payouts must be completed before closing.

### 4. Declare Tips

Associates must enter declared tips into the POS system before clocking out.

### 5. Management Verification

The manager or PIC must verify cash balances, deposits, and tip payouts.

### 6. Deposit Funds

Associates must use their own safe code to access the safe and deposit cash sales.

Safe codes may not be shared.

---

## Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Sharing registers.
- Sharing login credentials.
- Allowing another associate to use your register.
- Giving unauthorized discounts.
- Failing to provide receipts.
- Leaving the register drawer open.
- Processing transactions without ringing sales.
- Sharing safe codes or register passwords.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
