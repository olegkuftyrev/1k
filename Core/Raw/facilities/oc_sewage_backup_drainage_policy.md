# ORDINA

## Sewage Backup & Drainage Policy

### Summary

Sewage backups create an immediate food safety risk.

If sewage or wastewater enters food preparation areas, the restaurant may be required to stop operations until the issue has been corrected. Managers must act quickly to protect guests, associates, and food products.

When in doubt, safety comes first.

---

# Purpose

Establish clear guidelines for responding to sewage backups, drainage issues, and restroom failures.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Active Sewage Backup in Food Preparation Areas

An active sewage backup occurs when wastewater or sewage overflows from:

- Floor drains.
- Floor sinks.
- Prep sinks.
- Utility sinks.
- Plumbing fixtures.

Any amount of wastewater on the floor in a food preparation area is considered an active backup.

## Required Action

If an active sewage backup occurs in a food preparation area:

- Stop food production immediately.
- Restrict access to affected areas.
- Contact management immediately.
- Contact emergency repair services.
- Close the restaurant if necessary.
- Do not resume operations until repairs are completed and the area has been cleaned and sanitized.

---

# Slow Drains

A slow drain is not considered an active sewage backup.

The restaurant may remain open if:

- Water drains properly.
- No overflow occurs.
- No contamination is present.

Repairs should be scheduled immediately to prevent further deterioration.

---

# Restroom Failures

Associates must have access to functioning restroom facilities.

If all restroom toilets or sinks become unusable because of sewage backup:

- Management must be notified immediately.
- Repairs must be arranged immediately.
- Operations may need to stop until facilities are restored.

---

# Single Fixture Failure

If one toilet or sink is unavailable but another fixture remains operational:

- The restaurant may remain open.
- The fixture should be marked "Out of Service."
- Repairs should be scheduled immediately.

---

# Shared Facility Locations

For locations that share restrooms with another facility:

- Notify property management immediately.
- Ensure handwashing facilities remain available.
- Continue operations only if food safety is not affected.

---

# Cleaning and Recovery

After repairs are completed:

- Remove contaminated materials.
- Clean all affected surfaces.
- Sanitize all food contact areas.
- Dispose of contaminated food.
- Verify drains are functioning properly.
- Document corrective actions.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Assessing the situation.
- Protecting guests and associates.
- Contacting repair vendors.
- Determining operational status.
- Communicating with leadership.
- Verifying safe reopening conditions.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Operating with active sewage backups in food areas.
- Preparing food in contaminated areas.
- Ignoring drainage issues.
- Delaying repairs.
- Allowing associates to work in unsafe conditions.
- Reopening before proper cleaning and sanitation.

Failure to follow this policy may result in food safety violations, operational review, or corrective action.

---

# Guiding Principle

If wastewater or sewage creates a risk to food safety, operations must stop until the hazard has been eliminated.
