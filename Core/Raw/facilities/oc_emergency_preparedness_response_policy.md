# ORDINA

## Emergency Preparedness & Response Policy

### Summary

The safety of our guests and associates is always the first priority.

Every emergency requires three actions:

1. Protect people.
2. Secure the building.
3. Communicate quickly.

Associates should remain calm, follow emergency procedures, and notify leadership immediately.

---

## Purpose

Provide clear procedures for responding to emergencies while protecting associates, guests, company assets, and operations.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Emergency Priorities

### Be Prepared

- Understand local risks.
- Maintain emergency contacts.
- Train associates regularly.
- Inspect emergency equipment.
- Maintain building systems.

### Be Aware

- Monitor weather and local conditions.
- Review emergency procedures.
- Prepare the building when threats arise.
- Communicate with leadership.

### Be Safe

- Protect people first.
- Avoid unnecessary risks.
- Evacuate when necessary.
- Contact emergency services immediately.

---

# Emergency Contacts

Management should immediately notify appropriate leadership and support teams during emergencies.

Examples include:

- Operations leadership.
- Facilities.
- Risk management.
- Loss prevention.
- Emergency services.

---

# Kitchen Fire

## Prevention

- Inspect fire suppression systems regularly.
- Maintain exhaust systems.
- Clean grease buildup.
- Train associates on fire safety.

## Response

- Remain calm.
- Use the proper extinguisher.
- Activate the suppression system if necessary.
- Call 911.
- Evacuate guests and associates.
- Account for all personnel.

## Recovery

- Re-enter only when authorized.
- Dispose of contaminated food.
- Clean affected areas.
- Verify equipment operation before reopening.

---

# Robbery, Burglary, or Theft

## Response

- Remain calm.
- Follow security procedures.
- Do not resist.
- Protect associates and guests.
- Contact law enforcement.
- Notify management.

## Recovery

- Preserve evidence.
- Review security footage.
- Document damages or losses.

---

# Severe Weather

Severe weather events may include:

- Snowstorms
- Blizzards
- Tornadoes
- Hurricanes
- Flooding
- Wildfires

Managers should:

- Monitor weather reports.
- Communicate with leadership.
- Prepare the building.
- Secure cash and inventory.
- Determine if closure is necessary.

---

# Snow and Winter Weather

Managers should:

- Prepare vendors.
- Maintain safe walkways.
- Monitor road conditions.
- Protect plumbing systems.
- Communicate closures early.

Associates should never travel in unsafe conditions.

---

# Tornado

During a tornado:

- Move to interior areas.
- Stay away from windows.
- Protect the head and neck.
- Remain sheltered until conditions improve.

---

# Hurricane

Before a hurricane:

- Secure exterior items.
- Protect entrances.
- Secure cash.
- Shut down utilities if necessary.

Evacuation orders must always be followed.

---

# Earthquake

During an earthquake:

- Drop.
- Cover.
- Hold.

Stay away from:

- Windows.
- Glass.
- Falling objects.

Evacuate only when safe.

---

# Flooding

If flooding occurs:

- Shut off utilities if safe.
- Protect important assets.
- Evacuate early.
- Do not enter flooded buildings until approved.

---

# Power Outage or Lightning

During power loss:

- Protect guests and associates.
- Monitor food safety.
- Inspect electrical systems.
- Contact appropriate support vendors.

Extension cords should never be used as permanent solutions.

---

# Wildfire

When wildfire threatens:

- Monitor evacuation notices.
- Prepare associates.
- Secure cash and inventory.
- Shut off gas if evacuating.
- Leave immediately when ordered.

---

# Building Damage, Vandalism, or Arson

If damage occurs:

- Contact emergency services.
- Protect guests and associates.
- Evacuate if necessary.
- Secure the property.
- Document damages.
- Cooperate with authorities.

---

# Civil Disturbance, Riots, or Looting

During civil disturbances:

- Follow law enforcement instructions.
- Evacuate associates and guests.
- Secure the building.
- Protect people first.
- Reassess damage after conditions improve.

---

# Food Safety During Emergencies

Power loss or equipment failure may affect food safety.

Managers must:

- Evaluate food temperatures.
- Discard unsafe products.
- Follow food safety procedures.
- Document product losses.

---

# Disaster Supply Kit

Each location should maintain emergency supplies including:

- Drinking water.
- Non-perishable food.
- Flashlights.
- Batteries.
- First aid supplies.
- Emergency radio.
- Blankets.
- Hygiene supplies.
- Tool kits.
- Emergency contact information.

---

# Documentation Requirements

Following any emergency:

- Take photographs.
- Document damages.
- Record product losses.
- Report injuries.
- Complete required incident reports.

---

# Prohibited Actions

The following actions are not permitted:

- Ignoring evacuation orders.
- Re-entering unsafe buildings.
- Taking unnecessary risks.
- Operating damaged equipment.
- Concealing damages or losses.
- Failing to report emergencies.
- Allowing associates to remain in unsafe conditions.

---

# Emergency Philosophy

People come first.

Protect associates.
Protect guests.
Protect the community.

Property and equipment can be replaced. Human safety cannot.
