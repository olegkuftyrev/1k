# ORDINA

## Exterior Maintenance Policy

### Summary

The exterior of every Ordina location creates the guest's first impression.

Landscaping, sidewalks, windows, building appearance, and parking areas must remain clean, safe, and well maintained. Regular maintenance protects the brand, improves safety, and preserves the long-term condition of the property.

---

## Purpose

Maintain a clean, attractive, and safe exterior environment that reflects the standards of the Ordina brand.

This policy applies to all street locations and freestanding restaurants.

---

# Responsibilities

Store Managers are responsible for monitoring exterior conditions and ensuring maintenance issues are addressed.

Responsibility for certain exterior areas may belong to either:

- The property owner.
- The landlord.
- The restaurant.

Managers must understand the lease agreement and know who is responsible for each area.

Even when the landlord is responsible, managers should communicate concerns that affect the restaurant's appearance.

---

# Landscaping

Landscaping includes all:

- Grass areas.
- Trees.
- Shrubs.
- Planters.
- Ground cover.

Landscaping must remain:

- Clean.
- Healthy.
- Well maintained.
- Free of weeds and debris.

Managers should monitor landscaping regularly and address issues promptly.

---

# Sidewalk Cleaning

Sidewalks and guest walkways must remain clean and safe.

Stores should remove:

- Dirt.
- Grease.
- Trash.
- Stains.
- Debris.

Pressure washing may be used when appropriate.

Only safe cleaning methods should be used to prevent damage to concrete surfaces.

Managers must comply with local environmental regulations regarding water runoff and wastewater disposal.

---

# Windows and Awnings

Windows must remain clean and free of:

- Dirt.
- Water spots.
- Grease.
- Dust.

Store teams may clean reachable glass surfaces.

Professional vendors should be used for elevated or specialized cleaning.

Awnings should be cleaned using approved cleaning methods that prevent damage to materials.

Damaged or faded awnings should be replaced as necessary to maintain brand appearance.

---

# Building Exterior

Exterior walls should be inspected regularly for:

- Fading.
- Cracking.
- Peeling paint.
- Weather damage.

Painting schedules should be planned based on building conditions.

Preventive maintenance helps reduce long-term repair costs.

---

# Parking Lots and Driveways

Parking lots and driveways must remain:

- Safe.
- Clean.
- Well marked.
- Free of hazards.

Managers should inspect for:

- Cracks.
- Potholes.
- Surface damage.
- Faded striping.
- Uneven pavement.

Damaged surfaces should be repaired promptly.

Surface treatments and line striping should be completed as needed to maintain appearance and safety.

---

# Safety Standards

Exterior areas must never create hazards for:

- Guests.
- Associates.
- Vendors.
- Delivery drivers.

Unsafe conditions should be reported and corrected immediately.

---

# Budget and Planning

Exterior maintenance should be included in annual operating and capital budgets.

Managers should plan for:

- Landscaping services.
- Painting.
- Awning replacement.
- Parking lot repairs.
- Surface maintenance.
- Exterior cleaning.

Preventive maintenance protects both the property and the brand.

---

# Property Standards

Every Ordina location should provide:

- Clean sidewalks.
- Attractive landscaping.
- Clear windows.
- Well-maintained buildings.
- Safe parking areas.
- Professional curb appeal.

The outside of the restaurant should reflect the quality guests expect inside.

---

# Prohibited Conditions

The following conditions are unacceptable:

- Overgrown landscaping.
- Dirty sidewalks.
- Stained windows.
- Damaged awnings.
- Peeling paint.
- Unsafe pavement.
- Potholes.
- Faded parking lines.
- Excessive trash or debris.
- Neglected exterior areas.

Failure to maintain exterior standards may result in operational review and corrective action.
