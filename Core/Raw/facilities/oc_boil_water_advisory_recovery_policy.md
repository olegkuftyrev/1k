# ORDINA

## Boil Water Advisory Recovery Policy

### Summary

A boil water advisory may compromise water, ice, beverages, and food equipment.

Operations may only return to normal after local authorities declare the water supply safe. All affected equipment must be flushed, sanitized, and verified before use.

Guest safety always takes priority over reopening speed.

---

# Purpose

Provide clear procedures for safely restarting restaurant operations after a boil water advisory has been lifted.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Scope

This procedure applies to all equipment that uses water, including:

- Beverage dispensers.
- Ice machines.
- Coffee brewers.
- Tea brewers.
- Hot water machines.
- Water filters.
- Filtration systems.
- Other water-connected equipment.

These procedures may only begin after local authorities confirm that the water supply is safe.

---

# Immediate Actions

### 1. Discard Ice

All ice produced before or during the boil water advisory must be discarded.

Ice may not be used, served, or saved.

---

### 2. Confirm Water Safety

Management must verify that the boil water advisory has officially ended before restarting operations.

---

# Water Line Flushing

Before equipment is used:

- Flush incoming water lines.
- Run cold water at hand sinks for at least 30 minutes.
- Run all other cold water faucets for at least 5 minutes.

This helps remove potentially contaminated water from the plumbing system.

---

# Water Filters

All water filters should be replaced after a boil water advisory.

This includes:

- Fountain filters.
- Ice machine filters.
- Coffee equipment filters.
- Water filtration cartridges.

Contaminated filters may affect water quality.

---

# Ice Machines

Before returning ice machines to service:

1. Run two complete ice-making cycles.
2. Discard all ice produced.
3. Clean and sanitize the ice bin.
4. Verify proper operation.

Only new ice produced after cleaning may be used.

---

# Beverage Dispensers

Before serving beverages:

1. Clean and sanitize all ice bins.
2. Flush each beverage valve for at least four minutes.
3. Test beverage quality.
4. Verify taste, appearance, and quality.

If a beverage has an unusual taste or odor:

- Flush the valve again.
- Retest the beverage.
- Contact service support if necessary.

No beverage may be served if quality is questionable.

---

# Coffee and Tea Equipment

Before returning equipment to service:

- Brew at least four full batches of hot water.
- Discard all water.
- Verify water quality before use.

Repeat the process if necessary.

---

# Equipment Service

If equipment does not operate correctly after startup:

- Remove it from service.
- Contact an authorized service provider.
- Do not use equipment until repairs are completed.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Verifying the advisory has ended.
- Supervising flushing procedures.
- Replacing water filters.
- Verifying beverage quality.
- Approving equipment for use.
- Documenting corrective actions.

---

# Reopening Requirements

Operations may resume only when:

- The water supply is approved for use.
- Water lines have been flushed.
- Filters have been replaced.
- Ice machines have been sanitized.
- Beverage systems have been tested.
- Equipment is functioning properly.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Using ice produced during the advisory.
- Serving beverages without flushing equipment.
- Reusing contaminated water filters.
- Operating equipment without verification.
- Reopening before water safety is confirmed.

Failure to follow these procedures may result in food safety violations and corrective action.

---

# Guiding Principle

If the safety of the water supply is uncertain, the product is not safe to serve.
