# ORDINA

## Water Interruption & Hot Water Policy

### Summary

Safe water is essential for food preparation, handwashing, cleaning, and sanitation.

If water or hot water requirements cannot be maintained, the restaurant may be required to stop operations until conditions are corrected. Management must immediately evaluate the situation and determine whether the restaurant can safely continue operating.

When in doubt, food safety comes first.

---

# Purpose

Establish operational standards for water service interruptions and hot water failures.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Water Availability

Potable water must be available throughout the restaurant at all times.

Water is required for:

- Handwashing.
- Dishwashing.
- Food preparation.
- Cleaning and sanitation.
- Restroom operation.

---

# No Water Available

If the restaurant loses water service:

- Stop food production immediately.
- Notify management.
- Contact repair services.
- Contact leadership immediately.
- Close the restaurant until water service is restored.

Operations may not continue without running water.

---

# Handwashing Water Requirements

All handwashing sinks must:

- Reach at least 100°F.
- Reach the required temperature within 60 seconds.

Handwashing sinks must remain fully operational during business hours.

---

# Three-Compartment Sink Requirements

The three-compartment sink must:

- Reach at least 100°F.
- Provide sufficient hot water for cleaning and sanitation.

If the sink cannot meet these requirements, corrective action is required immediately.

---

# Complete Hot Water Failure

If none of the hand sinks or the three-compartment sink reach 100°F:

- The restaurant must stop operations.
- Management must be notified immediately.
- Repairs must be initiated immediately.
- The restaurant may not reopen until hot water service is restored.

---

# Partial Hot Water Failure

If at least one sink reaches 100°F while others do not:

- Immediate repairs must be scheduled.
- Management should monitor conditions.
- Operations may continue only if food safety can be maintained.

---

# Water Between 100°F and 119°F

Water temperatures between 100°F and 119°F require corrective action.

Hot water systems should be restored to normal operating temperatures as soon as possible.

Management should monitor the situation and arrange repairs if necessary.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Verifying water temperatures.
- Monitoring sink performance.
- Contacting repair vendors.
- Protecting food safety.
- Communicating with leadership.
- Determining whether operations can safely continue.

---

# Emergency Response

When water or hot water problems occur:

1. Assess all sinks.
2. Verify temperatures.
3. Determine operational impact.
4. Contact maintenance.
5. Notify leadership.
6. Protect guests and associates.
7. Resume operations only when safe conditions are restored.

---

# Reopening Requirements

Operations may resume only when:

- Water service is restored.
- Hot water requirements are met.
- Hand sinks are operational.
- Dishwashing sinks are operational.
- Food safety standards can be maintained.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Operating without running water.
- Operating without handwashing capability.
- Ignoring hot water failures.
- Delaying repairs.
- Continuing operations when sanitation cannot be maintained.
- Allowing associates to work without proper handwashing facilities.

Failure to follow this policy may result in food safety violations, operational review, and corrective action.

---

# Guiding Principle

If safe water is unavailable, safe food cannot be produced.
