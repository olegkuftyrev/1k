# HR-9 Leadership Role: Area Coach of Operations

## Purpose

The Area Coach of Operations provides leadership, coaching, and operational support for multiple locations within an assigned area.

The position exists to develop leaders, strengthen operations, improve financial performance, and ensure consistent execution of company standards.

The Area Coach is the direct connection between restaurant leadership and regional leadership.

---

## Position Summary

The Area Coach oversees approximately 7–15 locations and is accountable for:

- People development.
- Talent acquisition.
- Operational excellence.
- Guest experience.
- Financial performance.
- Leadership coaching.
- Safety and compliance.
- Business growth.

---

## Leadership Expectations

Area Coaches are expected to:

- Demonstrate workability.
- Demonstrate intensity.
- Demonstrate integrity.
- Lead by example.
- Develop future leaders.
- Inspire store management teams.
- Raise operational standards.

---

## Culture Leadership

The Area Coach:

- Models company mission and values.
- Builds positive team environments.

- Promotes ownership.
- Encourages growth.

Culture is created through daily leadership behaviors.

## Talent Acquisition

Responsibilities include:

- Recruiting management candidates.

- Building leaders.
- Developing talent.
- Improving operations.
- Strengthening culture.

- Creating future leaders.

The effectiveness of an Area Coach is measured not by the number of stores managed, but by the number of successful leaders developed.
