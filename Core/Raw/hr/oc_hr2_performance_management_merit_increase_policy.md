# ORDINA

## HR-2 Performance Management & Merit Increase Policy

### Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to provide a consistent process for evaluating performance, supporting employee growth, and recognizing meaningful contributions through performance-based compensation.

Performance discussions are intended to improve performance, encourage development, and recognize associates who contribute positively to the organization.

---

# Review Frequency

Performance reviews should be conducted at least every six months.

Additional coaching discussions may occur at any time to support development, address performance concerns, or recognize achievements.

---

# Performance Areas

Associates are evaluated in two primary areas:

### 1. Values and Behaviors

Evaluation of how associates demonstrate company values through daily actions and behaviors.

Examples include:

- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Teamwork.
- Growth mindset.
- Guest focus.
- Ownership.
- Communication.

### 2. Job Performance

Evaluation of job responsibilities including:

- Quality.
- Productivity.
- Safety.
- Reliability.
- Operational execution.
- Policy compliance.
- Service standards.

---

# Performance Ratings

Performance is evaluated using a five-point scale:

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 | Exceptional Performance |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations |
| 3 | Meets Expectations |
| 2 | Needs Improvement |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory Performance |

Most associates are expected to perform at the "Meets Expectations" level.

Higher ratings should reflect consistently exceptional contributions.

---

# Supervisor Responsibilities

Supervisors should:

- Provide specific examples.
- Recognize strengths.
- Identify opportunities.
- Deliver constructive feedback.
- Create development plans.
- Support career growth.

Feedback should focus on behaviors and performance rather than personal characteristics.

---

# Associate Participation

Associates are encouraged to:

- Participate in review discussions.
- Share feedback.
- Discuss career interests.
- Ask questions.
- Provide written comments if desired.

Performance reviews are collaborative discussions intended to support growth.

---

# Development Planning

Each review should include development goals for the next review period.

Development plans may include:

- Skill development.
- Cross-training.
- Leadership opportunities.
- Technical training.
- Operational knowledge.
- Career advancement preparation.

Typically, two to three measurable goals should be established.

---

# Merit Increases

Merit increases may be awarded based upon:

- Overall performance rating.
- Position responsibilities.
- Internal pay equity.
- Market competitiveness.
- Budget considerations.
- Pay range placement.

Higher performance levels may receive larger compensation increases.

---

# Increase Eligibility

Merit increases may be denied or delayed if:

- Performance expectations are not met.
- Corrective action is active.
- Required review periods have not elapsed.
- Compensation limits have been reached.
- Budget restrictions apply.

---

# Review Documentation

Performance reviews should include:

- Performance ratings.
- Supervisor comments.
- Associate comments.
- Development goals.
- Signatures.
- Review dates.

If an associate declines to sign, management may document "Employee declined to sign."

---

# Record Retention

Performance reviews should be maintained according to company record retention requirements.

Managers may retain working copies to support ongoing coaching discussions.

---

# Guiding Principles

Performance reviews should:

- Recognize strengths.
- Encourage growth.
- Support development.
- Improve performance.
- Reward contribution.
- Build future leaders.

The most valuable part of any review is the quality of the conversation.
