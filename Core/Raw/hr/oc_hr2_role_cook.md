# HR-2 Leadership Role: Cook

## Purpose

The Cook position exists to prepare all menu items according to company recipes and standards while supporting kitchen operations, developing Kitchen Help associates, and maintaining exceptional food quality.

The Cook serves as the technical expert of the kitchen and acts as the foundation for future kitchen leadership.

---

## Position Summary

The Cook is responsible for:

- Preparing all recipes.
- Maintaining food quality.
- Supporting kitchen operations.
- Training Kitchen Help associates.
- Following food safety standards.
- Assisting kitchen leadership.
- Supporting guest satisfaction.

---

## Leadership Expectations

Cooks are expected to demonstrate:

- Workability.
- Intensity.
- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Ownership.
- Consistency.

Leadership begins through actions, not position.

---

## Culture Responsibilities

The Cook:

- Lives company values.
- Supports teammates.
- Builds positive relationships.
- Creates a safe work environment.
- Encourages learning.
- Promotes accountability.

Strong kitchens are built through strong culture.

---

## Training Responsibilities

The Cook assists with:

- Training Kitchen Help associates.
- Teaching recipes.
- Demonstrating procedures.
- Coaching kitchen standards.
- Reinforcing food safety.
- Supporting development.

Every Cook helps develop the next Cook.

---

## Kitchen Operations

The Cook:

- Prepares recipes according to standards.
- Operates all cooking equipment.
- Supports production flow.
- Guides Kitchen Help associates.
- Assists Front of House when needed.
- Maintains kitchen organization.

Execution creates consistency.

---

## Food Quality Standards

The Cook ensures:

- Correct recipes.
- Proper portions.
- Product freshness.
- Correct cooking techniques.
- Visual presentation.
- Flavor consistency.

Guests return because food remains consistent.

---

## Food Safety Responsibilities

The Cook follows and supports:

- Food safety standards.
- Sanitation procedures.
- Temperature requirements.
- Equipment safety.
- Chemical safety.
- Daily safety checklists.

Safety protects associates and guests.

---

## Operations Excellence

The Cook:

- Maintains clean workstations.
- Keeps kitchen organized.
- Supports shift execution.
- Assists leadership.
- Maintains preparation standards.
- Promotes operational discipline.

Operational excellence starts in the kitchen.

---

## Guest Experience

The Cook contributes through:

- Product quality.
- Speed of production.
- Food consistency.
- Product availability.
- Freshness.
- Presentation.

Guests experience the kitchen through every plate served.

---

## Brand Standards

The Cook:

- Follows recipes.
- Supports new products.
- Maintains standards.
- Protects food quality.
- Supports promotions.
- Represents the brand.

Consistency builds trust.

---

## Administrative Support

The Cook may assist with:

- Ordering.
- Inventory.
- Product preparation.
- Equipment maintenance.
- Kitchen organization.

Operational understanding prepares future leaders.

---

## Required Qualifications

Recommended qualifications include:

- Some high school education or equivalent.
- One year of operations experience.
- Food handler certification where required.
- Flexible scheduling availability.

## Knowledge and Skills

Cooks should demonstrate:

- Recipe knowledge.
- Cooking skills.
- Food safety knowledge.
- Equipment operation.
- Communication skills.
- Teamwork.
- Kitchen organization.
- Problem solving.

Core competencies include:

- Self-knowledge.
- Customer focus.

## Success Measures

Performance may be measured through:

- Food quality.

## Career Progression

Potential advancement opportunities include:

- Chef.

---

## Work Environment

Cooks frequently:

- Work in hot kitchens.
- Operate cooking equipment.
- Lift products and supplies.
- Stand for extended periods.
- Work flexible schedules.
- Perform repetitive tasks.

---

## Leadership Philosophy

A Cook succeeds by:

- Mastering recipes.
- Supporting the team.
- Protecting food quality.
- Teaching others.
- Maintaining standards.
- Preparing for leadership.

The Cook is more than someone who prepares food. The Cook is the future kitchen leader who creates consistency, develops others, and protects the guest experience.
