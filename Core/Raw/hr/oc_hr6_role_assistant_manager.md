# HR-6 Leadership Role: Assistant Manager

## Purpose

The Assistant Manager supports the General Manager in leading the restaurant's daily operations while developing associates, maintaining operational standards, and delivering business results.

The Assistant Manager serves as the operational leader of the restaurant and assumes full responsibility during the absence of the General Manager.

---

## Position Summary

The Assistant Manager is accountable for:

- Daily restaurant operations.
- Associate development.
- Guest experience.
- Food quality.
- Safety and compliance.
- Financial performance.
- Operational standards.
- Leadership development.

---

## Leadership Expectations

Assistant Managers are expected to:

- Demonstrate workability.
- Demonstrate intensity.
- Demonstrate integrity.
- Lead by example.
- Build trust.
- Develop people.
- Maintain standards.

Leadership begins with daily behavior.

---

## Culture Leadership

The Assistant Manager:

- Lives company values.
- Creates accountability.
- Supports teamwork.
- Builds positive culture.
- Promotes growth.
- Encourages ownership.

Culture is reinforced through daily coaching.

---

## Recruitment

Responsibilities include:

- Assisting with hiring.
- Supporting interviews.
- Evaluating candidates.
- Maintaining staffing levels.
- Developing internal talent.
- Identifying future leaders.

Strong teams are built continuously.

---

## Associate Development

The Assistant Manager:

- Coaches associates.
- Trains new hires.
- Develops shift leaders.
- Builds future managers.
- Provides feedback.
- Creates development opportunities.

Leadership success is measured through people development.

---

## Training Responsibilities

The Assistant Manager trains associates on:

- Front of House operations.
- Back of House operations.
- Food safety.
- Work safety.
- Company policies.
- Operational procedures.

Training happens every shift.

---

## Operations Leadership

The Assistant Manager:

- Oversees daily operations.
- Assigns responsibilities.
- Maintains operational standards.
- Controls food waste.
- Manages shift execution.
- Supports labor deployment.

In the absence of the General Manager, the Assistant Manager assumes full operational responsibility.

---

## Safety and Associate Relations

Responsibilities include:

- Promoting workplace safety.
- Supporting food safety.
- Addressing associate concerns.
- Escalating issues appropriately.
- Supporting performance management.
- Encouraging open communication.

A safe workplace is a leadership responsibility.

---

## Guest Experience

Assistant Managers are responsible for:

- Building emotional connections with guests.
- Delivering exceptional service.
- Ensuring food quality.
- Maintaining restaurant cleanliness.
- Resolving guest concerns.
- Supporting hospitality culture.

Food, service, and ambiance create guest loyalty.

---

## Brand Representation

Responsibilities include:

- Supporting marketing initiatives.
- Executing promotions.
- Implementing new products.
- Maintaining recipe compliance.
- Supporting community involvement.

---

## Financial Leadership

Assistant Managers share responsibility for:

- Sales performance.
- Labor management.
- Food cost management.
- Cash handling.
- Waste reduction.
- Profitability.

Every operational decision impacts financial performance.

---

## Administrative Responsibilities

The Assistant Manager assists with:

- Inventory counts.
- Financial reports.
- Cash management.
- Safe procedures.
- Register procedures.
- Store administration.

---

## Required Qualifications

Recommended qualifications include:

- High school diploma or equivalent.
- One to two years of operations experience.
- Food safety certification.
- Schedule flexibility.

---

## Knowledge and Skills

Assistant Managers should demonstrate:

- Leadership.
- Communication.
- Financial understanding.
- Time management.
- Coaching ability.
- Operational knowledge.
- Problem solving.
- Team development.

---

## Leadership Competencies

Core competencies include:

- Developing direct reports and others.
- Listening.
- Motivating others.
- Caring about others.
- Hiring and staffing.

---

## Success Measures

Performance may be measured through:

- Sales growth.
- Profitability.
- Guest satisfaction.
- Associate retention.
- Operational standards.
- Food safety.
- Team development.
- Leadership effectiveness.

---

## Career Progression

Potential advancement opportunities include:

- General Manager in Training.
- General Manager.
- Training Leader.

Advancement depends on leadership performance and business needs.

---

## Work Environment

Assistant Managers frequently:

- Stand for extended periods.
- Work in hot kitchen environments.
- Lift and carry products.
- Perform repetitive tasks.
- Operate restaurant equipment.
- Work flexible schedules.

---

## Leadership Philosophy

The Assistant Manager succeeds by:

- Leading shifts.
- Developing people.
- Protecting standards.
- Supporting the General Manager.
- Delivering results.
- Preparing for future leadership.

The Assistant Manager role exists to develop future General Managers while ensuring daily operational excellence.
