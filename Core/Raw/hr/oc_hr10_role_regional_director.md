# ORDINA

## HR-10 Executive Leadership Role: Regional Director

### Purpose

The Regional Director is responsible for leading multiple operating locations through subordinate leaders while building organizational culture, developing talent, delivering financial results, and supporting long-term business growth.

The Regional Director serves as the bridge between field operations and executive leadership.

---

# Position Summary

The Regional Director oversees multiple operational locations through Area Managers or equivalent field leadership positions.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Building organizational culture.
- Developing leaders.
- Driving operational excellence.
- Achieving financial results.
- Improving guest experience.
- Supporting business growth.
- Preparing future leaders.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

## Culture Leadership

The Regional Director:

- Models company values.
- Reinforces organizational culture.
- Creates accountability.
- Builds alignment.
- Develops trust.

Leaders are expected to create environments where people can grow, contribute, and succeed.

---

## Talent Acquisition

Responsibilities include:

- Building leadership pipelines.
- Recruiting internal talent.
- Recruiting external talent.
- Assessing leadership potential.
- Supporting succession planning.

---

## Leadership Development

Regional leaders are responsible for:

- Identifying high-potential talent.
- Creating development plans.
- Coaching operational leaders.
- Conducting talent reviews.
- Preparing future leaders.

Leadership development is considered a core responsibility.

---

# Operational Excellence

The Regional Director ensures:

- Operational standards are maintained.
- Food safety standards are executed.
- Training programs are implemented.
- Policies and procedures are followed.
- Operational consistency exists throughout the region.

---

# Guest Experience

Regional leaders are responsible for:

- Improving guest satisfaction.
- Supporting service recovery.
- Maintaining brand standards.
- Coaching guest-focused leadership.
- Building community relationships.

---

# Financial Accountability

Regional leadership is accountable for:

- Sales growth.
- Profitability.
- Labor management.
- Cost controls.
- Productivity.
- Financial planning.
- Business performance.

---

# Strategic Planning

Responsibilities include:

- Identifying growth opportunities.
- Improving underperforming locations.
- Supporting market expansion.
- Developing action plans.
- Monitoring execution.
