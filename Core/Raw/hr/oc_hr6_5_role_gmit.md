# HR-6.5 Leadership Role: General Manager in Training (GMIT)

## Purpose

The General Manager in Training (GMIT) is responsible for developing the skills, leadership behaviors, and business knowledge necessary to successfully operate a restaurant independently.

The GMIT serves as the final stage of development before promotion to General Manager.

---

## Position Summary

The GMIT is accountable for:

- Leading store operations.
- Developing associates.
- Supporting staffing efforts.
- Driving financial performance.
- Maintaining operational standards.
- Delivering guest experience.
- Learning full business ownership.
- Preparing for General Manager responsibilities.

The GMIT transitions from supporting the business to owning the business.

---

## Leadership Expectations

GMITs are expected to demonstrate:

- Workability.
- Intensity.
- Integrity.
- Ownership.
- Accountability.
- Leadership.
- Initiative.
- Decision making.

Future General Managers must lead before receiving the title.

---

## Culture Leadership

The GMIT:

- Lives company values.
- Builds team culture.
- Creates accountability.
- Develops associates.
- Promotes growth.
- Leads through example.

Culture becomes a leadership responsibility.

---

## Store Leadership

The GMIT:

- Manages daily operations.
- Leads shifts independently.
- Allocates labor.
- Maintains operational standards.
- Oversees execution.
- Supports all departments.

The restaurant should operate successfully under GMIT leadership.

---

## People Responsibilities

The GMIT:

- Recruits associates.
- Develops hourly associates.
- Builds Shift Leaders and Cooks.
- Holds associates accountable.
- Conducts coaching conversations.
- Supports performance management.

People development is the foundation of leadership.

---

## Training Responsibilities

The GMIT:

- Trains associates on company standards.
- Develops future leaders.
- Conducts coaching sessions.
- Reinforces operational expectations.
- Supports personal development.

Leadership is measured by the development of others.

---

## Guest Experience

The GMIT:

- Maintains food quality.
- Maintains service standards.
- Resolves guest concerns.
- Builds emotional connections.
- Supports guest satisfaction.

Guest experience remains the top priority.

---

## Brand Leadership

The GMIT:

- Executes marketing initiatives.
- Supports community outreach.
- Maintains company standards.
- Represents the brand.

The restaurant reflects its leadership.

---

## Financial Responsibilities

The GMIT is expected to:

- Build sales.
- Improve check average.
- Drive catering growth.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Control labor.
- Manage food cost.
- Achieve profitability goals.

Business ownership begins before promotion.

---

## Operational Standards

The GMIT:

- Passes food safety audits.
- Maintains operational standards.
- Supports store visit expectations.
- Drives execution.
- Ensures compliance.

Standards create consistency.

---

## Required Qualifications

Recommended qualifications include:

- High school diploma or equivalent.
- Prior management experience.
- Completion of required management training modules.
- Food safety certification.
- Flexible scheduling availability.

---

## Knowledge and Skills

GMITs should demonstrate:

- Operational expertise.
- Financial understanding.
- Leadership ability.
- Team development.
- Coaching skills.
- Communication.
- Problem solving.
- Business understanding.

---

## Leadership Competencies

Core competencies include:

- Developing direct reports and others.
- Listening.
- Motivating and inspiring others.
- Caring about direct reports and others.
- Managing and measuring work.
- Decision quality.
- Priority setting.
- Delegation.
- Self-knowledge.
- Customer focus.
- Drive for results.
- Managing vision and purpose.
- Career ambition.
- Integrity and trust.
- Hiring and staffing.

---

## Success Measures

Performance may be measured through:

- Sales growth.
- Profitability.
- Staffing.
- Food safety.
- Guest satisfaction.
- Operational standards.
- Associate development.
- Leadership readiness.

---

## Career Progression

Potential advancement opportunities include:

- General Manager.

Promotion occurs when the candidate consistently demonstrates ownership of the business.

---

## Leadership Philosophy

A GMIT succeeds by:

- Thinking like a General Manager.
- Making decisions.
- Developing people.
- Driving results.
- Protecting standards.
- Taking ownership.

The GMIT is not preparing to become a leader.

The GMIT is already leading while preparing to assume full ownership of the business.
