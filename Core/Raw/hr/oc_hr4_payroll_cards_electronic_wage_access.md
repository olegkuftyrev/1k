# ORDINA

## HR-4 Payroll Cards & Electronic Wage Access

### Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for payroll cards and electronic wage payment methods.

Payroll cards provide associates with secure and convenient access to earned wages without requiring a traditional bank account.

---

# Eligibility

Payroll cards may be offered to:

- New hires.
- Associates without bank accounts.
- Associates who choose not to use direct deposit.
- Associates requesting electronic wage access.

Participation is voluntary unless otherwise required by applicable law.

---

# Payroll Card Enrollment

Associates enrolling in a payroll card program may be required to provide:

- Full legal name.
- Residential address.
- Date of birth.
- Government identification.
- Social Security number or legally required identification.

Personal information is used solely to establish the account.

---

# Payroll Card Activation

Associates must activate their payroll card according to the card provider's instructions.

Activation may require:

- Card verification.
- Personal identification number (PIN).
- Identity verification.

---

# Access to Wages

Associates may access wages through:

- ATM withdrawals.
- Point-of-sale purchases.
- Cash-back transactions.
- Mobile applications.
- Online account access.
- Bank transfers where available.

Fees may apply depending on the provider and transaction type.

---

# Direct Deposit Information

Payroll card accounts may provide:

- Routing numbers.
- Account numbers.
- Electronic transfer capabilities.

Associates should verify account information before making changes to payroll elections.

---

# Lost or Stolen Cards

Associates should report lost or stolen cards immediately.

The company may assist associates in obtaining replacement cards according to the provider's policies.

Replacement fees may apply depending on the provider.

---

# Mobile Wallets

Where supported, payroll cards may be used with:

- Mobile wallets.
- Digital payment platforms.
- Contactless payment systems.

Availability depends on the card provider.

---

# Payroll Questions

Questions regarding:

- Card balances.
- Transactions.
- Transfers.
- PIN resets.
- Card replacement.
- Account access.

Should be directed to the payroll card provider or Human Resources.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Providing enrollment information.
- Assisting associates with general questions.
- Protecting associate privacy.
- Referring account issues to the appropriate provider.
- Maintaining payroll confidentiality.

Managers should never:

- Access employee accounts.
- Request PIN numbers.
- Handle personal account information.

---

# Employee Rights

Associates retain the right to select available wage payment options as permitted by applicable law.

No associate shall be required to disclose account credentials or personal financial information.

---

# Guiding Principle

Pay should be:

- Accurate.
- Secure.
- Accessible.
- Confidential.

Every associate deserves reliable access to their earned wages.
