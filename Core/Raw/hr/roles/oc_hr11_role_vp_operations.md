# ORDINA

## HR-11 Executive Leadership Role: Vice President of Operations

### Purpose

The Vice President of Operations provides strategic leadership across multiple operating regions while developing senior leaders, driving business growth, and ensuring operational excellence.

This position is responsible for aligning regional operations with organizational strategy and long-term objectives.

---

# Position Summary

The Vice President of Operations oversees multiple regions through Regional Directors and field leadership teams.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Strategic leadership.
- Executive coaching.
- Culture development.
- Business growth.
- Financial performance.
- Leadership development.
- Organizational alignment.
- Brand stewardship.

---
