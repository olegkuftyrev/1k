# ORDINA

## HR-12 Executive Leadership Role: Zone Vice President

### Purpose

The Zone Vice President provides executive leadership for multiple regions while establishing organizational direction, developing senior leaders, expanding markets, and ensuring long-term business success.

The Zone Vice President serves as the senior field executive responsible for culture, growth, leadership development, and strategic execution.

---

# Position Summary

The Zone Vice President oversees multiple regions through Vice Presidents, Regional Directors, and field leadership teams.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Strategic leadership.
- Executive development.
- Market growth.
- Organizational culture.
- Financial performance.
- Leadership succession.
- Brand development.
- Long-term planning.

---

# Executive Leadership Expectations

Zone Vice Presidents are expected to:

- Demonstrate workability.
- Lead with intensity.
- Operate with integrity.
- Think strategically.
- Develop leaders.
- Build organizational capability.
- Create sustainable growth.
