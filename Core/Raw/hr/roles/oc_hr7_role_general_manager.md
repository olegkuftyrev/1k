# HR-7 Leadership Role: General Manager

## Purpose

The General Manager is responsible for the successful operation of a single restaurant through people development, operational excellence, financial performance, and guest experience.

The General Manager builds the culture, develops future leaders, and delivers business results while bringing the company mission to life.

---

## Position Summary

The General Manager is accountable for:

- Store operations.
- People development.
- Staffing.
- Guest experience.
- Financial performance.
- Food quality.
- Safety and security.
- Community engagement.
- Leadership development.

The General Manager owns the entire business.

---

## Leadership Expectations

General Managers are expected to demonstrate:

- Workability.
- Intensity.
- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Ownership.
- Leadership.
- Courage.
- Consistency.

The General Manager sets the standard for everyone in the restaurant.

---

## Culture Leadership

The General Manager:

- Lives the company mission.
- Builds culture daily.
- Creates accountability.
- Develops trust.
- Encourages growth.
- Promotes teamwork.
- Models company values.

Culture is the responsibility of the General Manager.

---

## People Management

The General Manager:

- Manages daily operations.
- Schedules labor.
- Assigns responsibilities.
- Holds associates accountable.
- Builds strong teams.
- Maintains staffing levels.
- Develops future leaders.

Great restaurants are built by great people.

---

## Recruiting and Staffing

The General Manager:

- Recruits talent internally and externally.
- Conducts interviews.
- Makes hiring decisions.
- Builds succession plans.
- Retains top performers.
- Maintains staffing requirements.

Talent is the General Manager's most important asset.

---

## People Development

The General Manager:

- Conducts coaching sessions.
- Creates development plans.
- Identifies high-potential associates.
- Provides feedback.
- Supports career growth.
- Develops successors.

Every successful leader develops another leader.

---

## Training Responsibilities

The General Manager ensures:

- Operations standards are followed.
- Associates receive proper training.
- Policies are understood.
- Skill development occurs.
- Leadership development continues.

Training never stops.

---

## Associate Relations and Safety

The General Manager:

- Maintains a safe workplace.
- Addresses associate concerns.
- Partners with Human Resources (HR).
- Supports food safety.
- Supports workplace safety.
- Promotes accountability.

People perform best when they feel safe.

---

## Guest Experience

The General Manager:

- Creates exceptional guest experiences.
- Maintains food quality.
- Maintains service standards.
- Maintains restaurant cleanliness.
- Resolves guest concerns.
- Builds emotional connections.

Guests return because of the experience.

---

## Brand Leadership

The General Manager:

- Executes company initiatives.
- Supports new products.
- Participates in community events.
- Builds local relationships.
- Promotes the brand.

The restaurant represents the company in the community.

---

## Financial Leadership

The General Manager is accountable for:

- Sales growth.
- Profitability.
- Labor management.
- Food cost management.
- Cash accountability.
- Inventory accuracy.
- Expense control.

The General Manager owns the business results.

---

## Planning and Execution

The General Manager:

- Identifies opportunities.
- Creates action plans.
- Follows up on execution.
- Solves operational issues.
- Improves performance.

Execution separates average stores from great stores.

---

## Required Qualifications

Recommended qualifications include:

- High school diploma or equivalent.
- Operations experience.
- Management experience.
- Food safety certification.
- Flexible scheduling availability.

---

## Knowledge and Skills

The General Manager demonstrates:

- Operational expertise.
- Financial understanding.
- Leadership ability.
- Time management.
- Communication skills.
- Team development.
- Coaching ability.
- Problem solving.

---

## Leadership Competencies

Core competencies include:

- Developing direct reports and others.
- Listening.
- Motivating and inspiring others.
- Caring about direct reports and others.
- Hiring and staffing.

Leadership is measured by the growth of others.

---

## Success Measures

Performance may be measured through:

- Sales growth.
- Profitability.
- Staffing.
- Turnover.
- Guest satisfaction.
- Safety performance.
- Associate development.
- Operational standards.
- Culture.

---

## Career Progression

Potential advancement opportunities include:

- Training Leader.
- Area Coach of Operations.
- Support center positions.

Promotion occurs through developing people and delivering results.

---

## Work Environment

General Managers frequently:

- Stand for extended periods.
- Work in hot kitchens.
- Lift supplies.
- Work weekends and holidays.
- Perform Front of House (FOH) and Back of House (BOH) duties.
- Operate restaurant equipment.

Leadership requires visibility.

---

## Leadership Philosophy

A General Manager succeeds by:

- Building people.
- Developing culture.
- Driving results.
- Protecting standards.
- Coaching continuously.
- Serving guests.
- Leading by example.

The General Manager is not responsible for doing all the work.

The General Manager is responsible for building the team that delivers the work.
