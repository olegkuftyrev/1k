# ORDINA

## OPS-LEAD-3 Training Leader Role & Leadership Standards

### Purpose

The Training Leader is responsible for operating a successful location while developing future leaders for the organization.

This position serves as both an operational leader and a talent developer, ensuring the organization maintains a strong pipeline of future managers.

---

# Position Summary

The Training Leader is accountable for:

- Operational excellence.
- Leadership development.
- Management training.
- Team development.
- Guest experience.
- Financial performance.
- Talent export.
- Community engagement.

The Training Leader leads the business while simultaneously developing future leaders.

---

# Core Leadership Expectations

Training Leaders are expected to demonstrate:

- Workability.
- Intensity.
- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Coaching.
- Leadership by example.
- Continuous improvement.

---

# People Leadership

## Culture

Training Leaders:

- Model organizational values.
- Reinforce culture daily.
- Create accountability.
- Build trust.
- Develop future leaders.

---

## Management Development

Training Leaders are responsible for:

- Training future managers.
- Developing assistant managers.
- Coaching store leaders.
- Teaching operational systems.
- Building leadership capability.

Training includes both operational skills and leadership behaviors.

---

## Recruitment

Responsibilities include:

- Hiring quality associates.
- Building talent pipelines.
- Identifying future leaders.
- Supporting succession planning.
- Retaining high performers.

---

## Associate Development

Training Leaders should:

- Develop individual growth plans.
- Coach performance.
- Identify strengths.
- Provide feedback.
- Prepare associates for advancement.

Leadership development is considered a primary responsibility.

---

# Training Responsibilities

Training Leaders ensure associates understand:

- Company policies.
- Operational standards.
- Safety procedures.
- Food safety practices.
- Customer service standards.
- Leadership expectations.

Training includes both technical and personal development.

---

# Guest Experience

The Training Leader is responsible for:

- Food quality.
- Service standards.
- Cleanliness.
- Safety.
- Guest recovery.
- Store atmosphere.

Every guest interaction reflects the quality of leadership.

---

# Brand Leadership

Responsibilities include:

- Executing marketing initiatives.
- Supporting product launches.
- Building community relationships.
- Representing the organization locally.
- Increasing brand awareness.

---

# Financial Accountability

Training Leaders are accountable for:

- Sales performance.
- Profitability.
- Labor management.
- Food costs.
- Asset protection.
- Store expenses.

Financial performance must be balanced with people development and guest experience.

---

# Operational Planning

Training Leaders should:

- Identify opportunities.
- Solve operational problems.
- Create action plans.
- Monitor execution.
- Improve store performance.

---

# Required Qualifications

Recommended qualifications include:

- High school diploma or equivalent.
- Successful General Manager experience.
- Food safety certification.
- Leadership experience.
- Operational expertise.

---

# Knowledge and Skills

Training Leaders should possess:

- Strong operational knowledge.
- Leadership skills.
- Financial understanding.
- Communication abilities.
- Coaching skills.
- Time management.
- Conflict resolution.
- Team building.

---

# Leadership Competencies

Core competencies include:

- Delegation.
- Accountability.
- Performance management.
- Coaching.
- Decision making.
- Work management.
- Leadership presence.

---

# Success Measures

Performance may be evaluated through:

- Store profitability.
- Guest satisfaction.
- Food safety.
- Associate development.
- Training success.
- Turnover management.
- Talent promotion.
- Operational standards.

---

# Career Progression

Potential advancement opportunities include:

- Area Manager.
- Regional leadership.
- Training positions.
- Support center roles.
- Executive leadership.

Advancement is based on business needs and demonstrated leadership performance.

---

# Work Environment

Training Leaders frequently:

- Stand for extended periods.
- Work in hot kitchen environments.
- Operate restaurant equipment.
- Lift and move supplies.
- Perform repetitive tasks.
- Work evenings and weekends.
- Support both front and back of house operations.

---

# Leadership Philosophy

A Training Leader succeeds when:

- The store performs.
- People grow.
- Future leaders emerge.
- Standards improve.
- Talent is exported.

The ultimate measure of a Training Leader is not how many people they manage, but how many leaders they develop.
