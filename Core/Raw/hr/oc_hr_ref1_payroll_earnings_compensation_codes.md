# ORDINA

## HR-REF-1 Payroll Earnings & Compensation Codes

### Purpose

This policy establishes a standardized reference for payroll earnings codes used throughout the organization.

The purpose of this guide is to:

- Improve payroll transparency.
- Help associates understand their pay statements.
- Support managers in answering payroll questions.
- Ensure accurate interpretation of earnings and premiums.
- Reduce payroll disputes and misunderstandings.

---

# Scope

This reference applies to:

- Hourly associates.
- Shift leaders.
- Managers.
- Support personnel.
- Leadership positions.

---

| DOUBLETIME PAY | Doubletime premium pay |
| ADMIN PAY | Administrative pay |

# Sick Pay

State and local regulations may require paid sick leave.

Examples include:

- Washington Sick Pay
- Seattle Sick Pay
- California Sick Pay
- Oregon Sick Pay
- New York Sick Pay
- Illinois Paid Leave
- Arizona Sick Pay
- Colorado Sick Pay

Sick pay eligibility and accrual are governed by applicable laws and company policy.

---

# Premium Pay

Premium pay may include:

- Meal break premium.
- Rest break premium.
- Predictability pay.
- Clopening premium.
- Spread of hours premium.
- Reporting time pay.
- Split shift premium.

Premium payments are governed by state and local labor laws.

---

# Reimbursements

Examples include:

- Phone reimbursement.
- Internet reimbursement.
- Mileage reimbursement.
- Per diem allowance.
- Relocation reimbursement.

Approved reimbursements are generally paid separately from wages.

---

# Tips

Where applicable, payroll may reflect:

- Tips paid out.
- Non-cash tips.
- Allocated tips.

Tip reporting must comply with applicable laws.

---

# Payroll Questions

Associates should review their earnings statements regularly.

Questions regarding:

- Missing hours.
- Incorrect premiums.
- Bonus payments.
- Overtime.
- Leave balances.
- Reimbursements.

Should be brought to management or Human Resources promptly.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Understanding compensation codes.
- Assisting associates with payroll questions.
- Reporting payroll concerns promptly.
- Maintaining accurate timekeeping practices.
- Ensuring proper documentation.

---

# Guiding Principle

Understand your pay.

Ask questions.

Verify accuracy.

Transparency builds trust.
