# Seattle Secure Scheduling — Legion Schedule Change Guide

*Revised: March 14, 2025 | Seattle-specific compliance document*

---

## Overview

This guide covers how to categorize schedule changes in Legion for Seattle locations. Each change type determines whether predictability pay (a premium) is owed to the associate.

Written documentation — obtained via QR code or WorkJam — is required wherever noted.

---

## Employee Initiated

### Associate Accepts Hours via Access to Hours (Written Communication)

**Criteria:**
- Associate accepts hours or shifts from the Notice of Available Shifts form.
- Obtain consent through QR code or WorkJam for written documentation.

**Example:** Marian accepted additional hours from the Notice of Available Shifts form and submitted written consent.

---

### Associate Called Off Work

**Criteria:**
- Associate notified the manager in writing that they would not be able to attend their scheduled shift.

**Example:** Joseph was scheduled for 9/1. He texted manager Jake on 8/31 to say he would not be able to attend due to feeling unwell.

---

### Associate is a No Call / No Show

**Criteria:**
- Associate fails to report to work without any prior notice or explanation.

**Example:** Tom was scheduled 10:30 AM – 4:00 PM, failed to report, and gave no prior notice.

---

### Associate Requested a Schedule Change (Written Communication)

**Criteria:**
- Associate requested a schedule change for personal reasons.
- Obtain consent through QR code or WorkJam for written documentation.

**Example:** Alyssa asked to adjust her schedule due to school. Manager Tim accommodated the request and Alyssa submitted written consent.

---

### Associate Requested Shift Swap (Without Management Involvement, Written Communication)

**Criteria:**
- Two associates mutually agree to a shift swap or coverage without manager intervention.
- Both associates must submit consent via QR code or WorkJam.
- Comments are mandatory — specify which associates traded or covered.

**Example:** Lisa and John agreed to swap their Saturday and Sunday shifts. Manager Henry updated the schedule.
*Comment: "Approved request for Lisa and John to swap Saturday and Sunday shifts."*

---

### Associate Volunteers for Shift via Mass Communication

**Criteria:**
- Use only when additional hours result from another associate being unable to work their scheduled hours.
- Manager must send a mass communication explicitly labeled **"Mass Communication"** to all qualified associates.
- The message must state: acceptance is voluntary, associates may decline, and associates who accept **will not receive predictability pay** for the schedule change.
- Obtain consent via QR code or WorkJam.

**Example:** Cook Jeff could not make his 10 AM – 4 PM shift. Manager William sent a group text labeled "Mass Communication" to all Cooks. Associate Adam accepted and submitted written consent.

---

### Associate Volunteers to Stay — In-Person Group Communication (Unanticipated Guest Needs Only)

**Criteria:**
- Use only for unanticipated customer/guest needs.
- Must be an in-person group communication with two or more qualified associates currently on duty.
- Communicate that: acceptance is voluntary, associates may decline, and associates who accept **will not receive predictability pay**.
- Obtain consent via QR code or WorkJam.

**Example:** Manager Bryan asked 2 Counter Helps on duty if anyone wanted to extend their shift due to increased guest traffic. Marian accepted and submitted written consent.

---

### Associate Was Tardy

**Criteria:**
- Associate arrives later than their scheduled start time for personal reasons.

**Example:** Lisa was scheduled at 10:30 AM and clocked in at 11:00 AM due to traffic.

---

### Other — Add Comments

**Criteria:**
- Use only when the reason for the schedule change is not listed in the dropdown.
- Comments are mandatory. Do not include personal or protected information.

**Example:** Robin left early due to a medical situation.
*Comment: "Associate left early due to personal reasons."*

---

## Exceptions

### New Hire Schedule

**Criteria:**
- Use only for a new hire on their first day, for their first schedule.
- Schedule changes on Day 1 still trigger a premium.
- Provide a schedule running through the last date of the currently posted 3-week schedule on or before Day 1 of onboarding.
- A premium is owed for all subsequent shifts if the schedule is provided after Day 1.
- Comments are mandatory — specify the onboarding date.

**Example:** Abby is hired and onboarded on 9/1. Schedule must be provided to her on 9/1.
*Comment: "Onboarding date: 9/1."*

---

### No Schedule Change — Change in Work Location

**Criteria:**
- Use only when the location changes but the originally scheduled times remain the same.
- Obtain consent via QR code or WorkJam.

**Example:** Daniel's schedule at store 2545 was 9/1, 10 AM – 4 PM. He consented to support store 3741 with the same schedule.

---

### Returning from Leave of Absence (LOA)

**Criteria:**
- Use only for an associate returning from an approved LOA.
- Provide a schedule running through the last date of the currently posted 3-week schedule on or before the effective return date.
- A premium is owed if the schedule is provided after the return date or for changes beyond the initial schedule.
- Comments are mandatory — specify the effective return date.

**Example:** John's effective LOA return date is 9/1. His first day back is 9/3. Schedule must be provided on 9/1.
*Comment: "Effective return date: 9/1."*

---

### Schedule Change Due to Associate Termination

**Criteria:**
- Use only for schedule reduction or removal resulting from termination.

**Example:** Manager Linda removed Bob's schedule following his termination.

---

### Statutory Meal Breaks

**Criteria:**
- Use only for legally mandated meal break periods during a shift.

**Example:** Jenny's schedule was 10 AM – 4 PM. Her time clock shows 10 AM – 4:05 PM. Legion reflects a 30-minute schedule reduction for her meal break.

---

### Store Closure Due to Emergency Conditions

**Criteria:**
- Threats to associates or property, or public official recommendation that work cannot continue.
- Failure of public utilities (electricity, water, gas, or sewer).
- Natural disaster or other cause outside the employer's control per applicable regulations.

**Example:** All associates were sent home due to a power outage affecting the entire block.

---

### Subtraction of Hours Due to Documented Disciplinary Action

**Criteria:**
- Use only for a schedule reduction resulting from a written, documented disciplinary action.

**Example:** Paige's schedule was 4 PM – 10 PM. Manager Eric issued Paige a written warning for a uniform violation and sent her home at 4:30 PM.

---

## Manager Initiated

**Criteria:**
- **A predictability pay premium applies.**
- Schedule changes initiated by the manager or required for business needs.

**Example:** Ethan was scheduled to clock out at 10 PM but clocked out at 10:40 PM to complete closing duties.
