# Attendance and Punctuality Policy

## Expectation

Associates are expected to:

- Arrive ready to work at the start of their scheduled shift.
- Remain at work for the full shift, except for authorized rest and meal breaks.
- Notify management in advance of any absence or late arrival.

Unapproved tardiness, early departures, or absences are disruptive. Repeated or excessive occurrences may result in corrective action up to and including termination.

If an associate believes their absence or tardiness is legally protected, they must notify their supervisor at the time of the absence. If discipline is applied in error, the associate should raise the issue with their supervisor or HR promptly. The company will investigate and correct any errors.

---

## Call-In Procedure

If unable to report to work, an associate must:

1. Call their supervisor or person in charge.
2. Call at least **2 hours before** the scheduled shift start.
3. If 2 hours advance notice is impossible, call as soon as possible.

If leaving early, the associate must notify their supervisor or person in charge as soon as they know they cannot complete the shift.

Unless on an approved leave, associates must call in on every scheduled day they will not report. If the call-in requirement cannot be met, the associate must explain why.

The company may ask for the general reason for any absence, tardiness, or early departure.

---

## Authorized Absences

The following absences will not result in disciplinary action:

- Pre-approved time off, including vacation or PTO.
- Paid sick and safe leave required by applicable law.
- Approved federal or state leaves (jury duty, military leave, FMLA, and similar).
- Absences approved as an accommodation under disability law.
- Work-related injuries covered by workers' compensation.
- Approved shift swaps.

---

## Unauthorized Absences

An absence is unauthorized when the associate fails to follow the call-in procedure or the absence is not legally protected or company-approved.

An associate is also considered to have an unauthorized absence if they are more than 2 hours late or miss more than 2 hours of a scheduled shift without approval.

**Progressive discipline — unauthorized absences within a 6-month period:**

| Occurrence | Action |
|------------|--------|
| 1st | Coaching |
| 2nd | Verbal Warning |
| 3rd | Written Warning |
| 4th | Final Warning |
| 5th | Termination |

---

## Tardiness

An associate is considered tardy if they clock in between **6 minutes and 2 hours** after their scheduled start time.

Being more than 2 hours late may be counted as an unauthorized absence.

**Progressive discipline — tardiness within a 6-month period:**

| Occurrence | Action |
|------------|--------|
| 1st | Coaching |
| 2nd | Verbal Warning |
| 3rd | Written Warning |
| 4th | Final Warning |
| 5th | Termination |

---

## Early Departure

Leaving before the end of a scheduled shift without approval is considered job abandonment and may result in termination.

An early departure is unexcused when it is not approved in advance by the General Manager, Store Manager, or person in charge.

---

## No Call – No Show

An associate who does not call in and does not report for **3 consecutively scheduled workdays** may be considered to have voluntarily abandoned their employment, absent extraordinary or legally protected circumstances.

**Progressive discipline — no call no show within a 6-month period:**

| Occurrence | Action |
|------------|--------|
| 1st | Written Warning |
| 2nd | Final Warning |
| 3rd | Termination |

---

## Policy Changes

The company reserves the right to modify this policy at any time. Variations may apply where required by state or local law.
