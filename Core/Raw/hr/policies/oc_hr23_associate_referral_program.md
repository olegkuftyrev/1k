# Associate Referral Program

*Effective: February 10, 2025 | Revised: December 19, 2024*

---

Associates who refer candidates that are hired may earn a referral bonus. This program applies to all company-owned store associates and all active Support Center associates (temporary or permanent).

---

## General Rules

- Participation is entirely voluntary. Referring associates should limit solicitation to after-hours conversations with friends, relatives, neighbors, and acquaintances.
- This is the only company-authorized referral bonus program. No associate may offer another associate any other form of referral payment.
- Both the referring associate and the referred associate must remain employed through the close of the Bonus Calculation Period for the bonus to be paid.
- Payment is processed on the payday following the close of the Bonus Calculation Period, via check or direct deposit. Bonuses appear on pay stubs as **"REF"** and are subject to applicable federal and state taxes.
- Only **one** referring associate is eligible for the bonus per referred candidate.

**How to submit a referral:**
1. Text `238837`, visit the referral portal, or scan the QR code and send the message `ReferToPanda`.
2. The hiring assistant will contact your candidate to complete an application.
3. You receive the bonus once the candidate is hired and reaches the required time frame.

## Operations Referrals

### Bonus Amounts

| Position | Bonus |
|---|---|
| General Manager | $3,000 |
| Assistant Manager or Chef | $1,500 |
| Shift Leader or Cook | $750 |
| Counter Help or Kitchen Help | $150 |

### Earning Requirements

The **Bonus Calculation Period** runs from the referred associate's date of hire through the close of the pay period in which the referred associate meets the 30- or 90-day requirement, or the approval date of GO100/SMIT/AMIT/CIT training — whichever applies.

**General Manager ($3,000 — paid in two parts):**
1. First $1,500: The referred associate must complete 90 days and graduate from the Store Manager In Training (SMIT) program to become a SM.
2. Second $1,500: The SM must complete GO100 and be promoted to GM within 11 periods of becoming an SM.

**Assistant Manager or Chef ($1,500 — paid in two parts):**
1. First $750: The referred associate must complete 90 days of employment.
2. Second $750: The AM/Chef must complete AMIT/CIT training within 12 weeks of their hire date.

**Shift Leader or Cook ($750):** Must complete 90 days of employment.

**Counter Help or Kitchen Help ($150):** Must complete 30 days of employment.

The bonus is determined by the position the referred associate is **initially hired into**, regardless of any promotion that occurs during the qualifying period.

### Ineligible Referrers

- Recruiters and Senior Leadership team members
- Area Leaders and above in Operations; Talent Acquisition Partners (these roles are eligible for the Support Center bonus instead)
- Any associate involved in interviewing or contributing to the hiring decision for that candidate
- The referred associate's direct manager at the time of hire

### Ineligible Candidates

- Associates being re-hired after fewer than two years away from the company
- Referrals to openings at licensee stores

## Support Center Referrals

### Bonus Amounts

| Job Level | Standard | Urgent Position |
|---|---|---|
| Director and above | $3,000 | $5,000 |
| P3 – M3 | $1,500 | $3,000 |
| Below P3 | $500 | $1,000 |

An **Urgent Position** must be listed as "Urgent" on the company careers page. To request that a position be designated as urgent, email your HR Business Partner.

### Earning Requirements

The **Bonus Calculation Period** runs from the referred associate's date of hire through the close of the pay period in which the referred associate completes **90 days** of employment.

The bonus is determined by the position the referred associate is **initially hired into**, regardless of any promotion during the qualifying period.

### Ineligible Candidates

- Contractors
- Consultants
- Any previously employed associates (rehires)

### Ineligible Referrers

- Support Center Directors and above; Support Center HR associates (these roles are eligible for the Operations bonus instead)
- Any associate involved in interviewing or contributing to the hiring decision for that candidate
