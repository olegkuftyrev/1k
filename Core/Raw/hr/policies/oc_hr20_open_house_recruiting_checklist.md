# Open House / Interview Day Recruiting Checklist

---

## 4 Weeks Before the Event

**Area Leader:**
- Contact the Recruiter or Talent Acquisition (TA) support at least 4 weeks before the event to assist with advertising the Open House online and ordering recruiting materials.
  - Information needed: hiring position, location, and time.
- Note: Online advertising should be posted 2–3 weeks before the event — enough lead time is critical.
- Order from Print On Demand and EBiz:
  1. Banner (1 for the interview day location)
  2. Posters and flyers for the interview day location and surrounding stores within a 10–15 mile radius — 1 double-sided poster and 200–400 half-sheet flyers per store
  3. Recruiting cards, first-time guest coupons, and General Manager tri-folds (50/pack) for direct sourcing, as needed
- Check business card inventory and order if needed.

---

## 3 Weeks Before the Event

**Area Leader:**
- Receive and distribute Interview Day materials to surrounding stores.

**TA Support:**
- Post the Interview Day listing online.
- Request marketing to post on social media (initiate this 4 weeks before the event).

---

## 2 Weeks Before the Event

**General Manager / Shift Manager:**
1. Communicate the interview day time, date, and location to every associate.
2. Ensure banners and posters are up in every store. Train every cashier (dining room and drive-thru) to pass out flyers to every guest.
3. Review the associate referral program and encourage associates to refer friends, family, or former supervisors from other companies.

**Area Leader:**
- Follow up on banner, poster, and flyer execution during store visits.

**Area Leader / TA Partner:**
- Train and discuss direct sourcing with the GM/SM team:
  1. Date, time, and target destination for direct sourcing
  2. What to bring during direct sourcing
  3. What to say during direct sourcing

---

## 1 Week Before the Event

**Area Leader / GMs / SMs / TA Partner:**
- Conduct direct sourcing in the surrounding neighborhood.

**Area Leader / Regional Leader:**
- Set multiple dates after the event for possible On-the-Job Preview (OJP) sessions.

**Area Leader:**
- Ensure the recruiting suitcase is stocked: thank-you letters, free entrée vouchers, sign-up sheets, etc.
- Review online applications and contact candidates of interest before the Open House.

---

## 2 Days Before and Day of Event

**TA Partner:**
- Send a reminder email to applicants in the applicant tracking system and to candidates from referrals and direct sourcing.

---

## Day of Event

**Area Leader:**
- Bring the recruiting suitcase.
- Arrive at least 1 hour before the event to set up the main sign-up table.
- Interview candidates using the interview guides.

**Area Leader / TA Partner / GM / SM:**
- Debrief after the Open House:
  1. What went well and areas for improvement
  2. Full candidate review — prioritize candidates in order of preference

---

## Day After

- Schedule second interviews and OJPs for the following week for top candidates.

**Within 1 Week:**
- Review remaining candidates, prioritize, and contact those of interest.
- Provide every candidate with a response or thank-you letter.
