# Personal Appearance Standards

*Effective: September 24, 2023*

---

## Overview

All associates are required to maintain a clean and professional appearance. Failure to comply may result in an associate being sent home. Continued non-compliance may result in corrective action, up to and including termination.

**Accommodations:** The company will reasonably accommodate exceptions to this policy for religious beliefs, medical conditions, or disabilities. Associates who need an accommodation should contact their supervisor and the HR Department. Nothing in this policy prevents associates from wearing a hair or facial hair style consistent with their cultural, ethnic, or racial heritage or identity. This policy will be interpreted in compliance with applicable local, state, and federal law.

---

## Operations Associates

### Uniform

Operations associates must be in their complete uniform — shirt, pants, socks, and shoes — and ready to work at their scheduled start time. Uniform components worn during working time must be put on promptly after clocking in and may be removed just before clocking out.

Wearing perfume or cologne is not permitted.

The uniform consists of:

- **Shirt** — Company-issued uniform top. T-shirt/shirt tails must be tucked in; collar buttoned (at minimum the bottom button).
- **Apron** — Company-issued. Must be worn during working time.
- **Cap** — Company-issued. Must be worn during working time.
- **Name Tag** — Company-issued. Must be worn during working time.
- **Safety Belt** — Company-issued. Must be worn during working time by Back of House associates.
- **Pants** — Clean, proper-fitting, solid color black or dark blue pants, or black or dark blue wash jeans. Must be full length. Not permitted: baggy or capri-style, too tight (no leggings, jeggings, leather, or yoga-style pants), athletic or stretchy fabric, distressed, faded, ripped, torn, or with holes, fringe, patches, embellishments, frayed/unfinished edges, or dangling loops or chains. No light-colored denim. Pants must not be worn below the waistline.
- **Socks** — Required. Any color or design. Ankle and no-show socks are permitted.
- **Shoes** — Closed-toe and heel, non-slip rubber sole. Any dark solid color (dark blue, black, dark brown, or dark grey) with black or white soles.
- **Sweater / Jacket** — May be worn over the uniform shirt when necessary.

Clothing must not contain words (in any language), symbols, or images that are obscene, profane, racist, sexual, or otherwise harassing.

**Uniform issuance:** At hire, associates receive a minimum of 2 shirts, 2 aprons, and 1 cap. Additional items may be issued based on weekly hours. Associates may request replacements from any manager if items become heavily soiled. Uniform items are company property and must be returned upon separation.

---

### Hair

- Hair must be pulled away from the face to the extent practicable.
- Long hair must be worn in a bun or ponytail above the collar and, where practicable, confined under the cap.
- Hair nets may be worn when appropriate and may be required by state law.
- Hair must be styled in a professional manner.

---

### Nails and Facial Hair

- Nails must be short and clean. Nail polish and artificial nails are not permitted for sanitary reasons.
- Mustaches, beards, and goatees must be well-maintained, neatly groomed, and trimmed to no more than ½ inch in length. Mustaches must not extend below the upper lip. Beard nets may be worn when appropriate and may be required by state law.

---

### Jewelry

- **Necklaces** — Permitted if they do not dangle outside the uniform.
- **Piercings** — One small stud nostril piercing on one nostril only is permitted. All other visible body or facial piercings (other than ear piercings) — including septum, nose cartilage, eyebrow/eyelid, lip, and tongue — are not permitted.
- **Earrings** — Any number of small conservative post-type earrings. Large or dangling earrings are not permitted. Plugs, hoops, gauges, spacers, and spikes are not permitted.
- **Watches** — One wristwatch may be worn. Watches are not permitted for Back of House associates for sanitary reasons.
- **Bracelets** — Not permitted.
- **Rings** — No more than one plain band.

---

### Tattoos

Visible tattoos up to 10 inches in length are permitted, with the following exceptions — these tattoos must be covered during working time:

- Tattoos on the head, face, or neck (small tattoos behind the ear are permissible).
- Tattoos containing words (in any language), symbols, or images that are obscene, profane, racist, sexual, or harassing.
- Tattoos affiliated with gangs or hate groups.

If a visible tattoo exceeds 10 inches but otherwise complies with this policy, only the portion exceeding 10 inches must be covered.

## Support Center Associates

Support Center associates should use their best judgment to maintain a professional work environment.

### Clothing

**Permitted:**
- Suits (required when the meeting warrants it)
- Casual pants: khakis, chinos, corduroys, and business-appropriate dark-colored jeans (no holes, tears, or patches)
- Shirts with collars, business casual crewneck or V-neck shirts, blouses, golf and polo shirts
- Dress sandals, mules, tie shoes, and non-athletic clean sneakers

**Not permitted:**
- Light-colored jeans*, shorts, skirts or dresses shorter than 2 inches above the knee
- Pants that are too tight (leggings, jeggings, yoga-style), athletic material, or worn below the waist
- T-shirts*, muscle shirts, crop tops, camouflage prints, tank tops, spaghetti strap tops
- Shirts or tops with holes, tears, patches, or large brand logos
- Tennis shoes*, athletic/gym shoes, flip flops

**\*Exception: Light-colored jeans, T-shirts, and tennis shoes are permitted on Casual Fridays.**

Clothing must not contain words (in any language), symbols, or images that are obscene, profane, racist, sexual, or otherwise harassing.

---

### Hair

Hair must be styled in a professional manner.

---

### Facial Hair

Mustaches, beards, and goatees must be well-maintained, neatly groomed, and trimmed to no more than ½ inch. Mustaches must not extend below the upper lip.

---

### Jewelry

- **Piercings** — One small stud nostril piercing on one nostril only is permitted. All other visible body or facial piercings (other than ear piercings) are not permitted.
- **Earrings** — No limit on ear piercings. Plugs, gauges, spacers, and spikes are not permitted.

---

### Tattoos

Same standards as Operations associates (see above).

---

### Field Training

All Support Center associates must adhere to the Operations Associates' Personal Appearance Standards when conducting field training at store locations.
