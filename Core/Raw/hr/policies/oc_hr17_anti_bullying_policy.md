# Anti-Bullying Policy

The company prohibits workplace bullying.

Workplace bullying includes actions taken by one associate against another, with malice, that are hostile, offensive, and unrelated to legitimate business interests.

Examples include:

1. Repeated verbal abuse — derogatory remarks, insults, epithets.
2. Verbal or physical conduct that a reasonable person would find threatening, intimidating, or humiliating.
3. Gratuitous sabotage or undermining of another person's work performance.

---

## Reporting

Any associate who believes they have been bullied, is being pressured to participate in bullying, or has information about bullying by a coworker, supervisor, agent, guest, vendor, or other third party must report it immediately — verbally or in writing — to HR, their supervisor, or any member of management.

If the supervisor is the subject of the complaint, report directly to HR.

---

## Investigation

The company will conduct a prompt, thorough, and objective investigation of every complaint. Privacy and confidentiality of all parties will be protected to the extent possible while allowing a complete investigation.

If a complaint is substantiated, disciplinary action up to and including termination will be taken.

---

## No Retaliation

The company will not tolerate retaliation against any associate who makes a good-faith bullying complaint.
