# Code of Conduct and Ethics

## Foundation

Integrity is the foundation of every relationship the company holds — with associates, business partners, guests, and the communities we operate in.

Every associate is expected to uphold the highest standards of ethical conduct. Individual integrity and mutual accountability are the best assurances this Code will be followed.

Our continued success depends on the trust others place in us. Protecting that trust requires exceeding the minimum requirements of the law and industry practice — not just meeting them.

Violations at any level will not be tolerated and may result in termination as well as civil or criminal liability for the company, individuals, or both.

---

## Core Principles

Every associate is expected to:

- **Obey the law.** Follow all applicable laws and regulations.
- **Be honest.** Present facts fully, fairly, and accurately.
- **Be fair.** Give everyone due regard and respect.
- **Be concerned.** Consider how company actions affect others and work to make those effects as beneficial as possible.
- **Use good judgment.** Avoid actions or circumstances that may compromise business judgment or create a conflict between personal and company interests.
- **Think long term.** Do not take shortcuts that may harm the company in the future.
- **Reject personal benefits.** Decline anything that could jeopardize the company's integrity.

Following these principles may occasionally mean declining business or accepting a short-term financial cost. That is acceptable. Over time, integrity gains business, builds strong relationships, and earns lasting trust.

---

## The Test

Before taking any action, ask yourself:

- Am I comfortable with this action?
- Would I be willing to tell people I love and respect what I am doing?
- Would I be comfortable seeing this reported on the front page of a local newspaper?

If the answer to any of these is no — **do not do it**. Ask your leader for guidance.

If you feel pressured to act against this Code — **do not do it**. Get guidance immediately.

---

## Reporting

If any part of this Code is not followed, associates are required to report it immediately so corrective action can be taken.

You may report concerns to:

- Your direct leader.
- The Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer, Chief People Officer, or General Counsel.
- The company's confidential HR hotline (contact HR for the current number).

Anonymous inquiries may be made through the confidential HR hotline.

---

## Changes to This Code

Any changes to this Code must be approved by the Board of Directors.
