# PTO, Vacation, and Paid Sick Leave Policy

---

## Part 1 — Paid Time Off (PTO): Management Associates

### Eligibility

Regular Operations Management and Support Center associates are eligible and begin earning PTO on their first day of work.

### Scheduling

- Request PTO at least **2 weeks in advance** whenever possible.
- All requests must be approved by the supervisor.
- If a planned request cannot be approved, discuss alternative dates with your supervisor.

### PTO as Paid Sick Leave

In locations with a mandatory paid sick leave (PSL) law, PTO serves as the exclusive mechanism for complying with those requirements to the fullest extent of the law.

- **Foreseeable absence:** advance notice required.
- **Unforeseeable absence:** notify your supervisor as soon as reasonably possible.

### Pay Rate and Overtime

Associates are paid at their regular hourly rate while on PTO. Overtime is not paid during PTO periods. PTO does not accrue during a leave of absence.

### Cash-Out

Accrued PTO may not be cashed out except upon termination.

### Accrual Cap

The maximum banked PTO at any time is **240 hours** or 1.5× the maximum annual entitlement, whichever is greater.

Once the cap is reached, accrual stops until hours are used below the cap. There is no retroactive accrual for time spent at the cap.

## Part 2 — Vacation: Non-Management Associates

### Eligibility

Regular Operations Non-Management associates working at least **30 hours per week** are eligible and begin earning vacation on their first day of work.

### Scheduling

- Request vacation at least **2 weeks in advance**.
- All requests must be approved by the supervisor.
- If two associates request the same period, priority goes to the one who requested first, or to longer service if requests were made simultaneously.

### Pay Rate and Overtime

Associates are paid at their regular hourly rate for the number of hours in their regular workday while on vacation. Vacation does not count toward overtime. Vacation does not accrue during a leave of absence.

Vacation may be taken in 1-hour increments, up to a maximum of 8 hours in a single day.

### Cash-Out

Accrued vacation may not be cashed out except upon termination.

### Accrual Cap

Maximum banked vacation: **120 hours** or 1.5× the maximum annual entitlement, whichever is greater.

Once the cap is reached, accrual stops until hours are used below the cap. There is no retroactive accrual for time at the cap.

### Vacation Accrual Table — Non-Management Associates

| Service | Accrual per Pay Period | Maximum per Year |
|---------|----------------------|-----------------|
| 0 – 1 year | 0.62 × all hours worked per pay period ÷ 80 | Up to 16 hrs/year |
| 1 – 5 years | 1.39 × all hours worked per pay period ÷ 80 | Up to 36 hrs/year |
| 5+ years | 2.16 × all hours worked per pay period ÷ 80 | Up to 56 hrs/year |

Hours worked per pay period are capped at 80 hours (biweekly) or 40 hours (weekly) for accrual purposes, even if more hours were worked.

Associates must work at least 60 hours per biweekly period (or 30 hours per weekly period) to earn vacation for that pay period.

## Part 3 — Paid Sick Leave (PSL): Non-Management Associates

*Applies to Non-California locations. California associates should contact HR for applicable rules.*

### Definitions

**Family Member** includes: spouse or partner, child, parent, parent-in-law, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, legal guardian or ward, registered domestic partner and their child or parent, and any other individual covered by applicable law. Includes biological, adoptive, step, and foster relationships. Child applies regardless of age or dependency status.

**Sick Time** may be used for the associate or a family member for:
- Diagnosis, care, or treatment of an existing medical condition.
- Preventive care.
- Medical or dental appointments.
- Care for a family member with a mental or physical illness.
- Recovery from an injury or health condition.

**Safe Time** may be used when an associate is a victim of domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking and needs time for safety planning, medical attention, counseling, or judicial assistance.

### Accrual

Associates earn **1 hour of PSL for every 30 hours worked**, beginning on the first day of employment.

- PSL accrues in 1-hour increments only (no partial hours, unless required by law).
- Banked accrual cap: **48 hours** or the equivalent of 6 workdays (whichever is greater).
- Once the cap is reached, accrual stops until hours are used below the cap.
- Unused PSL carries over year to year.

### Annual Use Cap

Associates may use up to **40 hours or 5 workdays per year** (whichever is greater based on the associate's schedule).

Example: An associate who works 10-hour days may use up to 50 hours per year (5 × 10 hours). An associate who works 6-hour days may use up to 40 hours per year (because 40 is greater than 5 × 6 = 30 hours).

PSL may be used in hourly increments or as required by applicable law.

### Notice

- **Foreseeable use:** provide advance notice to your supervisor.
- **Unforeseeable use:** notify your supervisor as soon as reasonably possible.

Always specify that the absence is for PSL purposes so it can be properly designated.

### Pay Rate

PSL is paid at the associate's regular rate of pay, or as required by applicable law.

### Termination and Rehire

Unused PSL is not paid out upon separation.

Associates rehired within 12 months of separation will have their previously accrued PSL reinstated (up to the 48-hour cap or the amount required by applicable law). Reinstated PSL may be used immediately.

### No Retaliation

The company prohibits discrimination or retaliation against any associate who:

- Requests or uses PSL for authorized purposes.
- Files a complaint or reports a suspected policy violation.
- Cooperates with an investigation into a claimed PSL law violation.
- Opposes a practice prohibited by applicable PSL law.

If applicable law provides greater or different PSL benefits, the company will comply with those requirements.
