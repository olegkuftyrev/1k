# Out-of-Store Time Card Tracking Form

Use this form to record time spent on business-related activities performed outside of your store location.

Complete this form before editing your time card for any out-of-store business time. This form does not need to be submitted to Payroll. Keep completed copies at the store so your Area Leader can review them if needed.

---

## Activities That Require Tracking

You must be paid for any of the following activities when done outside the store:

- Business meetings.
- Company-required trainings and events.
- Catering.
- Visiting other locations for business purposes on days off.
- Receiving or making business-related phone calls on days off.
- Business-related communications with your Area Leader on days off.
- Completing restaurant paperwork at home.
- Completing required trainings or reading assignments at home.
- Manager conferences for required activities.
- Returning or borrowing inventory after hours.
- Attending job fairs and recruiting events.
- Picking up or dropping off company personnel for travel.
- Attending workers' comp depositions or trials; picking up police reports; attending health department hearings.
- Travel time.
- Purchasing store supplies.
- Other unplanned business-related activities (explanation required).
