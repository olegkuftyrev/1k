# PAWS Security Level — FAQ

---

**Q1: What is the purpose of PMSEC?**

To grant appropriate system access to associates who need to use the PAWS application — for example, Xpient closing and Cash Sheet entry.

---

**Q2: Who can request a temporary PAWS security level change?**

Any associate with security level 20 or 30 who has been granted Security Administration Authority.

---

**Q3: Who can request a permanent PAWS security level change on ePAWS?**

The Person in Charge (PIC) of the store as listed on the Org Chart.

---

**Q4: If the store has a PIC but the Area Leader submits the request, is a second approver still required?**

Yes. One supervisor must submit the request and a different supervisor must approve it — regardless of who initiates the request.

---

**Q5: How long is a temporary authorization valid?**

24 hours from the time authorization is granted. For any access needed beyond 24 hours, use permanent authorization instead.

---

**Q6: Will a promotion or demotion automatically update an associate's PAWS security level?**

Yes. When a job title changes, the PAWS system automatically resets the associate's security level to match their new position.

---

**Q7: Can authorized-to-work associates be granted PAWS access?**

Yes. Authorized-to-work associates may be granted the same security level as permanent associates in the borrowing store.
