# AI Use Policy

## Purpose

AI tools automate tasks, support decisions, and provide operational insights. They also create risks: data leaks, IP loss, and legal exposure.

This policy sets the rules for using AI tools at work. It protects company information, ensures legal compliance, and reduces business risk.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all associates, contractors, partners, and authorized users who use AI tools in connection with company business — on or off company premises.

AI tools covered include:

- Large Language Models (LLMs).
- Machine Learning (ML) systems.
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools.
- Deep Learning (DL) systems.
- Computer Vision (CV) tools.
- Any other AI algorithm or AI-powered product.

---

## Confidentiality

Do not enter confidential, proprietary, or sensitive information into AI tools unless the tool has been approved for that use by company leadership.

Information that must never be entered into unapproved AI tools:

- Trade secrets.
- Personal information (names, addresses, dates of birth, Social Security numbers).
- Financial records.
- Associate records.
- Guest or customer information.
- Vendor contracts, pricing, or commitments.
- Legal, compliance, or investigation materials.
- Any internal data not intended for outside parties.

All company policies on confidential information apply equally to AI tool use.

---

## Recording and Transcription

Most AI tools record or transcribe conversations.

Before using an AI tool to record or transcribe any meeting, call, or conversation:

1. Disclose that you are using an AI tool.
2. Obtain affirmative consent from every participant.

The tool must be capable of capturing consent within the tool itself and generating a consent transcript. If it cannot, do not use it for recording.

Do not record conversations that involve:

- Sensitive personnel matters.
- Legal advice or privileged communications.
- Compliance investigations.

---

## IP Ownership

Content generated by AI tools — text, images, video, designs — is not company intellectual property (IP).

Do not:

- Assume the company owns AI-generated content.
- Use AI tools to generate materials that the company needs to own exclusively.
- Upload or reference company logos, trademarks, product images, or branded materials into any AI tool.

For new creative materials, route requests through the appropriate internal department.

---

## IP Infringement

Do not use AI tools to generate content that infringes the IP rights of others.

When writing prompts:

- Use descriptive or generic language only.
- Do not reference specific people, brands, characters, or copyrighted works.
- Do not request content that emulates a specific artist's style.
- Do not request content featuring real people, cartoon characters, or third-party trademarks.

For any AI-generated content intended for public distribution or external audiences, obtain approval from the appropriate internal department before release.

---

## Employment Decisions

Do not use AI tools to make or support employment decisions without prior approval from Legal.

All AI tool use must comply with applicable employment laws in each jurisdiction where the company operates.

---

## Privacy

Do not enter personally identifiable information into AI tools unless the tool has been approved for that specific use.

Comply with company Privacy Policy at all times when using AI.

---

## Human Review

AI generates output based on patterns. Output may be inaccurate, incomplete, biased, or inappropriate.

Before relying on AI-generated content for work:

- Review for accuracy.
- Review for professional tone.
- Confirm consistency with company values.

AI is a tool. It does not replace your judgment.

---

## Transparency

Label AI-generated content as such wherever appropriate.

Do not:

- Remove or alter AI-generated labels or watermarks.
- Represent AI output as original human work without disclosure.

Do not use AI to generate content that is illegal, discriminatory, offensive, or harmful.

---

## Monitoring

Company monitoring policies apply when using AI tools on company equipment or networks. The company may review prompts, messages, attachments, and files shared with AI tools.

---

## Violations

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contractual relationship.

---

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a specific AI tool or use is permitted, ask your manager before proceeding.
