# ICE Investigation Procedures

*U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE)*

---

## If ICE Agents Appear with a Notice of Inspection (NOI)

The SM / GM / Team Leader / PIC must:

1. Remain calm and cooperative.
2. Ask for ID and collect the agent's business card.
3. Notify the agent that the company's headquarters will work with ICE to provide requested information.
4. **Immediately contact** the Field HR Manager and Area Leader.
5. **Immediately forward** the NOI to the Field HR Manager.

**Field HR Manager must immediately forward the NOI to the Workplace Legal Team** — the company is under a **72-hour deadline** to provide the requested information.

---

### Special Instructions for California

- **Do not** allow ICE agents to access non-public areas (back of house, behind the counter, kitchen) unless they present a **warrant signed by a judge**.
- **Do not** voluntarily provide access to personnel records or hand over any records unless they present a **subpoena or search warrant signed by a judge**.
- If a subpoena is issued, the corporate office will work with ICE to provide the requested information.
- **Immediately forward** the NOI (and subpoena, if applicable) to the Workplace Legal Team — the company is under a **72-hour deadline** to notify associates that the NOI was issued and to post the NOI at the worksite for associates to see.

---

## If ICE Agents Appear with a Search Warrant or Arrest Warrant

The SM / GM / Team Leader / PIC must:

1. Ask for ID and collect the agent's business card.
2. Obtain a copy of the warrant.
3. Remain calm and cooperative — do not stop or interfere with the agent's duties.
4. Shadow the agent (the operator has the right to follow the agent throughout the visit).
5. Take notes of everything the agents are doing.
6. Make a list of all items removed from the premises.
7. If an associate is arrested, ask the agent where they will take the individual, then share that information with the Field HR Manager and Area Leader.
8. **Immediately contact** the Field HR Manager and Area Leader.
9. **Immediately forward** a copy of the warrant to the Field HR Manager.

**Field HR Manager must immediately forward the copy of the warrant to the Workplace Legal Team** for further review and handling.
