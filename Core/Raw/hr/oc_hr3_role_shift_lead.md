# HR-3 Leadership Role: Shift Lead

## Purpose

The Shift Lead provides quality service to guests and assumes responsibility for store operations when the General Manager and Assistant Manager are not available.

The Shift Lead develops Counter Help associates, upholds operational standards, and serves as the first line of store leadership.

---

## Position Summary

The Shift Lead is accountable for:

- Guest service.
- Counter Help training.
- Front of House (FOH) operations.
- Cash handling.
- Food safety compliance.
- Store environment.
- Administrative support.
- Store operations in the absence of the General Manager (GM) and Assistant Manager (AM).

---

## Leadership Expectations

Shift Leads are expected to demonstrate:

- Workability.
- Intensity.
- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Teamwork.
- Reliability.

Leadership begins before the title.

---

## Culture Leadership

The Shift Lead:

- Lives company mission and values.
- Creates a safe and empowering environment.
- Encourages teammates to listen, appreciate, and challenge each other.
- Proposes ways to improve processes.
- Models leadership behaviors every shift.

Culture is reinforced through daily actions.

---

## Training Responsibilities

The Shift Lead trains:

- Counter Help associates on FOH responsibilities.
- Back of House (BOH) associates on FOH responsibilities when needed.
- New associates on standards, procedures, and expectations.

Every Shift Lead helps develop the next leader.

---

## Guest Experience

The Shift Lead:

- Greets guests courteously and professionally.
- Provides accurate menu information.
- Handles guest questions and complaints effectively.
- Monitors food quality.
- Provides feedback to the GM, AM, or Chef on food quality concerns.

Guests return because of the experience.

---

## Store Environment

The Shift Lead maintains:

- A clean, safe, and sanitary store environment.
- Compliance with operations standards.
- Proper use, handling, and storage of cleaning chemicals.
- Food safety and work safety checklist completion.

Clean environments create safe environments.

---

## Operations Responsibilities

The Shift Lead:

- Upholds operational standards.
- Manages the steam table with fresh, presentable food.
- Orders appropriate food quantities during busy and slow periods.
- Takes and fulfills catering orders according to guest requests.
- Learns to prepare entrees under direction of the Cook or Chef.
- Assumes responsibility for smooth store operations when the GM and AM are unavailable.

Execution creates consistency.

---

## Cash Handling

The Shift Lead:

- Follows company cash handling procedures and policy.
- Accepts cash, credit cards, gift cards, and all approved payment methods.
- Operates the point-of-sale (POS) system accurately.
- May perform register opening and closing procedures.

Every cash transaction must follow policy.

---

## Administrative Support

The Shift Lead assists the GM with:

- Inputting invoices.
- Preparing reports.
- Ordering food and non-food items.

Administrative competence supports operational accuracy.

---

## Required Qualifications

Recommended qualifications include:

- Some high school education or equivalent.
- Minimum one year of operations experience.
- Food handler certification where required by local law.

---

## Knowledge and Skills

Shift Leads should demonstrate:

- Knowledge of store opening and closing procedures.
- Knowledge of steam table operations, serving protocols, menu, and recipes.
- Ability to assist with administrative work.
- Ability to maintain smooth operations in the absence of the GM and AM.
- Consistent application of cash handling policy.
- Ability to perform all FOH responsibilities.
- Ability to train Counter Help associates.
- Understanding of food safety and work safety checklists.
- Proper handling of cleaning chemicals.

---

## Leadership Competencies

Core competencies include:

- Self-knowledge.
- Customer focus.
- Interpersonal savvy.
- Drive for results.
- Career ambition.
- Integrity and trust.

---

## Success Measures

Performance may be measured through:

- Guest satisfaction.
- Operational standard execution.
- Store performance in the absence of GM and AM.
- Associate feedback.
- Administrative support effectiveness.
- Food safety compliance.
- Cash handling accuracy.

---

## Career Progression

Potential advancement opportunities include:

- Assistant Manager.

Advancement depends on leadership performance and business needs.

---

## Work Environment

Shift Leads frequently:

- Stand for extended periods.
- Work in hot environments with steam from the induction table.
- Work in high-noise kitchens.
- Reach across counter tops.
- Perform repetitive tasks.
- Work with hot and cold foods.
- Cross-train in BOH responsibilities.
