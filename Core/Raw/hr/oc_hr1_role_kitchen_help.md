# HR-1 Leadership Role: Kitchen Help

## Purpose

The Kitchen Help position serves as the foundation of kitchen operations. The role is responsible for supporting food preparation, maintaining cleanliness, learning operational standards, and building the skills necessary for future growth.

Kitchen Help is the first step toward becoming a Cook, Chef, and future operational leader.

---

## Position Summary

Kitchen Help is responsible for:

- Supporting kitchen operations.
- Assisting with food preparation.
- Cleaning and sanitizing equipment.
- Maintaining food safety standards.
- Supporting guest experience.
- Learning kitchen operations.

Every leader begins with mastering the fundamentals.

---

## Leadership Expectations

Kitchen Help associates are expected to demonstrate:

- Workability.
- Intensity.
- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Reliability.
- Teamwork.
- Positive attitude.

Leadership starts before the title.

---

## Culture Responsibilities

Kitchen Help:

- Lives company values.
- Supports teammates.
- Demonstrates respect.
- Maintains a positive attitude.
- Accepts coaching.
- Contributes to team success.

Culture is everyone's responsibility.

---

## Guest Experience

Kitchen Help contributes to guest satisfaction by:

- Preparing quality food.
- Supporting food availability.
- Maintaining cleanliness.
- Assisting kitchen operations.
- Delivering consistency.

Guests experience the kitchen through every product served.

---

## Food Preparation

Kitchen Help:

- Operates fryers, steamers, grills, and microwaves.
- Assists with ingredient preparation.
- Supports food production.
- Learns recipes according to training schedule.

Mastering fundamentals creates future opportunities.

---

## Store Environment

Kitchen Help maintains:

- Clean workstations.
- Clean equipment.
- Organized storage areas.
- Proper sanitation.
- Safe working conditions.

Clean kitchens create safe kitchens.

---

## Operations Responsibilities

Kitchen Help:

- Follows operational standards.
- Supports cooks and chefs.
- Stocks products and supplies.
- Organizes utensils and equipment.
- Assists Front of House (FOH) when needed.

Operational discipline begins with daily habits.

---

## Food and Workplace Safety

Kitchen Help follows:

- Food safety standards.
- Sanitation procedures.
- Equipment safety procedures.
- Chemical handling guidelines.
- Daily safety checklists.

Safety protects associates and guests.

---

## Learning and Development

Kitchen Help associates are expected to:

- Learn recipes.
- Develop cooking skills.
- Learn kitchen equipment.
- Learn food safety.
- Learn FOH responsibilities.
- Accept coaching and feedback.

Continuous learning creates growth opportunities.

---

## Required Qualifications

Recommended qualifications include:

- Some high school education or equivalent.
- Food handler certification where required.
- Flexible scheduling availability.
- Desire to learn and grow.

Experience is helpful but not required.

---

## Knowledge and Skills

Kitchen Help associates should demonstrate:

- Basic operational knowledge.
- Basic food preparation skills.
- Food safety awareness.
- Cleaning and sanitation knowledge.
- Teamwork.
- Communication.
- Willingness to learn.

---

## Leadership Competencies

Core competencies include:

- Self-knowledge.
- Customer focus.
- Interpersonal savvy.
- Drive for results.
- Career ambition.
- Integrity and trust.

---

## Success Measures

Performance may be measured through:

- Reliability.
- Food quality.
- Cleanliness.
- Safety compliance.
- Teamwork.
- Learning progress.
- Operational consistency.
- Positive attitude.

---

## Career Progression

Potential advancement opportunities include:

- Cook.
- Counter Help.
- Shift Leader.

Growth depends on performance, learning, and leadership.

---

## Work Environment

Kitchen Help frequently:

- Works in hot kitchens.
- Stands for extended periods.
- Operates kitchen equipment.
- Performs repetitive tasks.
- Lifts products and supplies.
- Works with hot and cold foods.

---

## Leadership Philosophy

A Kitchen Help succeeds by:

- Showing up consistently.
- Learning continuously.
- Supporting the team.
- Maintaining standards.
- Accepting feedback.
- Taking ownership.

Every leader begins by mastering the basics.

Kitchen Help is not an entry-level job.

It is the foundation of future leadership.
