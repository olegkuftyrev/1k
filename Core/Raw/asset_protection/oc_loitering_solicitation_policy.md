# ORDINA

## Loitering and Solicitation Policy

### Summary

Ordina is committed to providing a safe, welcoming, and comfortable environment for guests and associates.

Unauthorized solicitation, panhandling, or loitering on company property may negatively affect safety, guest experience, and business operations. Managers are responsible for addressing these situations professionally, respectfully, and in accordance with local laws.

---

# Purpose

Establish guidelines for managing loitering, solicitation, and unauthorized activity on or around company property.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Definitions

### Solicitation

Solicitation is the act of requesting money, donations, goods, services, or other items from guests, associates, or visitors.

This may include:

- Verbal requests.
- Signs or written messages.
- Physical gestures.
- Requests for donations or money.

---

### Loitering

Loitering is remaining on company property without conducting business or without a legitimate purpose.

Applicable laws regarding loitering vary by jurisdiction.

---

# Safety Concerns

Unauthorized activity on company property may:

- Create discomfort for guests.
- Affect associate safety.
- Disrupt operations.
- Impact sales.
- Increase property damage risks.
- Lead to vandalism or criminal activity.

Each situation should be evaluated individually.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers should:

- Observe the situation.
- Maintain a calm and professional approach.
- Prioritize guest and associate safety.
- Follow local laws and company procedures.
- Document recurring issues.

Managers should avoid unnecessary confrontation.

---

# Associate Responsibilities

Associates should:

- Notify management of concerns.
- Avoid direct confrontation whenever possible.
- Prioritize personal safety.
- Report suspicious activity.

Associates should never place themselves in unsafe situations.

---

# Law Enforcement and Security Support

Management may contact:

- Property management.
- Security personnel.
- Local law enforcement.
- Company leadership.

Additional security measures may be implemented when necessary.

---

# Prohibited Actions

Associates should not:

- Physically remove individuals.
- Escalate confrontations.
- Threaten or intimidate others.
- Place themselves in danger.
- Ignore safety concerns.

---

# Guiding Principle

Treat every individual with dignity and respect while protecting the safety of our guests, associates, and business operations.

Safety first. Professionalism always.
