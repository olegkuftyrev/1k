# ORDINA

## Armored Carrier & Deposit Bag Procedures

### Summary

Proper handling of change orders, deposits, and armored carrier services protects company funds and ensures accurate cash management.

All associates handling deposits or change orders must follow these procedures exactly.

---

# Purpose

Establish standards for:

- Change order exchanges.
- Manual deposits.
- Deposit preparation.
- Armored carrier interactions.
- Safe fund management.

This procedure applies to all Ordina locations utilizing armored cash services.

---

# General Rules

- Prepare all deposits before scheduled service.
- Verify all amounts.
- Secure funds inside the safe.
- Maintain accurate records.
- Report discrepancies immediately.

Cash accountability begins before the carrier arrives.

---

# Change Order Bags

Change order bags are used exclusively for:

- Change fund exchanges.
- Currency replenishment.

The following items may not be placed inside:

- Deposit slips.
- Checks.
- Loose coins.
- Miscellaneous documents.

Approved rolled coins may be included when permitted.

---

# Preparing Change Order Payments

Before the scheduled delivery:

1. Place the exact payment amount inside the change order bag.
2. Complete all required information:
  - Store identification.
  - Date.
  - Amount.
  - Order number.
  - Associate preparing the bag.
3. Seal the bag completely.
4. Secure the bag inside the safe.

---

# Delivery Procedures

Before carrier arrival:

- Move prepared bags to the authorized access area.
- Verify documentation is complete.

When the carrier arrives:

- Exchange the prepared bag.
- Receive the new change order.
- Obtain required signatures.
- Complete delivery documentation.

---

# After Delivery

Management must:

- Count received funds.
- Verify accuracy.
- Secure funds immediately.
- Report discrepancies.

Any shortages or overages should be investigated immediately.

---

# Manual Deposit Bags

Manual deposits are used for items such as:

- Checks.
- Catering payments.
- Unacceptable currency.
- Special deposits.

Manual deposits require:

- Deposit documentation.
- Deposit slips.
- Proper recording.
- Safe storage.

---

# Manual Deposit Procedures

1. Prepare the deposit.
2. Complete deposit documentation.
3. Secure the deposit.
4. Record the transaction.
5. Document carrier pickup.

Manual deposits should always be tracked separately.

---

# Safe Collection Bags

Safe collection bags are used exclusively by armored carriers during cash collection.

Associates may not:

- Open carrier collection bags.
- Reuse collection bags.
- Remove secured deposits.

---

# Safe Storage

All prepared deposits must remain:

- Inside the safe.
- Properly secured.
- Accessible only to authorized personnel.

Funds should never remain unsecured.

---

# Documentation

The following records should be maintained:

- Deposit logs.
- Carrier signatures.
- Delivery records.
- Change order records.
- Discrepancy reports.

Accurate documentation protects both the associate and the company.

---

# Discrepancies

If discrepancies occur:

- Stop processing immediately.
- Verify all counts.
- Notify management.
- Document the issue.
- Report the discrepancy according to company procedures.

---

# Prohibited Practices

Associates may not:

- Place unauthorized items in change order bags.
- Leave prepared deposits unsecured.
- Skip documentation.
- Share access credentials.
- Ignore discrepancies.
- Release deposits without verification.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every dollar must be accounted for.

Strong cash controls protect our associates, our business, and our guests.
