# ORDINA

## Vehicle Security & Break-In Prevention

### Summary

Vehicle theft and vehicle break-ins often occur because valuables are visible inside the vehicle.

Associates should take reasonable precautions to protect personal property while at work.

Ordina is committed to promoting a safe environment for associates and guests.

---

# Purpose

Provide guidance to help associates reduce the risk of vehicle theft, break-ins, and property loss.

---

# Remove Valuables From Sight

Visible items attract theft.

Whenever possible, do not leave the following items inside your vehicle:

### Electronics

- Laptops
- Mobile phones
- Tablets
- Cameras
- Headphones
- USB drives
- Chargers and cables

### Bags and Personal Property

- Handbags
- Purses
- Backpacks
- Briefcases
- Duffle bags
- Luggage
- Shopping bags

### Documents

- Mail
- Financial documents
- Identification documents
- Work materials
- Personal records

### Valuables

- Cash
- Coins
- Jewelry
- Spare keys
- Gift cards
- Wallets

---

# Parking Recommendations

Associates should:

- Park in well-lit areas whenever possible.
- Park in visible locations.
- Avoid isolated areas.
- Remain aware of surroundings.
- Travel in pairs after dark when possible.

---

# Secure Your Vehicle

Before leaving your vehicle:

- Lock all doors.
- Close all windows.
- Turn off the vehicle.
- Remove keys.
- Verify the vehicle is secured.

---

# Conceal Necessary Items

If valuables must remain inside a vehicle:

- Place them inside the trunk.
- Store them before arriving at the location.
- Keep them completely out of sight.

Do not move valuables into the trunk after parking if others are watching.

---

# Suspicious Activity

Immediately notify management or law enforcement if you observe:

- Individuals looking into vehicles.
- Attempted break-ins.
- Damaged vehicles.
- Suspicious behavior in the parking lot.
- Theft or vandalism.

---

# After a Vehicle Break-In

If your vehicle has been damaged or entered:

1. Do not touch the vehicle unnecessarily.
2. Contact local law enforcement.
3. Notify management if the incident occurred on company property.
4. Document any damage.
5. Remove the vehicle only after police approval if necessary.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers should:

- Maintain exterior lighting.
- Encourage safe parking practices.
- Report security concerns.
- Support associates following incidents.
- Coordinate with property management when necessary.

---

# Guiding Principle

Take valuables with you.

Keep vehicles secured.

Stay aware of your surroundings.

Prevention is the best protection.
