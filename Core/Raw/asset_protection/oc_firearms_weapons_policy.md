# ORDINA

## Firearms & Weapons Policy

### Washington State Locations

### Summary

Ordina is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and welcoming environment for guests and associates while complying with Washington State law.

The safety of our associates and guests remains our highest priority. Associates are expected to respond professionally and immediately report any unsafe behavior involving firearms or weapons.

---

# Purpose

This policy establishes standards regarding firearms and weapons at Ordina locations operating within Washington State.

---

# Guest Firearms

Ordina recognizes that Washington law permits lawful possession and carrying of firearms in certain circumstances.

Guests who legally possess firearms may enter the restaurant where permitted by law.

The presence of a lawfully carried firearm alone does not constitute a violation of company policy.

---

# Safe Firearm Handling

Any firearm brought into the restaurant must remain:

- Secured in a holster or carrying device.
- Under the control of the owner.
- Handled safely and responsibly.

Guests may not:

- Remove a firearm from a holster.
- Handle or display a firearm.
- Brandish a firearm.
- Use a firearm to threaten or intimidate others.

---

# Prohibited Conduct

The following behavior is prohibited:

- Displaying a firearm in a threatening manner.
- Handling or manipulating a firearm inside the restaurant.
- Threatening guests or associates.
- Reckless or unsafe behavior.
- Violating applicable laws.

Any unsafe or threatening behavior may result in removal from the property and law enforcement involvement.

---

# Associate Weapons

Associates, vendors, contractors, and visitors entering non-public work areas may not possess firearms or other weapons while performing work duties, except where otherwise protected by applicable law.

This includes:

- Kitchen areas.
- Back-of-house areas.
- Offices.
- Storage areas.
- Production areas.

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

---

# Guest Concerns

If a guest expresses concern regarding another guest lawfully carrying a firearm, associates should remain calm and professional.

Suggested response:

> "Ordina complies with Washington State law. If you would prefer another table or seating area, we would be happy to assist you."

Associates should never argue with guests regarding firearm laws.

---

# Unsafe Situations

Management should immediately intervene if an individual:

- Removes a firearm from a holster.
- Makes threats.
- Behaves aggressively.
- Appears impaired.
- Creates fear or panic.
- Refuses lawful instructions.

If an immediate threat exists, call 911.

---

# Incident Reporting

Managers must document any firearm-related incident, including:

- Unsafe firearm handling.
- Threats or intimidation.
- Law enforcement involvement.
- Guest complaints involving safety concerns.

Significant incidents should be reported to leadership immediately.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Maintaining a safe environment.
- Responding professionally.
- Avoiding unnecessary confrontation.
- Contacting law enforcement when necessary.
- Supporting associates and guests.

Managers are not expected to determine whether an individual is legally permitted to carry a firearm.

---

# Emergency Situations

If a weapon is displayed, threatened, or used:

1. Prioritize life safety.
2. Create distance when possible.
3. Contact emergency services.
4. Follow workplace violence procedures.
5. Cooperate with law enforcement.

---

# Compliance

This policy is intended to comply with Washington State law. Where applicable law differs from company policy, the law shall govern.

---

# Guiding Principle

Respect the law.

Protect our people.

Respond to behavior, not assumptions.

Safety remains our highest priority.
