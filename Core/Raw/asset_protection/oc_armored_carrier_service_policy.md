# ORDINA

## Armored Carrier Service Policy

### Summary

Armored carrier services provide secure transportation of company funds, change orders, and bank deposits.

Proper preparation, documentation, and accountability ensure the security of company assets and minimize cash handling risks.

---

# Purpose

Establish standards for armored carrier services, change orders, deposits, and cash transportation.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations utilizing armored carrier services.

---

# Service Windows

Armored carrier service windows may vary by location.

Management should:

- Know the scheduled service window.
- Prepare all materials before arrival.
- Ensure authorized personnel are available.

---

# Carrier Waiting Time

Armored carriers operate on limited schedules.

To avoid service delays:

- Prepare all deposits before arrival.
- Prepare change order payments in advance.
- Have required documentation ready.

Failure to prepare may result in additional service charges.

---

# Deposit Preparation

All deposits must:

- Be sealed properly.
- Be accurately counted.
- Include required documentation.
- Be secured inside the safe until pickup.

Manual deposits and change order payments must be prepared before the pickup window begins.

---

# Documentation

Pickup logs must include:

- Date.
- Deposit amount.
- Bag number.
- Change order information.
- Carrier signature.

All entries must be legible.

Documentation should be retained according to company record retention standards.

---

# Change Orders

Change orders should:

- Be submitted before the required cutoff time.
- Be verified upon delivery.
- Be documented properly.

Management is responsible for confirming receipt and accuracy.

---

# Change Fund Accountability

Change funds operate on an even exchange principle.

This means:

- Funds received must be paid according to established procedures.
- Outstanding balances should not remain unresolved.
- Late payments must be investigated.

---

# Missed Pickups

If a carrier fails to arrive:

1. Verify the scheduled service window has ended.
2. Contact the carrier.
3. Notify leadership if necessary.
4. Document the incident.

---

# Reporting Issues

Management should report:

- Missed pickups.
- Shortages.
- Delivery discrepancies.
- Damaged deposits.
- Service issues.

Prompt reporting improves resolution.

---

# Service Changes

Changes to armored services, including:

- Service cancellations.
- Schedule changes.
- Store closures.
- Remodels.
- Temporary suspensions.

must be approved through company leadership.

Advance notice may be required.

---

# Holiday Service

Holiday pickups may require:

- Advance scheduling.
- Leadership approval.
- Additional service fees.

Management should plan accordingly.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Preparing deposits.
- Securing funds.
- Maintaining records.
- Verifying deliveries.
- Reporting discrepancies.
- Protecting company assets.

---

# Prohibited Practices

Associates may not:

- Leave deposits unsecured.
- Delay required documentation.
- Release deposits without verification.
- Ignore shortages or discrepancies.
- Share safe access credentials.
- Transport company funds without authorization.

---

# Guiding Principle

Cash accountability begins before the carrier arrives.

Preparation, documentation, and verification protect both the associate and the business.
