# ORDINA

## AP-1 Asset Protection Program

### Summary

Asset Protection exists to protect people, property, cash, equipment, information, and business continuity.

Ordina's goal is to prevent loss, reduce risk, and create a safe environment for associates, guests, vendors, and leaders.

Asset Protection is not one department. It is a shared responsibility.

---

# Mission

Protect our people, guests, assets, and operations through safety, integrity, communication, partnership, and continuous improvement.

---

# Core Values

## Safety

People come first.

## Integrity

We protect company assets and act honestly.

## Communication

Fast communication prevents small issues from becoming major incidents.

## Partnership

Security works best when operations, leadership, and support teams work together.

## Improvement

We continuously improve training, systems, and procedures.

---

# Purpose

The Asset Protection Program exists to:

- Protect associates and guests.
- Prevent crime, theft, fraud, and loss.
- Reduce workplace violence risks.
- Improve cash handling accountability.
- Protect facilities and equipment.
- Support emergency readiness.
- Strengthen operational discipline.

---

# Scope

This program applies to all Ordina:

- Associates.
- Managers.
- Area leaders.
- Vendors.
- Contractors.
- Visitors on company property.

---

# Asset Protection Responsibilities

Asset Protection standards support operations by providing:

- Security procedures.
- Cash handling controls.
- Fraud prevention standards.
- Robbery and burglary prevention.
- Workplace violence prevention.
- Audit standards.
- Training resources.
- Risk assessments.
- Incident response guidance.

---

# Associate Responsibilities

Associates are expected to:

- Follow cash handling procedures.
- Keep doors, keys, and codes secure.
- Report suspicious activity.
- Protect company property.
- Follow safety and security procedures.
- Maintain awareness of surroundings.
- Escalate concerns to management.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

Leaders are responsible for:

- Training associates.
- Enforcing standards consistently.
- Reviewing security risks.
- Maintaining security systems.
- Completing audits and follow-ups.
- Responding to incidents.
- Protecting associates and guests.
- Creating a culture of accountability.

---

# Security Principles

Ordina uses layered security to reduce risk.

This includes:

- Clean exterior areas.
- Proper lighting.
- CCTV systems.
- Alarm systems.
- Door and key control.
- Safe controls.
- Cash pulls.
- Daily deposits.
- Associate awareness.

No single security tool is enough. Strong security comes from consistent execution.

---

# Training Program

Asset Protection training may include:

- Cash handling.
- Internal theft prevention.
- Burglary prevention.
- Robbery response.
- Scam prevention.
- Workplace violence prevention.
- De-escalation.
- Lock and key management.
- Safe access.
- Audit readiness.

Training should be practical, repeated, and connected to real store risks.

---

# Audits and Risk Reviews

Asset Protection audits help identify:

- Cash handling risks.
- Security gaps.
- Fraud indicators.
- Training needs.
- Equipment issues.
- Policy violations.

Audit findings should lead to coaching, corrective action, or process improvement.

---

# Incident Response

Managers must respond immediately to:

- Robbery.
- Burglary.
- Workplace violence.
- Threats.
- Fraud.
- Scams.
- Property damage.
- Security system failures.
- Cash discrepancies.

People must always be protected first.

---

# Accountability

Failure to follow Asset Protection standards may result in corrective action, up to and including termination.

Serious violations may include:

- Theft.
- Fraud.
- Sharing codes.
- Sharing keys.
- Falsifying records.
- Ignoring safety concerns.
- Mishandling cash.
- Concealing losses.

---

# Guiding Principle

Protect people.

Protect assets.

Protect the business.

Security is built through daily habits, strong leadership, and clear accountability.
