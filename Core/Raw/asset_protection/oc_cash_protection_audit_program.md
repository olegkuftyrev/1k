# ORDINA

## Cash Protection Audit Program

### Summary

The purpose of the Cash Protection Audit is to evaluate how effectively each location protects company assets, cash, associates, and operational systems.

The audit measures daily execution, accountability, and compliance with company standards.

The objective is prevention, accountability, and continuous improvement.

---

# Purpose

The Cash Protection Audit provides an objective assessment of:

- Cash handling practices.
- Asset protection.
- Security systems.
- Associate accountability.
- Physical security.
- Operational controls.

The audit is designed to identify risks before losses occur.

---

# Audit Categories

The audit evaluates the following areas:

### Fraud Prevention

- Register accountability.
- Transaction reviews.
- Unusual activity.
- Manager oversight.
- Exception reporting.

### Asset Protection

- Gift card controls.
- Cash controls.
- Change order accountability.
- Store property protection.

### Security Equipment

- Safe operation.
- CCTV functionality.
- Alarm systems.
- Time delay systems.
- Access controls.

### Security Management

- Back door security.
- Exterior conditions.
- Lighting.
- Dumpster security.
- Key management.

### Cash Controls

- Cash pulls.
- Register balances.
- Safe counts.
- Deposit procedures.
- Cash accountability.

---

# Management Review

Managers should periodically review:

- Transactions.
- CCTV footage.
- Exception reports.
- Cash trends.
- Security trends.

Reviews should focus on identifying:

- Unusual activity.
- Operational risks.
- Training opportunities.
- Security concerns.

---

# Fraud Prevention

Managers should verify that:

- Transactions match operational activity.
- Discounts are authorized.
- Refunds are legitimate.
- Cash pulls are completed.
- Register activity is accurate.

Unusual activity should be investigated promptly.

---

# Security Equipment

Management should verify:

- Cameras are operational.
- Safe functions properly.
- Time delays operate correctly.
- Alarm systems work.
- Access users are current.

Security equipment failures should be addressed immediately.

---

# Exterior Security

Managers should inspect:

- Lighting.
- Parking areas.
- Exterior visibility.
- Graffiti.
- Dumpster areas.
- Building condition.

Exterior conditions directly affect safety and security.

---

# Cash Accountability

Locations should maintain:

- Proper drawer limits.
- Timely cash pulls.
- Accurate safe counts.
- Daily deposits.
- Register accountability.

Cash exposure should remain as low as possible.

---

# Follow-Up Actions

Audit findings should result in:

- Coaching.
- Training.
- Corrective actions.
- Equipment repairs.
- Process improvements.

The purpose of the audit is improvement, not punishment.

---

# Manager Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Maintaining compliance.
- Reviewing trends.
- Investigating exceptions.
- Coaching associates.
- Protecting company assets.

---

# Guiding Principles

Protect cash.

Protect people.

Protect assets.

Inspect what you expect.

Accountability prevents loss.
