# ORDINA

## Workplace Violence Prevention Policy

### Summary

Ordina is committed to providing a workplace free from violence, threats, intimidation, harassment, and disruptive behavior.

Violence, threats, or acts of aggression toward associates, guests, vendors, contractors, or visitors will not be tolerated.

Every associate has the right to work in a safe environment.

---

# Purpose

Establish standards for preventing, reporting, investigating, and responding to workplace violence.

This policy applies to:

- Associates.
- Managers.
- Contractors.
- Temporary employees.
- Vendors.
- Visitors.
- Guests while on company property.

---

# Zero Tolerance Standard

Ordina maintains a zero-tolerance policy for:

- Physical violence.
- Threats of violence.
- Intimidation.
- Harassment involving threats.
- Aggressive behavior.
- Stalking.
- Bullying.
- Possession or use of weapons prohibited by law or company policy.
- Any behavior that causes others to fear for their safety.

Threats may be verbal, written, electronic, or physical.

---

# Examples of Prohibited Conduct

Examples include:

- Threatening another person.
- Fighting.
- Attempting to injure someone.
- Intimidation.
- Aggressive confrontation.
- Throwing objects.
- Verbal threats.
- Harassing messages.
- Stalking behavior.
- Damaging property to intimidate others.
- Bringing violence into the workplace.

These examples are not all-inclusive.

---

# Reporting Requirements

Associates must immediately report:

- Threats.
- Violent behavior.
- Harassment involving safety concerns.
- Intimidation.
- Suspicious behavior.
- Aggressive guests.
- Domestic situations that may affect the workplace.

Reports should be made to:

1. The Manager or Person in Charge.
2. Human Resources.
3. Senior leadership.
4. Emergency services if immediate danger exists.

---

# Emergency Situations

Call emergency services immediately if:

- A weapon is present.
- Violence is occurring.
- Someone is injured.
- A threat appears immediate.
- Associates or guests are in danger.

Protecting people is the first priority.

---

# Investigation Process

Reported incidents will be:

- Investigated promptly.
- Handled confidentially when possible.
- Reviewed objectively.
- Documented appropriately.

Leadership will determine appropriate corrective actions.

---

# Non-Retaliation

Associates who report concerns in good faith will not face retaliation.

Retaliation against any associate who reports safety concerns is prohibited.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Taking all reports seriously.
- Protecting associates.
- Reporting incidents immediately.
- Maintaining confidentiality.
- Coordinating investigations.
- Contacting law enforcement when necessary.
- Supporting affected associates.

---

# De-Escalation

Associates should attempt to:

- Remain calm.
- Speak respectfully.
- Create space.
- Reduce tension.
- Request assistance.

Associates should never place themselves in danger while attempting to de-escalate a situation.

---

# Corrective Action

Violations of this policy may result in:

- Coaching.
- Written warnings.
- Final warnings.
- Suspension.
- Termination.
- Law enforcement involvement.

The level of action depends on the severity of the conduct.

---

# Associate Responsibility

Every associate shares responsibility for:

- Maintaining a respectful workplace.
- Reporting concerns.
- Supporting coworkers.
- Following safety procedures.
- Promoting a culture of safety.

---

# Guiding Principle

People deserve to feel safe at work.

Violence, threats, and intimidation have no place at Ordina.
