# ORDINA

## Restaurant Security Policy

### Summary

Security is everyone's responsibility.

Proper security practices protect our associates, guests, cash, equipment, and company assets. Every leader is responsible for maintaining a secure restaurant and immediately addressing security risks.

Failure to follow security procedures may place associates and the business at risk.

---

# Purpose

Establish minimum security standards for all Ordina locations.

This policy applies to all managers, leaders, and associates.

---

# Building Security

### 1. Rear Door Security

Rear doors must remain locked at all times.

Rear entrances should only be used for approved operational purposes and should be minimized during evening hours.

---

### 2. Opening and Closing Security

All exterior doors must remain locked:

- Before opening.
- After closing.
- During final cash counts.
- Whenever the restaurant is not operating.

Doors should not be opened for unauthorized individuals.

---

# Key Control

### 3. Return of Keys

All company keys, access devices, and security equipment must be returned immediately upon:

- Transfer.
- Separation.
- Termination.
- Change of position.

---

### 4. No Sharing of Keys or Codes

Associates may not share:

- Building keys.
- Safe combinations.
- Alarm codes.
- Security credentials.
- Access codes.

Each associate is responsible for their assigned access.

---

### 5. Remove Inactive Access

Security access must be removed immediately when an associate:

- Transfers.
- Separates from employment.
- Changes positions.
- No longer requires access.

Unused codes create unnecessary risk.

---

# Surveillance Systems

### 6. Camera Systems

Security cameras must:

- Operate properly.
- Record continuously.
- Be inspected regularly.

Any malfunction must be reported immediately.

---

# Safe Security

### 7. Safe Procedures

Safe doors must remain closed and secured.

Managers should:

- Lock the safe immediately after use.
- Never leave the safe unattended.
- Never walk away from an open safe.

---

### 8. Cash Storage

The majority of restaurant funds should remain secured inside the protected section of the safe.

Limited operational funds may remain available for business needs.

Cash exposure should always be minimized.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Protecting associates.
- Securing company assets.
- Monitoring security systems.
- Controlling access.
- Verifying cash security.
- Responding to security concerns.

---

# Associate Responsibilities

Associates are expected to:

- Follow security procedures.
- Report suspicious activity.
- Protect company property.
- Maintain confidentiality of security information.
- Immediately report lost keys or compromised codes.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Sharing alarm codes.
- Sharing safe combinations.
- Sharing keys.
- Leaving doors unlocked.
- Leaving the safe open.
- Allowing unauthorized access.
- Failing to remove inactive codes.
- Ignoring security equipment failures.
- Leaving cash unsecured.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination.

---

# Guiding Principle

Protect people first.

Protect company assets second.

Security standards must be followed every day, not only during emergencies.
