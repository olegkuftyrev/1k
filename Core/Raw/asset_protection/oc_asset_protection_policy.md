# ORDINA

# Asset Protection Policy

### Mission

Protect our people, guests, assets, and operations through safety, accountability, training, and continuous improvement.

Our goal is to prevent loss, reduce risk, and create a secure environment where associates and guests can work and dine safely.

---

# Our Values

### Safety

People always come first.

### Integrity

We act honestly and protect company resources.

### Communication

Information shared quickly prevents incidents.

### Partnership

Security is everyone's responsibility.

### Improvement

We continuously evolve our processes, training, and technology.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Asset Protection is to:

- Protect associates and guests.
- Prevent loss and fraud.
- Reduce security risks.
- Support operational excellence.
- Improve workplace safety.
- Protect company assets.

This policy applies to all Ordina associates and leaders.

---

# Asset Protection Responsibilities

Asset Protection supports operations through:

- Security guidance.
- Cash handling standards.
- Fraud prevention.
- Security training.
- Risk assessments.
- Incident response.
- Investigations.
- Operational audits.
- Emergency planning.

Security is a partnership between leadership and operations.

---

# General Security Principles

The purpose of all security procedures is:

1. Protect people.
2. Prevent violence.
3. Reduce loss.
4. Protect company property.
5. Support safe operations.

Every associate shares responsibility for maintaining a secure workplace.

---

# Associate Responsibilities

Associates are expected to:

- Follow all cash handling procedures.
- Protect company property.
- Report suspicious activity.
- Follow security policies.
- Protect confidential information.
- Maintain awareness of their surroundings.

Strong daily habits prevent many incidents.

---

# Security Awareness

Criminal activity often targets:

- Large amounts of visible cash.
- Poor lighting.
- Weak security.
- Easy access points.
- Predictable routines.

Managers should reduce opportunities through:

- Cash pulls.
- Locked doors.
- Proper lighting.
- Camera systems.
- Alarm systems.
- Security awareness.

---

# Layered Security

Effective security uses multiple layers:

- Exterior lighting.
- Landscaping visibility.
- Access control.
- Security cameras.
- Alarm systems.
- Cash controls.
- Safe procedures.
- Leadership accountability.

No single security measure is sufficient.

---

# Security Systems

Ordina locations should maintain:

- CCTV systems.
- Alarm systems.
- Controlled access.
- Safe security.
- Cash management systems.
- Physical security measures.

Any malfunction should be reported immediately.

---

# Training and Development

Security training helps associates respond confidently during difficult situations.

Training topics may include:

- Burglary prevention.
- Robbery response.
- Cash handling.
- Internal theft prevention.
- Scam awareness.
- Workplace violence prevention.
- Emergency response.
- Security assessments.
- De-escalation techniques.

Training should occur regularly.

---

# Risk Assessments

Leadership should periodically evaluate:

- Physical security.
- Cash controls.
- Lighting.
- Camera coverage.
- Crime trends.
- Operational vulnerabilities.

Corrective actions should be implemented when risks are identified.

---

# Incident Response

Managers are responsible for:

- Protecting associates and guests.
- Responding to emergencies.
- Preserving evidence.
- Reporting incidents.
- Supporting affected associates.
- Coordinating recovery efforts.

---

# Accountability

Failure to follow security, cash handling, or asset protection procedures may result in corrective action.

Repeated violations may result in further disciplinary action.

---

# Leadership Commitment

Leaders are expected to:

- Set the example.
- Enforce standards consistently.
- Train associates.
- Investigate concerns.
- Protect people.
- Promote accountability.

Security culture begins with leadership.

---

# Guiding Principle

Protect people.

Protect the business.

Protect the future.

Asset Protection is not a department.

It is a responsibility shared by every associate.
