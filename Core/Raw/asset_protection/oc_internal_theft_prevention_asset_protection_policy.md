# ORDINA

## Internal Theft Prevention & Asset Protection Policy

### Summary

Asset protection is everyone's responsibility.

Theft, fraud, misuse of company resources, and dishonesty impact our associates, guests, and business performance. Ordina is committed to creating a culture of accountability, trust, and operational discipline.

The best theft prevention system is strong leadership, clear expectations, and consistent execution.

---

# Purpose

Establish standards that protect company assets, cash, inventory, equipment, time, and information.

This policy applies to all associates and leaders.

---

# Definitions

### Theft

The unauthorized use, removal, or misuse of company assets.

### Theft of Time

Receiving pay for time not worked, falsifying time records, or intentionally misrepresenting hours worked.

### Theft of Property

Taking food, products, supplies, equipment, or company property without authorization.

---

# Common Forms of Internal Theft

The following activities are prohibited:

- Under-ringing sales.
- Voiding completed transactions.
- False refunds.
- Unauthorized discounts.
- Excess food production for personal use.
- Taking food or products without payment.
- Providing free products to friends or family.
- False paid-outs.
- Cash shortages concealed through adjustments.
- Improper use of manager functions.
- Timecard manipulation.
- Misuse of gift cards or promotional offers.

---

# Early Warning Signs

Leaders should remain aware of:

- Repeated cash shortages.
- Excessive refunds or voids.
- Frequent policy violations.
- Unauthorized back door use.
- Large inventory variances.
- Excessive complaints.
- Associates handling their own transactions.
- Off-duty loitering.
- Unusual customer behavior involving specific associates.

Warning signs do not establish guilt but should be reviewed appropriately.

---

# Prevention Standards

## Hiring

Leaders should:

- Follow hiring standards.
- Conduct thorough interviews.
- Verify qualifications.
- Observe new associates closely.

---

## Accountability Culture

Managers should:

- Apply rules consistently.
- Maintain professional standards.
- Treat associates fairly.
- Set realistic expectations.
- Recognize strong performance.
- Address issues promptly.

Opportunity creates theft. Accountability reduces opportunity.

---

# Cash Controls

Associates must:

- Count starting banks.
- Use only their assigned register.
- Complete one transaction at a time.
- Count change back to guests.
- Provide receipts.
- Close drawers after each transaction.
- Complete cash pulls immediately.
- Follow counterfeit bill procedures.

Unauthorized use of discounts, voids, refunds, or register functions is prohibited.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

Managers must:

- Verify safe counts.
- Perform daily cash audits.
- Review cashier activity.
- Monitor discounts and refunds.
- Maintain security equipment.
- Conduct random audits.
- Verify deposits and cash counts.

---

# Inventory Controls

Leaders should:

- Conduct inventory regularly.
- Perform spot checks.
- Review variances.
- Monitor waste.
- Control employee meals.
- Secure dumpsters.
- Protect storage areas.
- Investigate unusual shortages.

Large inventory variances require immediate review.

---

# Investigations

Associates should never conduct their own investigations.

Suspected theft should be reported to leadership or asset protection.

False accusations can cause significant harm and should be avoided.

---

# Prohibited Conduct

The following actions may result in corrective action up to and including termination:

- Theft.
- Fraud.
- Falsification of records.
- Time theft.
- Unauthorized discounts.
- Cash manipulation.
- Inventory theft.
- Sharing security credentials.
- Concealing losses.
- Assisting others in theft.

---

# Guiding Principle

Trust is earned through accountability.

Protecting company assets protects our associates, our guests, and our future.
