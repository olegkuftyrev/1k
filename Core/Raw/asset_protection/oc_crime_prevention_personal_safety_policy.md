# ORDINA

## Crime Prevention & Personal Safety Policy

### Summary

Crime prevention begins with awareness.

Associates who remain observant, follow security procedures, and report suspicious activity help create a safer environment for guests, associates, and the community.

The goal of crime prevention is to reduce opportunities for criminal activity while protecting people first.

---

# Purpose

Establish daily practices that improve safety, reduce criminal activity, and protect associates, guests, and company assets.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Situational Awareness

Associates should remain aware of their surroundings while performing daily duties, including:

- Taking out trash.
- Exterior cleaning.
- Parking lot inspections.
- Opening procedures.
- Closing procedures.
- Property maintenance.

Unusual activity should be reported immediately.

---

# Vandalism

If vandalism is observed:

1. Do not intervene.
2. Notify the Person in Charge.
3. Contact law enforcement if necessary.
4. Document damages safely.

Personal safety always takes priority over property.

---

# Graffiti Removal

Graffiti should be removed as quickly as possible.

Prompt removal:

- Reduces repeat vandalism.
- Improves store appearance.
- Demonstrates community pride.
- Discourages additional criminal activity.

---

# Community Partnerships

Strong relationships improve safety.

Managers are encouraged to:

- Build relationships with neighboring businesses.
- Participate in local business groups.
- Maintain positive community relationships.
- Support local safety initiatives.

Communities often help protect businesses they trust.

---

# Law Enforcement Relationships

Managers are encouraged to maintain professional relationships with local law enforcement and public safety agencies.

Visible law enforcement presence may improve safety and deter criminal activity.

---

# Safe Security

Time-delay and security features should remain active and operational.

Managers should:

- Verify safe functions.
- Follow cash handling procedures.
- Minimize cash exposure.
- Maintain security signage when appropriate.

---

# Exterior Safety

Associates should remain alert around:

- Parking lots.
- Rear entrances.
- Side entrances.
- Exterior walkways.
- Trash areas.

Suspicious activity should be reported immediately.

---

# Traveling After Dark

When possible:

- Leave in pairs or groups.
- Park in well-lit areas.
- Request assistance if uncomfortable.
- Notify management of safety concerns.

No associate should feel unsafe leaving work.

---

# Rear Door Security

Rear doors must remain locked.

Rear entrances:

- Should not remain propped open.
- Should not be used unnecessarily.
- Should be limited during evening hours.

Access should always be controlled.

---

# Banking and Cash Transportation

Managers should avoid transporting company funds whenever possible.

Approved cash handling and deposit procedures should always be followed.

Cash exposure should be minimized.

---

# Closing Procedures

As business slows:

- Reduce open registers.
- Secure unused workstations.
- Minimize cash exposure.
- Verify doors are secured.
- Increase awareness after dark.

Management should maintain heightened awareness during late operating hours.

---

# Associate Safety

Associates should:

- Report suspicious activity.
- Trust their instincts.
- Seek assistance when needed.
- Avoid unnecessary risks.

Safety concerns should never be ignored.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Maintaining security standards.
- Protecting associates.
- Monitoring risks.
- Communicating concerns.
- Supporting safe operations.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Confronting criminals.
- Investigating suspicious individuals alone.
- Leaving doors unsecured.
- Leaving cash exposed.
- Ignoring suspicious activity.
- Placing associates in unsafe situations.

---

# Guiding Principle

Stay aware.

Stay safe.

Protect people first.
