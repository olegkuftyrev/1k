# ORDINA

## Managing Aggressive Guest Behavior

### Summary

Ordina is committed to providing a safe and respectful environment for guests and associates.

Most guest concerns can be resolved through calm communication, active listening, and professional leadership. Associates should always prioritize safety while working to resolve difficult situations.

---

# Purpose

Provide associates and managers with guidance for responding to upset, angry, or disruptive guests.

The goal is to:

- Protect associates and guests.
- Reduce conflict.
- Resolve concerns professionally.
- Maintain a safe environment.

---

# 1. Remain Calm

Your behavior influences the situation.

Managers and associates should:

- Speak calmly.
- Maintain professional body language.
- Control emotions.
- Avoid raising their voice.

Remaining calm often helps others become calm.

---

# 2. Listen First

Allow the guest to explain the concern.

- Introduce yourself.
- Ask the guest's name when appropriate.
- Listen without interruption.
- Maintain appropriate eye contact.
- Focus on understanding the issue.

Guests frequently become less upset when they feel heard.

---

# 3. Show Understanding

Acknowledge the guest's concern.

Examples:

> "I understand why you're frustrated."

> "Thank you for explaining the situation."

> "Let me make sure I understand."

Acknowledging emotions does not mean admitting fault.

---

# 4. Avoid Arguments

Associates should never:

- Argue with guests.
- Raise their voice.
- Use sarcasm.
- Respond emotionally.
- Take comments personally.

Professionalism must always be maintained.

---

# 5. Explain Policies Clearly

If necessary, calmly explain:

- Company policies.
- Available options.
- Possible solutions.
- Expectations for behavior.

Use simple, respectful language.

---

# 6. Offer Solutions

Whenever possible, provide reasonable options.

Examples:

- Replacement products.
- Alternative seating.
- Manager assistance.
- Refund procedures.
- Additional support.

People cooperate more readily when solutions are available.

---

# 7. Set Expectations

If inappropriate behavior continues, clearly communicate expectations.

Examples:

> "I want to help you, but I need us to speak respectfully."

> "I need you to lower your voice so we can continue the conversation."

Remain professional at all times.

---

# 8. Escalate When Necessary

Management should seek assistance if:

- Threats are made.
- Aggressive behavior continues.
- Property damage occurs.
- Violence is threatened.
- Safety becomes a concern.

Management may:

- Involve another manager.
- Ask the guest to leave.
- Contact security.
- Contact law enforcement.

---

# Immediate Safety Concerns

If a guest:

- Makes threats.
- Displays a weapon.
- Becomes physically aggressive.
- Attempts violence.

Associates should:

1. Prioritize safety.
2. Create distance.
3. Protect guests and associates.
4. Contact emergency services.
5. Follow workplace violence procedures.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Leading calmly.
- Supporting associates.
- Protecting guests.
- Making safety decisions.
- Documenting serious incidents.

---

# Guiding Principle

Listen first.

Stay calm.

Protect people.

Resolve the issue whenever possible.

When safety becomes a concern, seek help immediately.
