# ORDINA

## Robbery Response Policy

### Summary

The safety of our guests and associates is the highest priority during a robbery.

Property, cash, and merchandise can be replaced. Human safety cannot.

Associates should remain calm, comply with demands, and focus on ending the incident as quickly and safely as possible.

---

# Purpose

Provide clear guidance for associates and managers during and after a robbery.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# During a Robbery

The primary objective is to protect guests and associates.

Associates should focus on safely ending the incident and avoiding actions that increase risk.

---

# The Four C's

## 1. Stay Calm

- Remain calm.
- Avoid sudden movements.
- Do not argue.
- Do not stare at the suspect.
- Follow instructions carefully.

---

## 2. Comply

- Surrender requested cash or merchandise.
- Do not resist.
- Do not attempt to negotiate.
- Do not attempt to stop the suspect.

Property can be replaced.

---

## 3. Cooperate

- Listen carefully.
- Respond clearly.
- Move slowly.
- Complete requests safely.

---

## 4. Communicate

- Speak calmly.
- Explain your actions before moving.
- Identify yourself as the manager or person in charge if appropriate.
- Let the suspect know you intend to cooperate.

---

# Prohibited Actions During a Robbery

Associates must never:

- Chase the suspect.
- Physically resist.
- Attempt to disarm the suspect.
- Argue with the suspect.
- Block exits.
- Activate alarms if it increases danger.
- Make sudden movements.

The objective is to end the incident safely.

---

# After the Robbery

## Secure the Restaurant

- Lock exterior doors if safe.
- Prevent unauthorized entry.
- Protect guests and associates.

---

## Call Emergency Services

Call 911 immediately after the suspect leaves.

Provide:

- Location.
- Description of the suspect.
- Direction of travel.
- Vehicle information if known.

---

## Protect People

Managers should:

- Account for all associates.
- Account for guests.
- Check for injuries.
- Provide support and reassurance.

---

## Preserve Information

Witnesses should independently document:

- Physical description.
- Clothing.
- Tattoos or distinguishing features.
- Direction of travel.
- Statements made during the incident.

Witnesses should avoid discussing details with one another before speaking with law enforcement.

---

# Video and Evidence

Managers should:

- Preserve CCTV footage.
- Protect evidence.
- Avoid touching abandoned items.
- Cooperate with law enforcement.

---

# Associate Support

Robberies can be traumatic events.

Associates should be encouraged to:

- Speak with management.
- Utilize available support resources.
- Request time to recover if necessary.

---

# Guest Injuries

If any guest or associate is injured:

- Provide medical assistance.
- Contact emergency services.
- Complete required incident documentation.
- Report the incident to leadership.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Protecting people.
- Contacting emergency services.
- Preserving evidence.
- Supporting associates.
- Coordinating with leadership.
- Reviewing security procedures after the incident.

---

# Post-Incident Review

After the event, leadership should review:

- Security procedures.
- Camera coverage.
- Cash handling practices.
- Physical security.
- Training opportunities.

Additional security measures may be implemented when necessary.

---

# Guiding Principle

People first.

Cash, equipment, and merchandise can be replaced. The safety of our guests and associates is always the highest priority.
