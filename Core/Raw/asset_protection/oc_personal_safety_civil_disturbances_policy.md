# ORDINA

## Personal Safety During Civil Disturbances

### Summary

The safety of our associates is always the highest priority.

During protests, demonstrations, civil disturbances, or other public emergencies, associates should avoid unnecessary risks, remain aware of their surroundings, and follow instructions from local authorities and company leadership.

No business objective is more important than personal safety.

---

# Purpose

Provide guidance for associates traveling to, from, or working during demonstrations, protests, or civil disturbances.

This policy applies to all Ordina associates.

---

# Personal Safety While Walking

Associates should:

- Follow local curfews and emergency orders.
- Travel with another person whenever possible.
- Inform family, friends, or management of their location.
- Remain aware of their surroundings.
- Avoid unnecessary risks.
- Stay calm and make decisions early when danger is present.

Associates should remain neutral and avoid participating in demonstrations while representing the company.

---

# If Caught in a Crowd

If caught in a large crowd:

- Move toward the outer edges when possible.
- Seek shelter inside a nearby building if safe.
- Avoid moving against the flow of the crowd.
- Protect breathing space around your chest.

If children are present, keep them close and protected.

---

# If Knocked Down

If knocked to the ground:

- Protect your head and neck.
- Curl into a protective position.
- Remain covered until it is safe to move.

---

# If Violence Occurs

If violence or gunfire occurs:

- Seek immediate cover.
- Move away from danger if safe.
- Protect your head and neck.
- Follow instructions from emergency responders.

Personal safety always takes priority.

---

# Vehicle Safety

Associates should never drive into a crowd.

If a demonstration blocks the roadway:

- Turn around if possible.
- Leave the area safely.
- Avoid confrontation.
- Seek shelter if necessary.

If remaining inside the vehicle:

- Lock the doors.
- Turn off the engine if appropriate.
- Stay calm.
- Avoid aggressive behavior.

---

# Personal Appearance

During periods of civil unrest or emergency conditions, associates should:

- Avoid wearing expensive jewelry.
- Avoid displaying valuable items.
- Dress practically and safely.
- Wear comfortable footwear if travel conditions may change.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Monitoring local conditions.
- Communicating safety concerns.
- Adjusting schedules if necessary.
- Supporting associates affected by unsafe conditions.
- Providing transportation assistance when appropriate.

---

# Associate Responsibilities

Associates are encouraged to:

- Report unsafe conditions.
- Follow local emergency instructions.
- Communicate travel concerns.
- Prioritize personal safety.

No associate should feel pressured to place themselves in an unsafe situation.

---

# Guiding Principle

Protect yourself first.

Your safety is more important than schedules, sales, or operations.
