# ORDINA

## Civil Disturbance & Protest Security Policy

### Summary

The safety of our guests and associates is the highest priority during protests, demonstrations, or civil disturbances.

Operations may continue only when conditions remain safe. When safety is threatened, leadership must protect people first, secure company assets second, and restore operations only when conditions allow.

---

# Purpose

Provide guidance for preparing, responding, and recovering from protests, demonstrations, civil unrest, or similar public disturbances.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Leadership Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Monitoring local conditions.
- Communicating with leadership.
- Protecting associates and guests.
- Securing company assets.
- Making safety decisions.
- Following guidance from authorities.

---

# Pre-Event Preparation

Before potential demonstrations or disturbances:

### Building Security

- Ensure all building keys are immediately available.
- Verify that doors and locks function properly.
- Test alarm systems.
- Verify CCTV systems are operating.

### Exterior Safety

- Remove combustible materials from exterior areas.
- Remove cardboard, pallets, and storage items from the building exterior.
- Secure dumpster areas.
- Remove exterior trash containers if they may become safety hazards.

### Cash Security

- Conduct regular cash pulls.
- Minimize cash in registers.
- Secure all funds in the safe.
- Increase deposit frequency if necessary.

---

# Associate Safety

Management should determine whether outdoor work activities remain safe.

If conditions become unsafe:

- Stop outdoor activities.
- Move associates indoors.
- Restrict access to exterior areas.

Transportation assistance or escorts may be considered when conditions warrant.

---

# Active Disturbance Response

If violence, vandalism, or dangerous conditions occur:

1. Remain calm.
2. Move associates to the safest area available.
3. Identify emergency exits.
4. Protect guests and associates.
5. Contact emergency services if necessary.

People always take priority over property.

---

# Evacuation

If conditions require evacuation:

- Follow emergency procedures.
- Direct guests and associates away from danger.
- Use safe evacuation routes.
- Move away from the source of the disturbance.

Evacuations should only occur when it is safe to do so.

---

# Temporary Closure

Restaurants may temporarily close when:

- Authorities issue instructions.
- Leadership directs closure.
- Safety conditions deteriorate.
- Operations cannot continue safely.

All normal closing procedures should be followed when conditions permit.

---

# Security Measures

Depending on local conditions, management may consider:

- Security personnel.
- Off-duty law enforcement.
- Boarding services.
- Additional patrols.
- Transportation assistance for associates.

Security decisions should be based on risk assessments.

---

# Property Damage

If vandalism or damage occurs:

- Notify law enforcement.
- Photograph damages.
- Document losses.
- Preserve evidence.
- Notify leadership.

Broken glass and debris must be removed safely.

Contaminated food or beverages must be discarded.

---

# Reopening

Operations may resume only when:

- The area is safe.
- Authorities permit operations.
- The building is secure.
- Damages have been addressed.
- Food safety risks have been eliminated.

---

# Management Visits

If conditions remain unstable:

- Visits should occur during daylight hours.
- Managers should avoid unnecessary risks.
- Safety conditions should be evaluated before entering the property.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Confronting demonstrators.
- Putting associates in unsafe situations.
- Remaining open during dangerous conditions.
- Ignoring instructions from authorities.
- Leaving excess cash unsecured.
- Entering unsafe buildings.
- Delaying emergency reporting.

---

# Guiding Principle

Protect people first.

Buildings, equipment, and property can be repaired or replaced. The safety of our guests and associates cannot.
