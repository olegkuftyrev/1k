# ORDINA

## Temporary Store Closure Policy

### Summary

The safety of our associates and guests is the highest priority during protests, civil disturbances, or public emergencies.

When conditions outside the restaurant create a safety risk, leadership may temporarily close the location until it is safe to resume operations.

No business objective is more important than human safety.

---

# Purpose

Provide clear procedures for temporarily closing and reopening a restaurant due to protests, civil unrest, public disturbances, or other safety concerns.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Closure Criteria

A temporary closure may be necessary when:

- Protests or demonstrations are expected near the restaurant.
- Civil unrest may impact associate or guest safety.
- Law enforcement or local authorities issue warnings.
- Leadership determines operating conditions are unsafe.
- Property damage or security risks are likely.

Safety conditions should be evaluated continuously.

---

# Pre-Closure Actions

Management should:

- Verify associate contact information.
- Establish emergency communication methods.
- Identify a team rally point or communication plan.
- Monitor local news and public safety information.
- Notify leadership of developing conditions.

---

# Building Protection

When necessary, management may arrange:

- Temporary window protection.
- Security services.
- Additional patrols.
- Exterior property protection.

Protective measures should only be implemented when approved by leadership.

---

# Associate Communication

Managers must:

- Notify scheduled associates of the closure.
- Communicate expected updates.
- Provide reopening information when available.
- Confirm associate safety whenever possible.

---

# Closing Procedures

When closing the restaurant:

- Follow standard closing procedures.
- Secure all cash in the safe.
- Complete required deposits when possible.
- Secure doors and building access.
- Shut down operations safely.

All cash handling procedures remain in effect during emergency closures.

---

# Online Orders and Deliveries

Management should:

- Suspend online ordering if necessary.
- Review scheduled catering orders.
- Coordinate with nearby locations when possible.
- Communicate with delivery providers.

Future deliveries should be adjusted when operations are suspended.

---

# Store Access During Closure

After closure:

- Associates may not enter the building without authorization.
- Management should avoid unnecessary visits.
- Access should occur only when conditions are safe.

If the building has been secured or boarded, occupancy should be limited.

---

# Reopening Requirements

The restaurant may reopen only when:

- Leadership confirms the area is safe.
- Law enforcement restrictions have been lifted.
- The building is secure.
- Property damage has been addressed.
- Utilities and operations are functioning properly.
- Associates have been notified.

Protective coverings or emergency boarding should be removed before reopening to guests.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Protecting associates and guests.
- Communicating with leadership.
- Securing company assets.
- Monitoring local conditions.
- Coordinating reopening activities.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Remaining open during unsafe conditions.
- Entering closed facilities without approval.
- Allowing associates to work in dangerous situations.
- Ignoring instructions from authorities.
- Operating a damaged facility.
- Reopening before safety conditions are restored.

---

# Guiding Principle

People first.

Operations can resume later. The safety of our associates and guests cannot be replaced.
