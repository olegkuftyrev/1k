# ORDINA

## Firearms & Open Carry Policy

### Washington State Locations

### Summary

Ordina is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and welcoming environment for associates and guests while complying with Washington State law.

The presence of a lawfully carried firearm alone does not constitute a safety concern. Unsafe, threatening, or unlawful behavior involving a weapon will not be tolerated.

Safety remains our highest priority.

---

# Purpose

This policy establishes standards regarding firearms and weapons in Washington State locations.

This policy applies to:

- Guests
- Associates
- Vendors
- Contractors
- Visitors

---

# Washington State Firearm Laws

Washington State permits the lawful possession and carrying of firearms in accordance with applicable state and federal laws.

Ordina does not determine whether an individual is legally permitted to carry a firearm.

Associates should not request permits, licenses, or proof of ownership.

---

# Guests Carrying Firearms

Guests who lawfully possess firearms may enter the restaurant where permitted by law.

Firearms must remain:

- Secured.
- Under the owner's control.
- Handled safely.

Guests may not:

- Display firearms unnecessarily.
- Remove firearms from holsters.
- Handle firearms inside the restaurant.
- Threaten or intimidate others.

---

# Prohibited Conduct

The following behavior is prohibited:

- Brandishing a firearm.
- Handling a firearm inside the restaurant.
- Threatening associates or guests.
- Reckless behavior.
- Violating applicable laws.
- Creating a safety concern.

Management may request law enforcement assistance whenever safety is in question.

---

# Associate Weapons

Associates may not possess firearms or other weapons while performing work duties or while inside non-public work areas, except where otherwise protected by applicable law.

This includes:

- Kitchen areas.
- Offices.
- Storage rooms.
- Back-of-house areas.
- Manager stations.

Violations may result in corrective action up to and including termination.

---

# Vendors and Contractors

Vendors, contractors, and non-employees entering non-public work areas must comply with company safety requirements.

---

# Guest Concerns

If a guest expresses concern regarding another guest who is lawfully carrying a firearm, associates should remain professional.

Suggested response:

> "Ordina follows Washington State law. If you would prefer another seating area, we would be happy to assist you."

Associates should never debate firearm laws with guests.

---

# Unsafe Situations

Management should immediately intervene if an individual:

- Removes a firearm from a holster.
- Makes threats.
- Acts aggressively.
- Appears impaired.
- Refuses lawful instructions.
- Creates fear among guests or associates.

If an immediate threat exists, call 911.

---

# Suggested Management Response

If a firearm is being handled improperly:

> "For everyone's safety, we ask that all firearms remain secured while inside the restaurant. Thank you for your cooperation."

If the individual refuses or becomes confrontational:

- Disengage.
- Protect associates and guests.
- Contact law enforcement.

---

# Incident Reporting

Managers must document:

- Firearm-related incidents.
- Guest complaints involving safety.
- Threatening behavior.
- Law enforcement involvement.
- Actions taken by management.

Significant incidents should be reported to leadership immediately.

---

# Emergency Response

If a weapon is displayed, threatened, or used:

1. Prioritize life safety.
2. Create distance when possible.
3. Contact emergency services.
4. Protect associates and guests.
5. Cooperate with law enforcement.

---

# Compliance

This policy is intended to comply with Washington State law. If applicable law differs from company policy, applicable law shall govern.

---

# Guiding Principle

Respect the law.

Protect our people.

Respond to behavior, not assumptions.

Safety always comes first.
