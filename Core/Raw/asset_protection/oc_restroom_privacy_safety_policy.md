# ORDINA

## Restroom Privacy & Safety Policy

### Summary

Ordina is committed to protecting the privacy, safety, and dignity of our guests and associates.

Regular restroom inspections help maintain cleanliness, prevent unauthorized activity, protect privacy, and ensure a safe environment for everyone.

---

# Purpose

Establish standards for restroom inspections, privacy protection, and restroom safety.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

# Privacy Protection

Management and associates should remain alert for unauthorized devices or suspicious objects that could compromise guest or associate privacy.

Any suspicious device or object should be treated seriously.

Associates should not touch or remove suspected devices unless necessary for safety.

Law enforcement should be contacted when appropriate.

---

# Daily Restroom Inspection

Restrooms should be inspected during routine cleaning and closing procedures.

Areas to inspect include:

### Walls and Corners

- Inspect corners and wall surfaces.
- Look for unfamiliar devices or attachments.
- Report unusual objects immediately.

---

### Trash Receptacles

Inspect:

- Inside trash containers.
- Under lids.
- Around receptacle areas.

---

### Hand Dryers and Paper Towel Stations

Check:

- Under equipment.
- Behind dispensers.
- Around mounting areas.

---

### Under Sinks

Inspect:

- Plumbing areas.
- Cabinet spaces.
- Water line areas.
- Unusual objects or devices.

---

### Doors and Hardware

Inspect:

- Door hinges.
- Locks.
- Door frames.
- Mounted hardware.

---

# Restroom Safety Checks

Managers should periodically verify that:

- Restrooms are clean.
- Fixtures operate properly.
- Lighting functions correctly.
- No safety hazards exist.
- No unauthorized activity is occurring.

---

# Loitering Prevention

Restrooms should be checked periodically, especially:

- During slow business periods.
- Before closing.
- Prior to leaving for the night.

If someone remains in the restroom for an unusual amount of time, management should respond professionally and prioritize safety.

---

# Suspicious Activity

Associates should immediately notify management if they observe:

- Suspicious devices.
- Unauthorized electronics.
- Damaged fixtures.
- Evidence of vandalism.
- Unsafe conditions.
- Unusual activity.

Management should determine whether additional assistance is necessary.

---

# Management Responsibilities

Managers are responsible for:

- Conducting inspections.
- Protecting guest privacy.
- Maintaining safe facilities.
- Addressing suspicious activity.
- Coordinating with authorities when necessary.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following are not permitted:

- Tampering with suspicious devices.
- Ignoring privacy concerns.
- Allowing unsafe restroom conditions.
- Leaving vandalism unaddressed.
- Ignoring repeated loitering concerns.

---

# Guiding Principle

Privacy and safety are essential to the guest experience.

A clean, secure, and regularly inspected restroom protects both our guests and our associates.
