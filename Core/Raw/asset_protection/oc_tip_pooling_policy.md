# ORDINA

## Tip Pooling Policy

### Summary

Tips are shared to recognize the contributions of all front-of-house associates who create the guest experience.

Tip pooling ensures fairness, transparency, and accountability. All eligible tips must be reported and distributed according to company guidelines. Managers may never participate in tip pools.

---

## Purpose

Provide a fair and transparent system for collecting, distributing, and reporting tips.

This policy applies to all eligible front-of-house associates.

---

# Tip Pooling

All cash, credit card, and eligible electronic tips collected during a shift become part of the Total Earned Tips.

Tips are distributed according to the company's approved tip pool structure.

Keeping tips that belong to the pool is considered theft and may result in corrective action, up to and including termination.

---

# Tip Distribution

## Server, Bartender, and Cocktail Server Group

Servers, bartenders, and cocktail servers receive:

- 100% of their earned tips.
- Minus the required contribution to the Shared Tip Pool.

The Shared Tip Pool contribution is calculated based on daily net sales.

Bartender tip payouts may be distributed through payroll.

---

## Shared Tip Pool

The Shared Tip Pool supports front-of-house associates who contribute to the guest experience.

The pool is distributed as follows:

- 70% to Barbacks.
- 20% to Hosts and Hostesses.
- 10% to Cashiers, Runners, and Bussers.

Individual payouts are based on hours worked during the day.

---

## Kiosk and Walk-Up Tips

Tips received from kiosks and walk-up transactions are distributed among eligible support positions according to hours worked.

---

# Tip Payments

Tip payouts are calculated daily and distributed according to payroll schedules.

Associates are responsible for accurately reporting all tips for tax purposes.

---

# Management Restrictions

Managers and supervisors may not:

- Participate in tip pools.
- Receive tip distributions.
- Accept voluntary tips from associates.

Management positions are not eligible for tipped income.

---

# Guest Service Standards

Associates may never:

- Request tips from guests.
- Pressure guests to leave larger tips.
- Argue with guests regarding gratuities.

Gift card transactions do not change these standards.

Soliciting tips is strictly prohibited.

---

# Tip Protection

The company does not deduct:

- Credit card processing fees.
- Cash shortages.
- Register shortages.
- Breakage costs.
- Operating expenses.

Associates may keep only the portion of tips assigned to them through the approved distribution process.

---

# Third-Party Delivery Tips

Tips provided through third-party delivery platforms belong to the delivery service provider and are not included in the company tip pool.

---

# Voluntary Tip Sharing

Additional voluntary tip arrangements are discouraged.

Associates may not pressure coworkers to share tips outside the approved policy.

No associate may voluntarily tip management.

---

# New Associate Acknowledgment

All eligible associates will receive a copy of this policy.

Associates are required to acknowledge receipt and agree to comply with the policy.

Signed acknowledgments will be maintained in the associate personnel file.

---

# Prohibited Practices

The following actions are not permitted:

- Keeping pooled tips.
- Failing to report tips.
- Soliciting tips from guests.
- Arguing with guests about gratuities.
- Giving tips to managers.
- Allowing managers to participate in the tip pool.
- Using tip money to cover shortages.
- Pressuring associates to share tips outside the approved policy.

Violations of this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of employment.
