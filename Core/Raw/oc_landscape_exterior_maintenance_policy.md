# ORDINA

## Landscape & Exterior Maintenance Policy

### Summary

The exterior of every Ordina location creates the first impression for our guests.

Landscaping, parking areas, and exterior spaces must remain clean, safe, and well maintained at all times. Proper maintenance protects the brand, improves guest experience, and prevents safety hazards.

---

## Purpose

Maintain an attractive, welcoming, and safe exterior environment for guests, associates, and the community.

This policy applies to locations responsible for their own parking areas, landscaping, and exterior grounds.

---

# Responsibilities

Store Managers are responsible for maintaining:

- Landscaping appearance.
- Parking lot conditions.
- Exterior cleanliness.
- Vendor performance.

Operations leaders are responsible for monitoring overall property conditions and ensuring maintenance issues are addressed promptly.

---

# Scope of Services

Exterior maintenance includes:

- Lawn maintenance.
- Shrub and tree care.
- Tree trimming.
- Irrigation systems.
- Parking lot maintenance.
- Seasonal services.
- Ground cover maintenance.

---

# Lawn Maintenance

### Mowing

Grass must be maintained at an appropriate height through regular mowing.

### Edging

Curbs, sidewalks, landscape beds, and walkways must be edged regularly.

### Cleaning

Leaves, grass clippings, trash, and debris must be removed during each service.

### Fertilization and Weed Control

Lawn treatments and weed control should be performed regularly to maintain appearance and plant health.

Shrubs and landscape plants should receive seasonal fertilization.

---

# Trees and Shrubs

### Pruning

Trees and shrubs should be trimmed to maintain:

- Proper shape.
- Appropriate size.
- Healthy growth.

Dead or damaged branches must be removed promptly.

### Weed Removal

Unwanted vegetation must be removed from:

- Landscape beds.
- Concrete cracks.
- Asphalt areas.
- Ground cover areas.

---

# Tree Trimming

Tree trimming should be performed as needed.

Large trimming projects should be planned and budgeted in advance.

Trees must not become:

- Safety hazards.
- Visibility hazards.
- Property damage risks.

---

# Irrigation Systems

Irrigation systems must be inspected regularly.

This includes:

- Sprinkler heads.
- Valves.
- Timers.
- Water coverage.

Damaged systems should be repaired promptly to prevent water waste and landscape damage.

Water schedules should be adjusted seasonally.

---

# Parking Lot Maintenance

Parking lots must remain:

- Safe.
- Clean.
- Clearly marked.

Damaged pavement, potholes, and uneven surfaces should be repaired promptly.

Line striping and surface treatments should be completed as needed based on property conditions.

---

# Seasonal Services

Landscape services should adjust according to seasonal conditions.

This may include:

- Leaf removal.
- Winter preparation.
- Snow removal.
- Seasonal planting.
- Storm cleanup.

Snow removal services should be arranged where necessary to maintain safe access.

---

# Ground Cover

Areas without grass should be protected using:

- Bark.
- Mulch.
- Decorative stone.
- River rock.

Ground cover helps prevent erosion and improves appearance.

---

# Budget and Planning

Landscape maintenance is considered a necessary operating expense.

Managers should budget for:

- Routine maintenance.
- Seasonal services.
- Tree trimming.
- Irrigation repairs.
- Parking lot repairs.
- Surface maintenance.

Large exterior projects should be planned annually.

Preventive maintenance reduces long-term costs and protects the appearance of the property.

---

# Property Standards

Every Ordina location should provide:

- A clean exterior.
- Healthy landscaping.
- Safe walkways.
- Well-maintained parking areas.
- Professional curb appeal.

The exterior of the restaurant reflects the quality of the brand.

---

# Prohibited Conditions

The following conditions are unacceptable:

- Overgrown grass.
- Dead landscaping.
- Excessive weeds.
- Broken irrigation systems.
- Unsafe pavement.
- Unrepaired potholes.
- Uncontrolled erosion.
- Excessive debris or trash.
- Neglected exterior areas.

Failure to maintain property standards may result in corrective action or additional operational review.
