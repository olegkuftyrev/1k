# ORDINA

## Safe & Register Funds Policy

### Summary

Cash accountability protects the business and the team.

Safe and register funds must remain accurate, balanced, and secured at all times. All funds must be counted daily, recorded in Safe Wizard, and stored in the safe at the end of the day.

Any discrepancies must be investigated immediately.

---

## Purpose

Maintain accurate cash balances, protect company assets, and ensure accountability during every shift.

This policy applies to all Ordina locations.

---

## Change Funds

### 1. Change Orders

Change funds should only be ordered when necessary.

Smaller change orders are encouraged to reduce outstanding balances and prevent late repayments.

### 2. Delivery and Exchange

When receiving a change order:

- Prepare the exchange bag before delivery.
- Exchange the bag with the armored carrier.
- Obtain the carrier's signature.
- Record bag numbers and amounts clearly.

Courier receipts must be retained for at least 90 days.

### 3. Change Order Issues

If a change order is incorrect or missing:

- Notify Loss Prevention immediately.
- Report discrepancies as soon as they are discovered.
- Delays in reporting may result in financial losses for the location.

### 4. Change Fund Adjustments

Permanent increases or decreases to change funds require approval.

Temporary adjustments may be approved during high-volume periods such as holidays.

---

## Daily Cash Counts

### 1. Safe Wizard

Safe Wizard is the official tool used to verify safe and register balances.

Every manager or person responsible for cash must ensure accurate counts.

### 2. Start of Day

Safe and register funds must be counted at the beginning of the day.

### 3. End of Day

At the end of the day:

- Count all cash in the location.
- Enter actual amounts into Safe Wizard.
- Return all funds to their authorized balance.

Any shortages or overages must be investigated immediately.

---

## Cash Balance Requirements

Change and register funds must remain intact.

Cash paid-outs or operational expenses may not reduce assigned cash balances.

Example:

If the authorized fund is $1,500, the safe must contain $1,500 at the end of every day.

---

## Cash Security

All cash must be secured inside the safe at the end of the day.

This includes:

- Change funds
- Register funds
- Daily deposits

No more than one day's deposit should remain in the safe.

Cash must never be left unattended, unsecured, or outside the designated safe.
