# Required Workplace Postings

All locations are required to post federal and state labor law notices in a visible area accessible to all associates.

Contact HR to confirm the specific postings required for your state.

---

## Federal — Required Postings

- Employee Polygraph Protection Act (1988)
- Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA, 1970)
- Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) — Minimum Wage
- Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA, 1993)
- Title VII of the Civil Rights Act (1964)

---

## State — Common Required Postings

Requirements vary by state. The following types of notices are commonly required:

- Fair Employment
- Minimum Wage
- OSHA / Workers Compensation
- Unemployment Insurance
- Child Labor Laws
- Sexual Harassment
- Leave of Absence
- Whistleblower Protection
- Payday Notice
- Emergency Notice
- USERRA Military Leave Benefits

Verify current state requirements with HR before posting.
