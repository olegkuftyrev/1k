# Seattle Secure Scheduling Ordinance

**Seattle Municipal Code (SMC) 14.22**
**Effective: July 1, 2017**

*Source: Seattle Office of Labor Standards*
*This document is a summary of legal requirements. For full legal text, consult SMC 14.22.*

---

## Coverage

This ordinance applies to **hourly employees** at retail and food service establishments with:

- **500 or more employees worldwide**, AND
- For **full-service restaurants**: also must have **40 or more full-service locations worldwide**.

This law applies to employees **regardless of immigration status**.

---

## Employer Requirements

### 1. Good Faith Estimate

For the upcoming year, the employer must provide each employee a **written forecast** of:

- The employee's **median hours per work week**, and
- Whether the employee should expect **on-call shifts**.

---

### 2. Right to Request Input into Work Schedule

Before a work schedule is posted, the employer must **grant schedule requests** related to a **major life event**, including:

- Transportation
- Housing
- Other job(s)
- Education
- Caregiving
- Self-care for a serious health condition

The employer may deny a request only if there is a **bona fide business reason** (significant cost or disruption).

---

### 3. Advance Notice of Work Schedule

Employer must post work schedules **14 days in advance**.

---

### 4. Premium Pay for Work Schedule Changes After Schedule is Posted

**Additional hours added after posting:**
- Employer must pay **one additional hour of pay**, plus wages earned.

**Hours subtracted after posting:**
- Employer must pay for **half of the hours not worked**, plus wages earned — applies to both regular and on-call shifts.

#### Exceptions to Premium Pay (no premium owed):

- Schedule additions or subtractions of **15 minutes or less** (grace period).
- **Employee-requested** schedule changes and/or shift swaps with another employee.
- Employer reduction of hours due to **documented disciplinary reasons**.
- Employer inability to begin or continue operations due to:
  - Public utilities failure
  - Natural disaster
  - Inclement weather
- Employee **voluntarily accepts** additional hours in response to:
  - A **mass communication** about additional hours because a scheduled employee cannot work, **OR**
  - An **in-person group communication** about additional hours due to **unanticipated customer needs** that are **consecutive to the employee's current shift**.

---

### 5. Right to Rest Between Work Shifts (Clopening)

Employer must pay **time-and-a-half** for any hours worked during a closing-to-opening ("clopening") shift where the closing and opening shifts are separated by **less than 10 hours**.

---

### 6. Access to Hours for Current Employees

Before hiring new employees, the employer must:

1. **Post notice of available hours** for **3 days**, and
2. **Offer the available hours to qualified current employees**, subject to exceptions.

---

## Prohibited Conduct

- Employers **must not engage in a pattern or practice of underscheduling**.
- **Retaliation is illegal.**
