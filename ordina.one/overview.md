# ordina.one

Internal platform for Ordina Cresce staff.

## What is this

`ordina.one` is the internal hub for the Ordina Cresce team. It is not a public product. It is the place where staff log in and access the tools they need for their role.

Access to tools is controlled per user. Not everyone sees the same dashboard.

## URLs

| URL | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| `ordina.one` | Entry point. Login form only. |
| `app.ordina.one` | Staff dashboard. Links to tools based on user role. |
| `crm.ordina.one` | Twenty CRM. Opens separately. |

## Design

Follows Ordina Cresce brand identity: deep forest charcoal background, warm ivory text, antique gold accents.

All views are mobile first.

## Who uses it

Internal staff only. No clients. No public access.
