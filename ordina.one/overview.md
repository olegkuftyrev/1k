# ordina.one

Internal platform for Ordina Cresce staff.

## What is this

`ordina.one` is the internal hub for the Ordina Cresce team. It is not a public product. It is the place where staff log in, access tools, and manage day-to-day operations.

## URLs

| URL | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| `ordina.one` | Entry point. Landing page with login. |
| `app.ordina.one` | The application. Staff dashboard after login. |
| `crm.ordina.one` | Twenty CRM. Contacts, clients, opportunities. |

## Who uses it

Internal staff only. No clients. No public access.

Pre-build. Documentation in progress.
