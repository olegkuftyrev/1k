# Market Analysis

Washington State has thousands of independently owned and franchise restaurant and retail locations. The Seattle metro area alone has a dense concentration of small and mid-size operators in this segment.

The market is not served by consultants who focus on operations at the unit level for businesses under $5M in revenue. Large consulting firms do not go there. That is the gap.

**Key advantage:** we work at the operational level — inside the business, not above it. We do not produce strategy decks. We fix the actual problem and leave the client with a system that works without us.
